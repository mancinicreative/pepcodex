## Overview

[Retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide) is a GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor agonist. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. This page quotes the retatrutide Phase 2 obesity trial against STEP 1, STEP 3, STEP 5, and SELECT. Those programmes are not one head-to-head obesity RCT.

Retatrutide had no Drugs@FDA match on openFDA drugsfda accessed 2026-09-02. Semaglutide applications fetched that day are listed under Development status.

## Key Facts

| Aspect | Retatrutide | Semaglutide |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Drugs@FDA (2026-09-02)** | No match | NDA 209637, 215256, 218316, 213051 |
| **Mechanism** | GIP / GLP-1 / glucagon agonist | GLP-1 agonist |
| **Developer in the papers quoted here** | Eli Lilly (Phase 2 funding statement) | Novo Nordisk (STEP 1 / SELECT funding statements) |
| **Obesity efficacy quoted here** | Phase 2, n=338, 24 and 48 weeks | STEP 1 n=1961 at 68 weeks; STEP 3 n=611; STEP 5 n=304 at 104 weeks |

## Clinical Trial Evidence

These trials differ in phase, duration, background therapy, and (for retatrutide Phase 2) whether the abstract names a treatment-regimen estimand. Do not divide one percent by another.

### Retatrutide Phase 2 (PMID 37366315, NCT04881760)

n=338 adults; BMI ≥30, or BMI 27 to &lt;30 plus at least one weight-related condition; once-weekly subcutaneous retatrutide or placebo for 48 weeks. **Primary end point:** percentage change in body weight from baseline to **24 weeks**. **Secondary:** percentage change to **48 weeks** and ≥5% / ≥10% / ≥15% weight reduction. The abstract does not name a treatment-regimen estimand [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315]. CT.gov: COMPLETED; enrollment 338 actual; hasResults true [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

Least-squares mean percentage change (combined 4 mg and combined 8 mg arms as reported):

| Time point | 1 mg | Combined 4 mg | Combined 8 mg | 12 mg | Placebo |
|------------|------|---------------|---------------|-------|---------|
| **24 weeks (primary)** | −7.2% | −12.9% | −17.3% | **−17.5%** | −1.6% |
| **48 weeks (secondary)** | −8.7% | −17.1% | −22.8% | **−24.2%** | −2.1% |

−17.5% is 12 mg at **24 weeks**, not 4 mg at 48 weeks. −24.2% vs −2.1% is the 12 mg **48-week secondary** [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

At 48 weeks, ≥5% / ≥10% / ≥15% responders: 4 mg 92%/75%/60%; 8 mg 100%/91%/75%; 12 mg 100%/93%/83%; placebo 27%/9%/2% [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

### STEP 1 (PMID 33567185, NCT03548935)

n=1961 adults without diabetes; BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with ≥1 weight-related coexisting condition; 68 weeks of once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. **Primary estimand:** effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (**treatment-regimen**). Coprimary: percentage change in body weight and ≥5% weight reduction [step-1-pmid-33567185].

Mean change at week 68: **−14.9%** vs **−2.4%** placebo (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% 86.4% vs 31.5%; ≥10% 69.1% vs 12.0%; ≥15% 50.5% vs 4.9% (P&lt;0.001 for all three). Mean kg change −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### STEP 3 (PMID 33625476, NCT03611582)

n=611; 68 weeks; semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo **as adjunct to intensive behavioral therapy** with an initial low-calorie diet. Estimated mean change: **−16.0%** vs **−5.7%** (difference −10.3 percentage points; 95% CI −12.0 to −8.6; P&lt;.001). ≥5% 86.6% vs 47.6%. The abstract does not name a treatment-regimen estimand [step-3-pmid-33625476]. This is not the same background as STEP 1.

### STEP 5 (PMID 36216945, NCT03693430)

n=304; 104 weeks. Efficacy assessed among all randomized participants regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue intervention (**treatment-regimen**). Mean change: **−15.2%** vs **−2.6%** (ETD −12.6 percentage points; 95% CI −15.3 to −9.8; P&lt;0.0001). ≥5% 77.1% vs 34.4% [step-5-pmid-36216945].

## Cardiovascular Evidence

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, BMI ≥27, no diabetes; once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo. Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Primary MACE (CV death, nonfatal MI, or nonfatal stroke) 569/8803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8801 (**8.0%**); **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72 to 0.90; P&lt;0.001) [select-pmid-37952131].

Quote the hazard ratio and event rates. This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent.

The retatrutide Phase 2 abstract does not report a MACE hazard ratio [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315]. TRIUMPH-Outcomes (NCT06383390) is event-driven; estimated completion 2029-02; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02.

## Safety Comparison

Phase 2 retatrutide: most common adverse events were gastrointestinal; dose-related; mostly mild to moderate; partially mitigated with a 2 mg vs 4 mg starting dose. Dose-dependent heart-rate increases peaked at 24 weeks and declined thereafter. The abstract does not give nausea percents [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; GI events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% placebo [step-1-pmid-33567185].

STEP 3: gastrointestinal adverse events 82.8% vs 63.2%; discontinued for those events 3.4% vs 0% [step-3-pmid-33625476].

STEP 5: gastrointestinal adverse events 82.2% vs 53.9% [step-5-pmid-36216945].

SELECT: adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2% (P&lt;0.001) [select-pmid-37952131].
