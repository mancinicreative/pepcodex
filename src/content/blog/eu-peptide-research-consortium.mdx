**Correction notice.** This article previously reported the launch of an "EUPeptide Consortium" funded by the European Commission with 380 million euros over six years, spanning 15 member states, 42 universities, 28 biotechnology companies and 8 pharmaceutical corporations, with three manufacturing centres of excellence in Germany, France and the Netherlands and a first annual conference in Copenhagen.

None of this could be verified. Searches of European Commission communications and the CORDIS project database return no consortium by that name and no initiative matching that funding figure or structure. Every specific claim in the original article has been removed.

## What We Know

EU peptide research funding is real but is distributed across many smaller, individually named projects rather than a single flagship consortium. Horizon Europe and its predecessor Horizon 2020 have funded peptide-focused projects in oncology, infectious disease and delivery technology, typically at the scale of single-digit millions of euros per consortium. Projects are searchable by name and grant number through CORDIS, which is the authoritative record.

Anyone researching EU funding in this area should work from CORDIS project pages and official Commission announcements rather than secondary summaries.

## Why This Correction Matters

Fabricated funding announcements are unusually damaging in this domain. They create the impression of institutional momentum behind a field, which in turn lends unearned credibility to individual compounds and companies. A reader who believes the EU has committed hundreds of millions of euros to peptide therapeutics will read every subsequent peptide claim with less scepticism than it deserves.

The underlying observations that motivated the original article remain reasonable: peptides do occupy an awkward regulatory position between small molecules and biologics; pilot-scale manufacturing access genuinely is a bottleneck for academic groups; and antimicrobial resistance genuinely is an EU public health priority. None of that requires an invented consortium to be true.
