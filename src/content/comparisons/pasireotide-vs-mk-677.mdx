## Different clinical questions

Pasireotide is an FDA-approved somatostatin analog with **specific labeled uses** in acromegaly and Cushing's disease. MK-677 (ibutamoren) is an oral nonpeptide growth hormone secretagogue with **no FDA-approved product**. Opposite effects on parts of the GH axis do not make these treatments alternatives for the same patient or establish a safety ranking. [FDA Signifor LAR label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/203255s005lbl.pdf); [FDA ibutamoren evaluation](https://www.fda.gov/media/182087/download).

| Question | Pasireotide | MK-677 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence context | Labeled treatment in selected pituitary diseases | Investigational trials in different populations |
| GH-axis action | Somatostatin-receptor signaling suppresses secretion in relevant disease settings | Ghrelin-receptor agonism stimulates GH release |
| FDA status | Approved for specific labeled indications | No approved ibutamoren product |
| Glucose caution | Label warns of hyperglycemia and diabetes | Older-adult trial found higher fasting glucose and lower insulin sensitivity |

In a randomized **65-person trial of healthy older adults**, MK-677 raised GH, IGF-1 and fat-free mass, but did not significantly improve strength, function or abdominal visceral fat. The trial was insufficiently powered and too short to rule out a functional effect. It does not estimate efficacy or adverse-event rates relative to pasireotide. [Nass et al., PMID 18981485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18981485/).

The pasireotide label's glucose warnings concern patients treated for its **approved diseases**; the MK-677 finding concerns healthy older adults. Comparing the severity or frequency of these effects across those unlike studies would be misleading. [FDA Signifor LAR label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/203255s005lbl.pdf).

Ibutamoren remains in pediatric development as **LUM-201**: official Phase 3 [NCT06948214](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06948214) was recruiting in the September 29, 2026 archive. Development is not approval or evidence for the labeled pasireotide diseases. [FDA ibutamoren review](https://www.fda.gov/media/182087/download).

No cited head-to-head or combination trial establishes a ranking or treatment strategy. This page makes no use recommendation.
