Boehringer Ingelheim is developing survodutide, a dual GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist, for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH). Phase 2 data showed improvement in MASH histology without worsening of fibrosis, and the programme has since received FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation and moved into two Phase 3 trials.

## What We Know

### Phase 2 Results Review

The phase 2 trial randomized 293 patients with biopsy-confirmed MASH and stage F1-F3 fibrosis to once-weekly subcutaneous survodutide at 2.4 mg, 4.8 mg, or 6.0 mg, or placebo, over 48 weeks (a 24-week dose-escalation phase followed by 24 weeks of maintenance), with liver biopsies at baseline and week 48 [survodutide-mash-data].

**Primary endpoint — MASH improvement with no worsening of fibrosis:**
- Survodutide 2.4 mg: 47%
- Survodutide 4.8 mg: 62%
- Survodutide 6.0 mg: 43%
- Placebo: 14%

**Fibrosis improvement by at least one stage:**
- Survodutide 2.4 / 4.8 / 6.0 mg: 34% / 36% / 34%
- Placebo: 22%

**Liver fat reduction of at least 30%:**
- Survodutide 2.4 / 4.8 / 6.0 mg: 63% / 67% / 57%
- Placebo: 14%

Gastrointestinal adverse events were substantially more common with survodutide than placebo (nausea 66% vs. 23%; diarrhoea 49% vs. 23%; vomiting 41% vs. 4%), while serious adverse events were similar (8% vs. 7%) [survodutide-mash-data].

### Mechanism Driving MASH Efficacy

Survodutide's dual mechanism appears particularly suited to MASH treatment [survodutide-mash-data]:

**GLP-1 receptor activation:**
- Weight loss reduces hepatic fat accumulation
- Improved insulin sensitivity addresses metabolic dysfunction
- Anti-inflammatory effects in liver tissue

**Glucagon receptor activation:**
- Direct hepatic effects on lipid metabolism
- Enhanced fatty acid oxidation
- Reduced hepatic lipogenesis
- Increased energy expenditure

The combination of metabolic and direct hepatic effects may explain the superior histological outcomes compared to GLP-1-only approaches.

### Regulatory Status

**Breakthrough therapy designation:** Survodutide received FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation for non-cirrhotic MASH with moderate to advanced fibrosis in 2024, which can expedite development and review.

**Phase 3 programme:** Boehringer Ingelheim's Phase 3 MASH programme is named LIVERAGE, not to be confused with SYNCHRONIZE, its Phase 3 obesity programme.

**Submission timeline:** No FDA submission date for the MASH indication has been publicly confirmed. Any filing would follow the Phase 3 readouts.

## What It Means

### For MASH Patients

MASH affects millions of Americans, yet until resmetirom's approval in March 2024, no therapy was specifically approved for the condition. Key implications:

**Treatment options:** Survodutide would provide a second approved option with a distinct mechanism.

**Metabolic benefits:** Unlike resmetirom (a thyroid hormone receptor agonist), survodutide addresses obesity and metabolic dysfunction that commonly accompany MASH.

**Fibrosis regression:** Fibrosis improvement rates exceeded placebo in phase 2, though the margin was modest and requires confirmation in phase 3.

### Competitive Landscape

The MASH treatment space is becoming increasingly competitive, with resmetirom approved and incretin-based candidates including semaglutide, tirzepatide, and pemvidutide in development. PepCodex does not publish a cross-trial efficacy table here: MASH trials differ in fibrosis stage at entry, biopsy reading methodology, and endpoint definition, so head-to-head percentages taken from separate trials are not comparable.

### Market Considerations

Several factors will influence survodutide's market position:

**Efficacy differentiation:** If phase 3 confirms the histological benefit seen in phase 2, that could drive prescribing preference.

**Weight loss benefit:** Dual benefit for MASH and obesity addresses two conditions that often coexist.

**Safety profile:** Long-term safety data will be important for a chronic condition.

**Pricing and access:** Healthcare system willingness to cover MASH treatments remains variable.

## What's Next

### Phase 3 Program

The LIVERAGE phase 3 programme is evaluating survodutide across two MASH populations:

**LIVERAGE:** MASH with moderate or advanced liver fibrosis
- Approximately 1,800 participants
- Recruiting [liverage-phase3]

**LIVERAGE - Cirrhosis:** MASH with cirrhosis
- Approximately 1,590 participants
- Recruiting [liverage-cirrhosis]

### Regulatory Milestones

Boehringer Ingelheim has not published dates for a MASH regulatory submission or an FDA decision. The meaningful near-term milestone is completion of the two LIVERAGE trials.

### Broader Development

Beyond MASH, survodutide is being developed for:

**Obesity:** Phase 3 trials ongoing for chronic weight management
**Type 2 diabetes:** Potential additional indication
**Cardiovascular outcomes:** Outcomes trial planned

The convergence of MASH and obesity as interrelated conditions supports development of therapies like survodutide that address both.
