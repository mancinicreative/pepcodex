## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [Survodutide](/peptides/survodutide) is Boehringer Ingelheim's investigational glucagon and GLP-1 receptor dual agonist. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, and SYNCHRONIZE-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse a 36-week early-phase estimated mean into a 76-week phase 3 treatment-regimen percent. The programme name on the fetched records is **SYNCHRONIZE**, not SYNCHRONY.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide), [survodutide vs semaglutide](/compare/survodutide-vs-semaglutide), and [survodutide vs tirzepatide](/compare/survodutide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ. Do not divide the amycretin 36-week estimated mean by the SYNCHRONIZE-1 76-week treatment-regimen percent.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### SYNCHRONIZE-1 (obesity, no diabetes) — not SYNCHRONY

PMID 42253238, NCT06066515. The article title is “Survodutide Once Weekly for the Treatment of Adults with Obesity.” The conclusions name **SYNCHRONIZE-1**. Phase 3; 725 participants in the paper (241 on 3.6 mg, 242 on 6.0 mg, 242 on placebo); 76 weeks. The **primary efficacy analysis is the treatment-regimen estimand**, which the paper says incorporates early discontinuation, disallowed obesity medications, and a prolonged escalation period.

At week 76, mean body-weight change (treatment-regimen): **−12.2%** (95% CI −13.6 to −10.8) with 3.6 mg, **−13.0%** (95% CI −14.4 to −11.6) with 6.0 mg, **−5.4%** (95% CI −6.9 to −4.0) with placebo. At least 5% weight reduction: 72.6%, 71.9%, and 46.3% (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9%, 89.7%, and 47.9%. No deaths reported [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06066515 as completed Phase 3, acronym SYNCHRONIZE-1 (actual enrollment **726**; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Percents above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT06066515` on 2026-09-02 returned PMIDs 42253238, 41187967, and 39495965. A PubMed search the same day for `"SYNCHRONY" survodutide` returned **0** records.

PMID 39495965 is the SYNCHRONIZE-1 / SYNCHRONIZE-2 **design** paper (NCT06066515 and NCT06066528). It publishes **no efficacy percent** [synchronize-1-design-pmid-39495965]. PMID 41187967 is the SYNCHRONIZE-1 **baseline** paper (n=725; mean BMI 37.9). It is not an efficacy readout.

### Phase 2 obesity trial (46 weeks, not a 48-week 18.7/19.5 table)

PMID 38330987, NCT04667377. 387 enrolled; 386 treated; **46 weeks** (20-week escalation, 26-week maintenance). Primary endpoint: percentage change in body weight to week 46. Planned-treatment mean body-weight change: −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), **−14.9% (4.8 mg)**, −2.8% placebo. Gastrointestinal adverse events in 75% of survodutide recipients vs 42% on placebo [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

This is not SYNCHRONIZE-1. This page does not quote an unsourced 48-week ~18.7% / ~19.5% obesity table. A PubMed search on 2026-09-02 for `survodutide[Title] AND 18.7` returned 1 record (PMID 40963161, a *Cardiology in Review* narrative citing “weight loss up to 18.7%”). That review is **not** quoted as a trial result. `survodutide[Title] AND 19.5` the same day returned **0** records.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04667377 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 387; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract, not the results module.

### MASH phase 2 (48-week histology, not obesity Phase 3)

PMID 38847460, NCT04771273. 48-week phase 2; 293 participants received at least one assigned amount. Primary end point: histologic improvement in MASH with no worsening of fibrosis. Improvement: 47% (2.4 mg), 62% (4.8 mg), 43% (6.0 mg) vs 14% placebo (P&lt;0.001 for the quadratic assigned-amount-response curve). At least 30% liver-fat reduction: 63% / 67% / 57% vs 14%. Fibrosis improvement by at least one stage: 34% / 36% / 34% vs 22% [survodutide-mash-pmid-38847460]. These are **not** body-weight percents and are not SYNCHRONIZE-1.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04771273 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 295; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract.

### SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD (48-week MASLD, both estimands labelled)

PMID 42252333. Phase 3; 216 adults with obesity and at-risk MASLD; 48 weeks; survodutide 6.0 mg (n=146) vs placebo (n=70). Co-primary: ≥30% MRI-PDFF liver-fat reduction and percent body-weight change.

Liver fat ≥30% reduction: **efficacy estimand** 84.2% vs 24.3%; **treatment-regimen estimand** 68.5% vs 28.6% (P&lt;0.0001).

Mean body-weight change: **efficacy estimand −12.2% vs −1.0%**; **treatment-regimen estimand −8.7% vs −1.4%** (P&lt;0.0001) [synchronize-masld-pmid-42252333].

This is not SYNCHRONIZE-1. The −12.2% efficacy-estimand weight figure here is a 48-week MASLD result, not a 76-week obesity treatment-regimen percent.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; the survodutide obesity figure on this page is a phase 3 treatment-regimen percent at 76 weeks.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **SYNCHRONIZE-1, not SYNCHRONY:** treatment-regimen −12.2% / −13.0% vs −5.4% at 76 weeks. PubMed `"SYNCHRONY" survodutide` on 2026-09-02 returned 0 records.
5. **Phase 2 obesity is 46 weeks:** planned-treatment −14.9% vs −2.8% at 4.8 mg. No unsourced 48-week ~18.7 / ~19.5 table. `survodutide[Title] AND 19.5` = 0.
6. **SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD:** 48-week MASLD; efficacy-estimand weight −12.2% vs −1.0%; treatment-regimen −8.7% vs −1.4%. Not SYNCHRONIZE-1.
