## Overview

Maridebart cafraglutide (MariTide, AMG 133) is Amgen's investigational peptide-antibody conjugate: a GLP-1 receptor agonist paired with a GIP receptor antagonist. Semaglutide is Novo Nordisk's GLP-1 receptor agonist. The Phase 2 maridebart trial, STEP 1, and SELECT are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [maritide](/peptides/maritide), [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide), and [maritide vs tirzepatide](/compare/maritide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations and estimands differ.

### Maridebart cafraglutide Phase 2

PMID 40549887, NCT05669599. n=592 enrolled as two cohorts. Primary end point: percent change in body weight from baseline to week 52. The paper reports the **treatment-policy estimand** (intention-to-treat). Arms included 140, 280, or 420 mg every 4 weeks, 420 mg every 8 weeks, and 420 mg every 4 weeks with 4-week or 12-week escalation. The abstract publishes a range across maridebart groups, not a per-arm percent table.

Obesity cohort (465 participants; no diabetes): mean percent change at week 52 ranged from −12.3% (95% CI −15.0 to −9.7) to −16.2% (95% CI −18.9 to −13.5) versus −2.5% (95% CI −4.2 to −0.7) placebo.

Obesity-diabetes cohort (127 participants): −8.4% (95% CI −11.0 to −5.7) to −12.3% (95% CI −15.3 to −9.2) versus −1.7% (95% CI −2.9 to −0.6) placebo. Mean change in glycated haemoglobin on the treatment-policy estimand: −1.2 to −1.6 percentage points versus 0.1 percentage points placebo.

Gastrointestinal adverse events were common; the abstract states they were less frequent with escalation and a lower starting assignment. It does not publish nausea or vomiting percents [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887].

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT05669599 as completed (actual enrollment 592; completion 2025-12-16) with `hasResults` false as of 2026-09-02.

### Phase 1 (no percent in the abstract)

PMID 38316982, NCT04478708. Phase 1 single- and multiple-ascending study in participants with obesity. The abstract reports an acceptable safety and tolerability profile, weight loss that increased across ascending arms, and maintenance of weight loss in multiple-ascending cohorts for up to 150 days after the last administration. The abstract does not publish a percent. This page does not invent one [maritide-p1-pmid-38316982].

### STEP 1 (semaglutide, no diabetes)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1,961; 68 weeks. **Treatment-regimen** estimand (effects regardless of discontinuation or rescue). Mean change at week 68: −14.9% versus −2.4% placebo (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). Weight reduction of 5% or more: 86.4% versus 31.5%; 10% or more: 69.1% versus 12.0%; 15% or more: 50.5% versus 4.9%. Gastrointestinal events caused discontinuation in 4.5% versus 0.8% placebo [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### SELECT (semaglutide, cardiovascular outcomes)

PMID 37952131, NCT03574597. n=17,604 (8,803 semaglutide; 8,801 placebo). Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Primary composite of cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke: 569 of 8,803 (6.5%) versus 701 of 8,801 (8.0%); hazard ratio 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). This page quotes the hazard ratio and event counts; it does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent. Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation: 16.6% versus 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

### Registered Phase 3 (design only)

MARITIME-1 (NCT06858839) is Phase 3, active not recruiting, actual enrollment 3,853, estimated primary completion 2027-01-21. Primary outcome on the registry: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 72. ClinicalTrials.gov `hasResults` was false as of 2026-09-02. PubMed `NCT06858839` and `"MARITIME-1"` each returned 0 records on 2026-09-02 [nct-06858839].

MARITIME-2 (NCT06858878) is Phase 3, active not recruiting, actual enrollment 1,105, estimated primary completion 2027-01-21. Primary outcome on the registry: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 72. `hasResults` false. PubMed `NCT06858878` and `"MARITIME-2"` each returned 0 records on 2026-09-02 [nct-06858878].

This page does not quote a Phase 3 percent from those registry records, and it does not quote an unpublished head-to-head percent versus semaglutide.

## Side Effect Profile

Phase 2 maridebart: gastrointestinal events common; the abstract does not publish nausea, vomiting, or discontinuation percents [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; gastrointestinal events caused discontinuation in 4.5% versus 0.8% placebo [step-1-pmid-33567185]. The STEP 1 abstract does not publish separate nausea or vomiting percents; this page does not invent them.

SELECT: adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation 16.6% versus 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** Phase 2 maridebart is 52 weeks (treatment-policy); STEP 1 is 68 weeks (treatment-regimen).
2. **Phase 2 is a range, not a single arm:** obesity cohort −12.3% to −16.2% versus −2.5% placebo; obesity-diabetes −8.4% to −12.3% versus −1.7%. The abstract does not publish a per-arm table.
3. **Treatment-regimen leads for semaglutide obesity:** STEP 1 2.4 mg −14.9% versus −2.4%.
4. **SELECT:** HR 0.80 (6.5% vs 8.0%); this page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent.
5. **Phase 1 abstract has no percent:** NCT04478708 reports weight loss that increased across ascending arms without a published figure in the abstract.
6. **Phase 3 is registered, unpublished:** MARITIME-1 and MARITIME-2 `hasResults` false and PubMed 0 as of 2026-09-02; no Phase 3 percent quoted.
