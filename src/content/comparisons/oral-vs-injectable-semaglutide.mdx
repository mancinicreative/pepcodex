## Overview

Oral and injectable semaglutide are the same molecule in different products. This page quotes two obesity trials: STEP 1 (once-weekly subcutaneous 2.4 mg) and OASIS 1 (once-daily oral 50 mg). Those doses are not Rybelsus 14 mg and are not Ozempic. For type 2 diabetes PIONEER estimands, see [Rybelsus vs Ozempic](/compare/rybelsus-vs-ozempic).

## Key Facts

| **Trial** | OASIS 1 [oasis-1-pmid-37385278] | STEP 1 [step-1-pmid-33567185] |
| **Dose in that trial** | Oral 50 mg daily | Subcutaneous 2.4 mg weekly |
| **Duration** | 68 weeks | 68 weeks |

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs.

### STEP 1 (injectable 2.4 mg, no diabetes)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961; 68 weeks. Primary estimand assessed effects regardless of discontinuation or rescue. Mean body-weight change: −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). At least 5/10/15% reduction: 86.4%/69.1%/50.5% vs 31.5%/12.0%/4.9%. Kilograms: −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### OASIS 1 (oral 50 mg, no diabetes)

PMID 37385278, NCT05035095. n=667 (334 oral, 333 placebo); 68 weeks. Intention-to-treat. Mean body-weight change: −15.1% vs −2.4% (ETD −12.7 percentage points; 95% CI −14.2 to −11.3; P&lt;0.0001). At least 5/10/15/20%: 85%/69%/54%/34% vs 26%/12%/6%/3%. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 80% vs 46% [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].

OASIS 1 is not a Rybelsus 14 mg trial.

### PIONEER 1 (T2D, oral 14 mg — not an obesity head-to-head)

PMID 31186300. n=703; 26 weeks. Treatment-policy HbA1c difference vs placebo at 14 mg: −1.1%. Weight, treatment policy, 14 mg: −2.3 kg [pioneer-1-pmid-31186300]. Full PIONEER 1/2/4 tables are on [Rybelsus vs Ozempic](/compare/rybelsus-vs-ozempic).

## Administration

STEP 1 used a weekly injection. OASIS 1 and PIONEER 1 used daily oral tablets at different doses. This increment did not re-fetch a prescribing-information page; this page does not copy a water-volume or fasting instruction.

## Safety

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; GI discontinuation 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

OASIS 1: GI adverse events 80% vs 46% [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Twin split:** this page is obesity STEP 1 vs OASIS 1. T2D PIONEER lives on Rybelsus vs Ozempic.
2. **OASIS 1 is 50 mg oral**, not Rybelsus 14 mg.
3. **STEP 1 is 2.4 mg weekly**, not an Ozempic 1 mg row.
4. **Cross-trial:** same 68-week duration in STEP 1 and OASIS 1; still not one RCT.
