## Overview

[Mazdutide](/peptides/mazdutide) is a glucagon and GLP-1 receptor dual agonist. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. GLORY-1, GLORY-2, STEP 1, and SELECT are separate trials. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

A [mazdutide versus tirzepatide](/compare/mazdutide-vs-tirzepatide) page covers a different comparator.

## Clinical Trial Data

These are separate programmes, not a head-to-head RCT.

### GLORY-1 (PMID 40421736, NCT05607680)

Phase 3, China, n=610, 1:1:1 to mazdutide 4 mg, mazdutide 6 mg, or placebo for 48 weeks. **Treatment-policy estimand** (effects regardless of early discontinuation and of starting new antiobesity therapies). Coprimary end points: percent body-weight change and ≥5% reduction **at week 32**. CT.gov enrollment 610 actual; COMPLETED; hasResults false (figures below are from the journal abstract, not a results module) [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

| Metric | 4 mg | 6 mg | Placebo |
|--------|------|------|---------|
| **Weight change (week 32, primary window)** | −10.09% | −12.55% | +0.45% |
| **≥5% (week 32)** | 73.9% | 82.0% | 10.5% |
| **Weight change (week 48, also reported)** | −11.00% | −14.01% | +0.30% |
| **≥15% (week 48)** | 35.7% | 49.5% | 2.0% |
| **AE discontinuation** | 1.5% | 0.5% | 1.0% |

Week 32 ≥5% and week 48 ≥15% comparisons vs placebo: P&lt;0.001. Gastrointestinal events were the most frequent adverse events and were mostly mild to moderate. The abstract does not publish a nausea percent [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

### GLORY-2 (PMID 42251595, NCT06164873)

Phase 3, China, 2:1 to mazdutide 9 mg or placebo for 60 weeks as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity. Analyses included participants who received at least one treatment (n=461; 307 vs 154). CT.gov enrollment 462 actual; overallStatus UNKNOWN; hasResults false. The abstract does **not** name treatment-policy versus efficacy (hypothetical). This page does not invent that label [glory-2-pmid-42251595].

At week 60, mean percent body-weight change was **−16.65%** (95% CI −18.19 to −15.12) vs **−1.50%** (95% CI −3.43 to 0.43); between-group difference −15.15% (95% CI −17.22 to −13.09); P&lt;0.001. ≥5% reduction: 84.3% vs 33.1%. AE discontinuation 2.9% vs 0%. Vomiting 53.1% vs 1.3%; nausea 46.9% vs 3.2%; diarrhea 39.4% vs 6.5%; mostly mild to moderate [glory-2-pmid-42251595].

This page does not collapse GLORY-1 6 mg week 48 −14.01% and GLORY-2 9 mg week 60 −16.65% into one range.

### Hsia US phase 2 (PMID 42628555, NCT06124807)

US multicentre phase 2; 179 randomly allocated (32 to 3–6 mg, 48 to 10 mg, 51 to 16 mg, 48 placebo); 48 weeks. **Primary end point: percent body-weight change at 32 weeks, efficacy (hypothetical) estimand.** The abstract has no treatment-policy number. CT.gov enrollment 179 actual; COMPLETED; hasResults true — this page quotes the journal abstract, not the results module [hsia-pmid-42628555].

At 32 weeks, least-squares mean percent change: **−7.3%** (3–6 mg), **−15.6%** (10 mg), **−18.1%** (16 mg) vs **−0.9%** placebo. Estimated treatment differences vs placebo ranged from −6.5% to −17.2% (p&lt;0.0001, all comparisons). Additional body-weight reductions were observed at 48 weeks; the abstract does not publish 48-week percents, so this page does not invent them. AE discontinuation occurred most frequently with 16 mg (**20%**) [hsia-pmid-42628555].

Do not treat Hsia 16 mg −18.1% (efficacy estimand, 32 weeks, US phase 2) as interchangeable with GLORY-1 6 mg −14.01% (treatment-policy, week 48, China phase 3) or with STEP 1 −14.9%.

### STEP 1 (PMID 33567185, NCT03548935)

n=1961 adults with BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with at least one weight-related coexisting condition, without diabetes; 68 weeks; 2:1 to once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. **Primary estimand: treatment-regimen** (effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions). CT.gov enrollment 1961 actual; COMPLETED; hasResults true [step-1-pmid-33567185].

Mean weight change at week 68: **−14.9% vs −2.4%** (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% / ≥10% / ≥15%: 86.4% / 69.1% / 50.5% vs 31.5% / 12.0% / 4.9%. −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Nausea and diarrhea were the most common adverse events. Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

This page does not invent a STEP-programme discontinuation average.

### SELECT (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597)

n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, BMI ≥27, no diabetes; mean follow-up 39.8 months. Primary MACE (death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569/8803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8801 (**8.0%**); **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72 to 0.90); P&lt;0.001. AE discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2%. CT.gov enrollment 17604 actual; COMPLETED; hasResults true [select-pmid-37952131].

This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent.

## Side Effect Profile

GLORY-1: gastrointestinal events most frequent, mostly mild to moderate; AE discontinuation 1.5% / 0.5% / 1.0% (4 mg / 6 mg / placebo). No nausea percent in the abstract [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

GLORY-2: vomiting 53.1% vs 1.3%; nausea 46.9% vs 3.2%; diarrhea 39.4% vs 6.5%; AE discontinuation 2.9% vs 0% [glory-2-pmid-42251595].

Hsia: gastrointestinal-related events most common, mostly mild to moderate; 16 mg AE discontinuation 20% [hsia-pmid-42628555].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; gastrointestinal discontinuation 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

SELECT: AE discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

This page does not invent a matched nausea table across programmes.

## Summary

- **GLORY-1** treatment-policy: 6 mg week 48 **−14.01% vs +0.30%**; primary window was week 32 (−12.55% vs +0.45%) [glory-1-pmid-40421736].
- **GLORY-2** 9 mg week 60 **−16.65% vs −1.50%**; estimand not named in the abstract [glory-2-pmid-42251595].
- **Hsia** 32-week **efficacy (hypothetical)** estimand: 16 mg **−18.1% vs −0.9%**; no treatment-policy number [hsia-pmid-42628555].
- **STEP 1** treatment-regimen: **−14.9% vs −2.4%** at 68 weeks [step-1-pmid-33567185].
- **SELECT**: MACE **HR 0.80** (6.5% vs 8.0%) [select-pmid-37952131].
- Mazdutide is not in Drugs@FDA as of 2026-09-02. Semaglutide is [drugsfda-mazdutide-404] [drugsfda-semaglutide].
