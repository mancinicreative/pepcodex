## What the evidence actually compares

AOD-9604 is a growth-hormone fragment derivative proposed for metabolic effects. It is not a GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist.

FDA's 2024 advisory-committee briefing describes the randomized OPTIONS phase 2b trial: 536 enrolled, 502 randomized, and no statistically significant weight-loss advantage over placebo at its week-12 primary endpoint or over 24 weeks. The full efficacy report was not a peer-reviewed publication in the reviewed set. This negative controlled human result matters more than proposed mechanisms or older animal findings. [FDA AOD-9604 briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/183584/download)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

Mazdutide's published GLORY trials report within-trial weight effects against placebo in Chinese adults. AOD-9604's OPTIONS result should not be treated as a smaller version of those effects; the substances, interventions and trial designs differ. [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [FDA briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/183584/download)

No FDA-approved AOD-9604 product was identified. FDA also flags potential immunogenicity and peptide-impurity concerns for compounded AOD-9604; that notice is a compounding-safety assessment, not efficacy evidence. [FDA compounding notice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks) The source for the AOD-9604 evidence above is [this primary record](https://www.fda.gov/media/183584/download); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.
