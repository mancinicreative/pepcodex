## What the evidence actually compares

Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin analogue studied alone and with semaglutide. Mazdutide is a single GLP-1/glucagon dual agonist. Results from CagriSema, the cagrilintide-plus-semaglutide combination, are not cagrilintide monotherapy results.

A published phase 2 randomized dose-finding trial tested cagrilintide monotherapy against placebo and an active comparator in adults with overweight or obesity. REDEFINE 1 also contained a cagrilintide-alone arm, but its combination-arm primary finding cannot be assigned to that monotherapy arm. [Cagrilintide phase 2, PMID 34798060](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34798060/) · [REDEFINE 1, PMID 40544433](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40544433/)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

Both substances have human randomized weight studies, but they work through different receptor systems and were tested in separate trials. No cited study directly compares cagrilintide monotherapy with mazdutide.

Cagrilintide monotherapy remained investigational in the reviewed sources; no FDA approval for it or mazdutide was verified. China-specific mazdutide approval is a separate sponsor-reported claim. The source for the Cagrilintide evidence above is [this primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34798060/); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.
