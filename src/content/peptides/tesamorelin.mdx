Tesamorelin is a synthetic GHRH analog. The strongest direct evidence is for
reducing CT-measured visceral abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated
lipodystrophy, the population named in the US EGRIFTA WR indication. In the
2007 pivotal trial, VAT changed -15.2% with tesamorelin and +5.0% with placebo
at 26 weeks. Those figures describe **VAT, not trunk fat**. The second pivotal
trial and the pooled analysis support the same population and endpoint; the
pooled report reuses the trial participants. [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf),
[PMID:18057338](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18057338/),
[PMID:20101189](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20101189/),
[PMID:20554713](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20554713/).

### How it works and what was measured

The FDA label describes receptor binding, pulsatile endogenous GH release,
and increases in IGF-1. The pivotal trials then measured VAT by CT. This
supports the causal chain through an imaging endpoint in the indicated
population; it does not establish better cardiovascular outcomes or safer GH
replacement. The 2007 trial's 81% IGF-1 increase is a biomarker result from
that trial, not a general expected response. [FDA label, sections 12 and
14](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf),
[PMID:18057338](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18057338/).

### Findings outside the approved indication

In a 60-person randomized study of abdominal obesity with reduced GH
secretion, VAT fell relative to placebo. This does not establish an indication
for general obesity. In a separate HIV-associated NAFLD trial, hepatic fat
fraction fell over 12 months and fibrosis progression was less frequent in a
small paired-biopsy subset. Related transcriptomic and proteomic publications
analyzed the same trial, not new patient cohorts. A completed non-HIV fatty
liver study has posted registry results, with only 18 and 16 participants in
the two primary outcome analyses; the clinical importance needs independent
review. Tesamorelin is not FDA-approved for fatty liver disease.
[PMID:23015655](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23015655/),
[PMID:25038357](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25038357/),
[PMID:31611038](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31611038/),
[PMID:32701508](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32701508/),
[PMID:32270862](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32270862/),
[PMID:34006921](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34006921/),
[NCT03375788](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03375788).

A 73-person open-label HIV cognition trial reported no significant
between-group cognitive benefit at six months (P=.673). An earlier trial in
older adults reported a favorable signal, but a 2026 placebo-controlled
22-person pilot found no significant direct changes in its study measures.
Neither establishes a cognitive indication.
[PMID:39813152](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39813152/),
[PMID:22869065](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869065/),
[PMID:42382101](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42382101/).

### Safety and regulatory limits

The March 2025 EGRIFTA WR label lists contraindications for hypothalamic-
pituitary-axis disruption, active malignancy, known hypersensitivity, and
pregnancy. It warns about persistent IGF-1 elevation, glucose intolerance or
diabetes, fluid retention, hypersensitivity, injection-site reactions, and
acute critical illness. It identifies potential interactions with drugs
metabolized by CYP450 enzymes and with glucocorticoid replacement. The label
does not provide tesamorelin-specific evidence for combining it with other
GH secretagogues or incretin therapies. EGRIFTA WR and SV are distinct,
non-substitutable formulations. [FDA EGRIFTA WR label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf).

This page summarizes research and labeling. It does not provide treatment,
dosing, preparation, or purchasing guidance.
