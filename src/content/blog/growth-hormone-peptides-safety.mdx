## What this page is for

Most GH-releasing peptides discussed online are **not FDA-approved** and have limited human safety data. Class effects of elevated GH (arthralgia, fluid retention, glucose intolerance, theoretical IGF-1/cancer concern) are a reason to read a labelled product — not a reason to invent an AE table for an unapproved listing [pmid-15623804].

Sermorelin was approved historically; it is not a current stand-in for ipamorelin or CJC-1295. The labelled GHRH analog on this site is [tesamorelin](/peptides/tesamorelin); its AE frequencies live on [tesamorelin safety](/safety/tesamorelin-safety). Do not copy that table onto unapproved secretagogues.

WADA prohibits GH secretagogues in sport. Unregulated listings cannot be assumed to match trial-grade material. Suspected GH deficiency is an endocrinology evaluation, not a research-chem substitution.
