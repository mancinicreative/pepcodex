## Overview

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). Retatrutide is a triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). SURMOUNT-1, SURMOUNT-2, the retatrutide phase 2 obesity trial, and TRANSCEND-T2D-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse a 48-week phase 2 secondary into a 72-week phase 3 treatment-regimen figure.

Dossiers: [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), [retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide). Related compares: [retatrutide vs semaglutide](/compare/retatrutide-vs-semaglutide), [tirzepatide vs semaglutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, populations, and endpoints differ.

### Retatrutide phase 2 obesity (not TRIUMPH)

PMID 37366315, NCT04881760. Phase 2; n=338; 48 weeks. Primary end point: percentage change in body weight at **24 weeks**. Week 48 change is a secondary end point. The abstract reports least-squares means and does not name a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy estimand.

Week 24: −7.2% (1 mg), −12.9% (combined 4 mg), −17.3% (combined 8 mg), −17.5% (12 mg), −1.6% placebo.

Week 48: −8.7% (1 mg), −17.1% (combined 4 mg), −22.8% (combined 8 mg), −24.2% (12 mg), −2.1% placebo.

At week 48, a reduction of 5% / 10% / 15% or more occurred in 100% / 93% / 83% on 12 mg vs 27% / 9% / 2% on placebo. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events, dose-related, and mostly mild to moderate. Dose-dependent heart-rate increases peaked at 24 weeks and declined thereafter [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

This page does not restore a 4 mg 48-week −17.5% row. −17.5% in the abstract is the 12 mg week-24 figure.

### TRANSCEND-T2D-1 (type 2 diabetes phase 3, not obesity)

PMID 42250575, NCT06354660. Phase 3; n=537; 40 weeks. Primary end point: HbA1c change. Treatment-regimen estimand. HbA1c: −1.69% (4 mg), −1.86% (9 mg), −1.94% (12 mg), −0.81% placebo (P&lt;0.0001 vs placebo). Body weight (key secondary): −11.5%, −13.9%, −15.3% vs −2.6% placebo. Discontinuation for adverse events: 2–5% vs 0% placebo [transcend-t2d-1-pmid-42250575].

This is not a TRIUMPH obesity readout.

### TRIUMPH (design only)

PMID 41090431 describes four phase 3 studies (TRIUMPH-1 through TRIUMPH-4) in over 5,800 participants. The primary weight-management end point is percent change in body weight. The abstract publishes no efficacy percent. A PubMed search on 2026-09-02 for `"TRIUMPH-1" retatrutide weight` returned only this design paper. This page does not quote an unpublished TRIUMPH obesity percent [triumph-design-pmid-41090431].

PMID 42608321 is a phase 2 post-hoc biomarker analysis of NCT04881760 and NCT04867785. It is not a TRIUMPH efficacy paper [retatrutide-biomarkers-pmid-42608321].

### SURMOUNT-1 and SURMOUNT-2 (tirzepatide)

**SURMOUNT-1** (PMID 35658024, NCT04184622): n=2539; 72 weeks; no diabetes. Treatment-regimen mean change at week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg), −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). At least 5% reduction: 85%, 89%, 91% vs 35% placebo. At least 20% reduction: 50% (10 mg) and 57% (15 mg; 95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5/10/15 mg and placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

**SURMOUNT-2** (PMID 37385275, NCT04657003): n=938 with obesity and type 2 diabetes; 72 weeks. Treatment-regimen body-weight change: −12.8% (10 mg), −14.7% (15 mg), −3.2% placebo. At least 5% reduction: 79–83% vs 32%. Gastrointestinal events were the most frequent adverse events; few led to discontinuation (&lt;5%) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275].

## Side Effect Profile

Retatrutide phase 2: gastrointestinal events most common, dose-related, mostly mild to moderate; heart-rate increases peaked at week 24 and then declined. The abstract does not publish a nausea percent [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

TRANSCEND-T2D-1: gastrointestinal events most frequent; discontinuation 2–5% vs 0% placebo [transcend-t2d-1-pmid-42250575].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation 6.2% (15 mg) vs 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** 48-week phase 2 and 72-week SURMOUNT-1 are different trials.
2. **Phase 2 primary is 24 weeks:** the −24.2% (12 mg) vs −2.1% placebo figure is a week-48 secondary least-squares mean.
3. **SURMOUNT-1 treatment-regimen:** 15 mg −20.9% vs −3.1%; ≥20% at 15 mg is 57% (95% CI 53–61).
4. **TRIUMPH:** design paper only in this increment; no unpublished obesity percent.
