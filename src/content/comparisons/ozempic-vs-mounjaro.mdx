## Overview

[Ozempic](/peptides/semaglutide) is a semaglutide brand. [Mounjaro](/peptides/tirzepatide) is a tirzepatide brand. This page quotes the type 2 diabetes head-to-head (SURPASS-2) and two cardiovascular outcomes trials that used those molecules. It does not invent a SUSTAIN-versus-SURPASS program range, a source census, or an unpublished head-to-head cardiovascular percent.

The molecule-level twin is [tirzepatide vs semaglutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-semaglutide). Obesity-brand evidence is [Wegovy vs Zepbound](/compare/wegovy-vs-zepbound).

## Head-to-head: type 2 diabetes (SURPASS-2)

PMID 34170647, NCT03987919. Open-label, 40-week, phase 3 trial. n=1879. Tirzepatide 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg versus semaglutide **1 mg**. Baseline mean glycated hemoglobin 8.28%; mean weight 93.7 kg. Primary end point: change in glycated hemoglobin from baseline to 40 weeks. The abstract does not label treatment-regimen versus efficacy [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

Estimated mean HbA1c change:

| Arm | HbA1c change | Difference vs semaglutide 1 mg |
|-----|--------------|--------------------------------|
| Tirzepatide 5 mg | −2.01 percentage points | −0.15 (95% CI −0.28 to −0.03; P=0.02) |
| Tirzepatide 10 mg | −2.24 percentage points | −0.39 (95% CI −0.51 to −0.26; P&lt;0.001) |
| Tirzepatide 15 mg | −2.30 percentage points | −0.45 (95% CI −0.57 to −0.32; P&lt;0.001) |
| Semaglutide 1 mg | −1.86 percentage points | — |

The abstract reports that tirzepatide at all doses was noninferior and superior to semaglutide on that HbA1c end point. Body-weight least-squares mean estimated treatment differences versus semaglutide were −1.9 kg, −3.6 kg, and −5.5 kg (P&lt;0.001 for all). The abstract does **not** publish absolute mean kilogram change per arm, an HbA1c-below-7% rate, or a ≥10% weight-loss rate; those rows were not invented [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

Gastrointestinal events (primarily mild to moderate): nausea 17 to 22% (tirzepatide) vs 18% (semaglutide); diarrhea 13 to 16% vs 12%; vomiting 6 to 10% vs 8%. Hypoglycemia (blood glucose &lt;54 mg/dL): 0.6% / 0.2% / 1.7% on tirzepatide 5 / 10 / 15 mg vs 0.4% on semaglutide. Serious adverse events: 5 to 7% vs 3% [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

## Cardiovascular outcomes (not a head-to-head)

These are separate programmes. SUSTAIN-6 compared semaglutide with placebo. SURPASS-CVOT compared tirzepatide with dulaglutide. Neither is an Ozempic-versus-Mounjaro cardiovascular trial. SURPASS-CVOT is reported; this page does not describe it as ongoing.

### SUSTAIN-6 (semaglutide vs placebo)

PMID 27633186, NCT01720446. n=3297 adults with type 2 diabetes on a standard-care regimen; once-weekly semaglutide 0.5 mg or 1.0 mg versus placebo for 104 weeks. The paper hypothesized noninferiority (margin 1.8 for the upper bound of the 95% CI). Primary composite (first cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 108/1648 (**6.6%**) vs 146/1649 (**8.9%**); **HR 0.74** (95% CI 0.58 to 0.95; P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority). This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].

Nonfatal myocardial infarction: 2.9% vs 3.9% (HR 0.74; 95% CI 0.51 to 1.08; P=0.12). Nonfatal stroke: 1.6% vs 2.7% (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.38 to 0.99; P=0.04). Rates of death from cardiovascular causes were similar. New or worsening nephropathy was lower with semaglutide. Retinopathy complications (vitreous hemorrhage, blindness, or conditions requiring an intravitreal agent or photocoagulation) were higher (HR 1.76; 95% CI 1.11 to 2.78; P=0.02) [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].

### SURPASS-CVOT (tirzepatide vs dulaglutide)

PMID 41406444, NCT04255433. Active-comparator, double-blind, noninferiority trial in type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. Weekly tirzepatide (up to 15 mg) versus dulaglutide 1.5 mg. Modified intention-to-treat: 6586 tirzepatide vs 6579 dulaglutide (13,299 randomised; 134 later excluded). Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, myocardial infarction, or stroke): 801 (**12.2%**) vs 862 (**13.1%**); **HR 0.92** (95.3% CI 0.83 to 1.01); P=0.003 for noninferiority; P=0.09 for superiority. Noninferior, not superior, to dulaglutide. This is not a placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcomes trial and not a semaglutide comparison [surpass-cvot-pmid-41406444].

## What these trials do not settle

- SURPASS-2 used semaglutide **1 mg** in type 2 diabetes. It is not an obesity head-to-head against 2.4 mg.
- SUSTAIN-6 and SURPASS-CVOT used different comparators (placebo vs dulaglutide) and different populations.
- This page does not quote SELECT, STEP 1, SURMOUNT-1, or SURMOUNT-5. Those obesity or obesity-brand trials are on [tirzepatide vs semaglutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-semaglutide) and [Wegovy vs Zepbound](/compare/wegovy-vs-zepbound).
