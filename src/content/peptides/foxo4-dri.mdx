FOXO4-DRI is a D-retro-inverso peptide designed to selectively eliminate senescent cells by disrupting the FOXO4-p53 interaction that allows these "zombie cells" to evade apoptosis.

### How It Works (Simplified)

FOXO4-DRI triggers selective death of senescent cells through a targeted mechanism:

**Key Research:** Baar MP et al. (2017) demonstrated selective senolysis in aged mice with restoration of fitness, fur density, and renal function. [PMID:28340339](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28340339/)

### Important Limitations

- **No human trials** — All efficacy data is from animal and cell studies
- **Senolysis is not uniformly beneficial** — In mouse and rat pulmonary hypertension models, clearing senescent cells (including with FOXO4-DRI) *promoted* disease development and progression. [PMID:36515093](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36515093/)
- **Selectivity ≠ benefit** — In human chondrocytes FOXO4-DRI removed the senescent cells but did not improve the cells' chondrogenic potential. [PMID:33996787](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33996787/)
- **Safety profile unknown** — Long-term effects of senescent cell depletion unclear
- **Complex synthesis** — D-retro-inverso peptides are difficult to manufacture
- **Product quality concerns** — Research-grade only, variable purity
