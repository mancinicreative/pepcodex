## Overview

Rybelsus and Ozempic are semaglutide products for type 2 diabetes: a once-daily oral tablet and a once-weekly subcutaneous injection. PIONEER 1, PIONEER 2, and PIONEER 4 studied oral semaglutide. None of those three abstracts is a head-to-head of Rybelsus versus Ozempic.

| Aspect | Rybelsus | Ozempic |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Active ingredient** | Semaglutide | Semaglutide |
| **Route** | Oral tablet | Subcutaneous injection |
| **FDA status on this page** | FDA-approved product | FDA-approved product |

## Clinical Trial Data

Treatment-policy estimands lead. Trial-product figures are labelled when quoted.

### PIONEER 1 (oral semaglutide vs placebo, 26 weeks)

PMID 31186300. n=703; mean baseline HbA1c 8.0%. Placebo-adjusted HbA1c differences at week 26, treatment policy: −0.6% (3 mg), −0.9% (7 mg), −1.1% (14 mg). Trial product: −0.7%, −1.2%, −1.4% (P&lt;0.001 for all). Body weight, treatment policy: −0.1 kg, −0.9 kg, −2.3 kg (14 mg P&lt;0.001); trial product 14 mg −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events. Trial-product discontinuations 2.3–7.4% vs 2.2% placebo [pioneer-1-pmid-31186300].

Do not treat the trial-product −1.4% as the treatment-policy number.

### PIONEER 2 (oral semaglutide 14 mg vs empagliflozin 25 mg)

PMID 31530666. n=412 oral semaglutide, n=410 empagliflozin; 52-week trial; primary HbA1c at week 26. Treatment policy week 26: HbA1c −1.3% vs −0.9% (ETD −0.4%; 95% CI −0.6 to −0.3; P&lt;0.0001). Trial product week 26: −1.4% vs −0.9% (ETD −0.5%; 95% CI −0.7 to −0.4; P&lt;0.0001). Superior weight loss was not confirmed at week 26 on the treatment-policy estimand. Week 52 trial-product weight: −4.7 kg vs −3.8 kg (P=0.0114). Gastrointestinal adverse events were more common with oral semaglutide [pioneer-2-pmid-31530666].

### PIONEER 4 (oral semaglutide vs subcutaneous liraglutide vs placebo)

PMID 31186120, NCT02863419. n=711 (285 oral semaglutide, 284 liraglutide, 142 placebo). Treatment policy was the primary estimand. Week 26 HbA1c: −1.2% oral semaglutide, −1.1% liraglutide, −0.2% placebo. Oral semaglutide was non-inferior to liraglutide (ETD −0.1%; 95% CI −0.3 to 0.0; P&lt;0.0001) and superior to placebo (ETD −1.1%; 95% CI −1.2 to −0.9; P&lt;0.0001). Week 26 weight, treatment policy: −4.4 kg vs −3.1 kg vs −0.5 kg (oral vs liraglutide ETD −1.2 kg; 95% CI −1.9 to −0.6; P=0.0003). Adverse events: 80% oral semaglutide, 74% liraglutide, 67% placebo [pioneer-4-pmid-31186120].

This is not an Ozempic trial.
