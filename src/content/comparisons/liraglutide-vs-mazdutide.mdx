## What the evidence actually compares

Liraglutide activates GLP-1 receptors. Mazdutide activates GLP-1 and glucagon receptors. A mechanistic difference does not establish a clinical advantage.

Liraglutide has mature product-specific trial and regulatory evidence. The LEADER cardiovascular-outcomes trial studied liraglutide in people with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk; that is a distinct endpoint and population from mazdutide's cited GLORY weight trials. Saxenda's US weight-management indication and Victoza's US type 2 diabetes uses belong to separate labels, rather than to every liraglutide preparation. [LEADER, PMID 27295427](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27295427/) · [FDA Saxenda label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/206321s025lbl.pdf) · [Victoza label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5a9ef4ea-c76a-4d34-a604-27c5b505f5a4)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

No cited randomized trial directly compares liraglutide with mazdutide. Their weight, diabetes and cardiovascular studies ask different questions; a publication count or percentage from dissimilar trials is not a treatment ranking.

US FDA approval is verified for the labeled Saxenda and Victoza products and their respective indications. No FDA mazdutide approval was verified; Innovent reports specified Chinese mazdutide approvals. The source for the Liraglutide evidence above is [this primary record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/206321s025lbl.pdf); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.

This comparison is educational research context, not medical advice or a treatment protocol.
