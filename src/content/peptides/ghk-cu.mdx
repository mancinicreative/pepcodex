GHK-Cu is the **copper(II) complex of glycyl-histidyl-lysine (GHK)**. Findings for free GHK, a specific topical formulation and an injected copper complex should be assessed separately. The complex increased collagen and other extracellular-matrix measures in a rat wound-chamber experiment. That supports a mechanistic hypothesis, not a patient benefit. [PMID:8227353](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8227353/)

### What human topical studies found

In a randomized study after CO2 laser resurfacing, 13 participants completed topical regimens with or without GHK-Cu. Blinded objective assessments found **no significant between-group improvement** in erythema, wrinkles or overall skin quality; a patient questionnaire favored the GHK-Cu regimen for perceived skin quality. In an 86-person randomized venous-ulcer trial, tripeptide-copper cream did **not** outperform vehicle, while silver sulfadiazine reduced ulcer area more. These different settings and formulations cannot establish a broad skin or wound-healing effect. [PMID:16847171](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16847171/) · [PMID:1495150](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1495150/)

Gene-expression reports and fibroblast experiments remain useful for mechanism research, but a count of changed transcripts does not demonstrate clinical regeneration or rejuvenation. Reviews summarizing cosmetic claims are secondary sources and should not be counted as independent human trials. [PMID:22937864](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22937864/)

### Route and safety boundary

FDA separately flags **GHK-Cu for injectable routes** in its compounding safety review, citing possible immunogenicity from aggregation and peptide-related impurities and limited human safety information. Cosmetic use does not validate injectable use. No clinical combination evidence was identified for the other peptides previously listed on this page. [FDA compounding safety review](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks)
