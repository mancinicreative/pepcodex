## Overview

[VK2735](/peptides/vk2735) is Viking Therapeutics' investigational dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. VENTURE, STEP 1, OASIS 1, and SELECT are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

A related dual-agonist compare is [VK2735 vs tirzepatide](/compare/vk2735-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are separate programmes, not a head-to-head RCT.

### VENTURE (subcutaneous VK2735, 13 weeks)

**VENTURE** (PMID 41508550, NCT06068946): Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, weekly subcutaneous VK2735 in adults with obesity or overweight and at least one weight-related comorbidity. Participants with diabetes were ineligible. Primary endpoint: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 13. Mean weight reduction ranged from 9.2 kg (2.5 mg; 9.1%) to 14.6 kg (15 mg; **14.7%**); placebo 1.8 kg (**1.7%**). Across active-treatment groups, ≥5% loss occurred in 93% (130/140) vs 12% (4/34) placebo. Common adverse events were gastrointestinal. The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen vs efficacy estimand [venture-pmid-41508550].

CT.gov: COMPLETED; enrollment 176 actual; start 2023-08-31 actual; primary completion 2024-02-27 actual; hasResults **false**. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module.

This page does not quote a 2.4 mg VK2735 row.

### STEP 1 and OASIS 1 (semaglutide weight trials)

**STEP 1** (PMID 33567185, NCT03548935): n=1961 adults with overweight or obesity, no diabetes; 68 weeks; once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo plus lifestyle intervention. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (**treatment-regimen**). Mean change −14.9% vs −2.4% (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% 86.4% vs 31.5%; ≥10% 69.1% vs 12.0%; ≥15% 50.5% vs 4.9%. Mean change −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

**OASIS 1** (PMID 37385278, NCT05035095): oral semaglutide 50 mg versus placebo; n=667 randomised; 68 weeks; no type 2 diabetes. Coprimary endpoints assessed regardless of treatment discontinuation or other bodyweight-lowering therapies (intention-to-treat / treatment-policy). Estimated mean change −15.1% vs −2.4% (ETD −12.7 percentage points; 95% CI −14.2 to −11.3; P&lt;0.0001). ≥5% 85% vs 26%. Gastrointestinal adverse events 80% vs 46% [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].

OASIS 1 studied the 50 mg oral tablet. That is not a Rybelsus label-strength table.

Do not divide VENTURE’s 13-week percent by STEP 1’s 68-week percent.

### SELECT (cardiovascular outcomes)

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, overweight or obesity, no diabetes. Primary MACE (CV death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke) 569/8803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8801 (**8.0%**); HR **0.80** (95% CI 0.72–0.90); P&lt;0.001. Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

This page quotes the hazard ratio and event rates. It does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent.

VENTURE and VANQUISH are not cardiovascular-outcomes trials.

## Side Effect Profile

VENTURE: gastrointestinal events were common; reported frequency decreased after titration to steady state. The abstract does not publish a nausea percent [venture-pmid-41508550].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% placebo [step-1-pmid-33567185].

OASIS 1: gastrointestinal adverse events 80% vs 46% placebo [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].
