## Overview

Orforglipron is a nonpeptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 peptide agonist given weekly. ATTAIN-1 and SURMOUNT-1 are separate 72-week obesity trials, not one RCT. Semaglutide cross-trials are on [Orforglipron vs Semaglutide](/compare/orforglipron-vs-semaglutide).

## Key Facts

| Aspect | Orforglipron | Tirzepatide |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Molecule** | Nonpeptide small molecule | Peptide |
| **Targets** | GLP-1 | GIP + GLP-1 |
| **Route in the trials quoted here** | Once-daily oral (ATTAIN-1) | Once-weekly injection (SURMOUNT-1) |
| **FDA status on this page** | Foundayo, NDA 220934, ORIG AP 2026-04-01 [drugsfda-nda-220934] | Mounjaro and Zepbound (FDA-approved products) |

## Clinical Trial Data

### ATTAIN-1 (orforglipron, obesity without diabetes)

PMID 40960239, NCT05869903. n=3127; 72 weeks; treatment-regimen estimand. Mean body-weight change: −7.5% (6 mg), −8.4% (12 mg), −11.2% (36 mg) vs −2.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001). At 36 mg, ≥10/15/20% reduction: 54.6%, 36.0%, 18.4% vs 12.9%, 5.9%, 2.8% placebo. Adverse events led to discontinuation in 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7% placebo. Gastrointestinal effects were the most common adverse events [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

### Phase 2 (orforglipron)

PMID 37351564, NCT05051579. n=272; 36 weeks. Week 36 mean change ranged from −9.4% to −14.7% vs −2.3% placebo. Week 26 (primary) ranged from −8.6% to −12.6% vs −2.0% placebo. Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 10–17% across dose cohorts [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564].

The abstract publishes a range, not a 36 mg vs 45 mg split.

### SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2539; 72 weeks; treatment-regimen estimand. Week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg) vs −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001). At least 20% reduction: 50% (10 mg) and 57% (15 mg; 95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5/10/15 mg and placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Side Effects

ATTAIN-1: AE discontinuation 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7%; GI effects most common [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

Phase 2: GI discontinuation 10–17% [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564].

SURMOUNT-1: GI events most common; discontinuation 6.2% (15 mg) vs 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].
