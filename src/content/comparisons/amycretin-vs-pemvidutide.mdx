## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [Pemvidutide](/peptides/pemvidutide) is Altimmune's investigational dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist (ClinicalTrials.gov also lists ALT-801 on earlier records). The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, IMPACT, and the MASLD MRI-PDFF papers are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [pemvidutide vs semaglutide](/compare/pemvidutide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Indications, durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### Pemvidutide IMPACT MASH phase 2b (Lancet)

PMID 41237796, NCT05989711. Phase 2b; 212 patients randomly assigned 1:2:2 to weekly pemvidutide 1.2 mg, 1.8 mg, or placebo, without titration, in biopsy-confirmed MASH with fibrosis stage F2 or F3. Dual **primary** endpoints at 24 weeks in the **intention-to-treat** population: MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis, or at least one-stage fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH.

MASH resolution without fibrosis worsening (ITT):

- 1.2 mg: 24 (58%) of 41 vs 18 (20%) of 86 placebo (difference 38%; 95% CI 21–56; P&lt;0.0001)
- 1.8 mg: 45 (52%) of 85 vs 18 (20%) of 86 placebo (difference 32%; 95% CI 19–46; P&lt;0.0001)

Fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH (ITT; **not met**):

- 1.2 mg: 13 (33%) of 41 vs 24 (28%) of 86 placebo (difference 5%; 95% CI −13 to 22; P=0.59)
- 1.8 mg: 30 (36%) of 85 vs 24 (28%) of 86 placebo (difference 8%; 95% CI −6 to 22; P=0.27)

The authors state pemvidutide met MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis at 24 weeks and did **not** meet fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH at this timepoint [pemvidutide-impact-pmid-41237796]. Adverse events: 32 (78%) of 41 on 1.2 mg, 69 (81%) of 85 on 1.8 mg, 58 (67%) of 86 on placebo; most mild or moderate. Discontinuations due to adverse events: none of 41 (1.2 mg), one (1%) of 85 (1.8 mg), two (2%) of 86 (placebo).

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05989711 as completed Phase 2 (hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Altimmune, Inc. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT05989711` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 41237796 only.

Do not read IMPACT histology as an obesity Phase 3 weight-loss result, and do not divide IMPACT percents by the amycretin 36-week estimated mean.

### Pemvidutide MASLD papers (MRI-PDFF, not obesity Phase 3)

PMID 39002641, NCT05006885. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled. Adults with BMI ≥28.0 kg/m² and liver fat content ≥10% by **magnetic resonance imaging–proton density fat fraction (MRI-PDFF)** received weekly pemvidutide 1.2 mg, 1.8 mg, 2.4 mg, or placebo for 12 weeks. Ninety-four patients were randomised and dosed. The paper's primary efficacy endpoint was relative reduction (%) from baseline in liver fat content after 12 weeks. Relative liver-fat reductions: 46.6% (95% CI −63.7 to −29.6), 68.5% (−84.4 to −52.5), and 57.1% (−76.1 to −38.1) vs 4.4% (−20.2 to 11.3) for placebo (P&lt;0.001, all arms). Maximal body-weight change reported in the abstract was −4.3% (P&lt;0.001) at 1.8 mg [pemvidutide-masld-pmid-39002641]. ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05006885 as completed Phase 1 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02); registry primary is treatment-emergent adverse events. PubMed `NCT05006885` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 39002641 only.

PMID 41113119, NCT05292911. Double-blind 12-week extension of that MASLD trial (64 participants; total 24 weeks at the originally assigned arm). Primary efficacy endpoint was relative reduction (%) from baseline in liver fat content by **MRI-PDFF** after 24 weeks. Relative liver-fat reductions: 56.3%, 75.2%, and 76.4% vs 14.0% for placebo (P&lt;0.001, all arms). Body weight was also reduced by 6.2% (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo) over 24 weeks [pemvidutide-masld-ext-pmid-41113119]. ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05292911 as completed Phase 1 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05292911` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 41113119 only.

These two papers are liver-fat (MRI-PDFF) studies. This page does not treat −4.3% or −6.2% as an obesity Phase 3 result.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; the quoted pemvidutide lead figure is IMPACT 24-week ITT MASH histology.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **IMPACT:** ITT MASH resolution 58% (1.2 mg) and 52% (1.8 mg) vs 20% placebo; fibrosis co-primary not met (33% / 36% vs 28%).
5. **MASLD papers:** MRI-PDFF liver-fat reductions. Weight-change figures in those abstracts are not obesity Phase 3.
6. **MOMENTUM:** completed Phase 2 obesity record; hasResults false; PubMed 0 as of 2026-09-02. No unpublished obesity percent is quoted.
