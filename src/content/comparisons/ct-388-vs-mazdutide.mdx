## What the evidence actually compares

CT-388 (enicepatide; RO7795068) is an investigational peptide activating GLP-1 and GIP receptors with signaling bias. Mazdutide activates GLP-1 and glucagon receptors. The receptor pair is therefore different.

A published report combines preclinical experiments with a randomized phase 1 trial (NCT04838405) in otherwise healthy adults with overweight or obesity, including single-dose and four-week repeated-dose cohorts. Longer-duration weight figures publicized by the sponsor should not be presented as peer-reviewed phase 3 outcomes. [CT-388 phase 1, PMID 41319798](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41319798/)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

The cited CT-388 clinical exposure is early and short; the published mazdutide GLORY weight trials have longer phase 3 follow-up. Neither study set makes a direct comparative efficacy or long-term safety estimate between the molecules.

CT-388 remained investigational in the reviewed sources; no FDA approval for it or mazdutide was verified. Mazdutide's specified China approvals are sponsor-reported. The source for the CT-388 evidence above is [this primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41319798/); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.

This comparison is educational research context, not medical advice or a treatment protocol.
