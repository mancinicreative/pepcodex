## Overview

Liraglutide and semaglutide are both GLP-1 receptor agonists developed by Novo Nordisk. SCALE used once-daily liraglutide; STEP 1 used once-weekly semaglutide. Both products named here are FDA-approved. This page does not collapse those programmes into one efficacy ratio.

This comparison illustrates the evolution of GLP-1 therapeutics and helps understand the practical differences between these related medications.

## Structure Comparison

| Aspect | Liraglutide | Semaglutide |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Homology to GLP-1** | 97% | 94% |
| **Amino Acid Changes** | 1 (Lys34→Arg) | 2 (Ala8→Aib, Lys34→Arg) |
| **Fatty Acid Chain** | C16 (palmitic acid) | C18 diacid chain |
| **Half-life** | ~13 hours | ~165 hours (~7 days) |

### Why Semaglutide Lasts Longer

- Aib substitution at position 8 resists DPP-4 degradation
- C18 fatty acid chain enhances albumin binding
- Slower release from albumin reservoir
- Allows weekly dosing vs. daily

## Efficacy Comparison

SCALE (no T2D) and STEP 1 (no T2D) are separate obesity programmes. SUSTAIN 10 is a type 2 diabetes head-to-head at 30 weeks. Do not divide STEP 1’s percent by SCALE’s kilograms.

### SCALE vs STEP 1 (obesity trials, not head-to-head)

| Outcome | SCALE (PMID 26132939) | STEP 1 (PMID 33567185) |
|---------|----------------------|------------------------|
| **Design** | n=3731, 56 weeks, no T2D | n=1961, 68 weeks, no T2D |
| **Mean change** | −8.4 kg vs −2.8 kg placebo (difference −5.6 kg; 95% CI −6.0 to −5.1). Abstract reports kg, not a mean percent [scale-pmid-26132939] | −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo (ETD −12.4 pp; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5) [step-1-pmid-33567185] |
| **≥5%** | 63.2% vs 27.1% [scale-pmid-26132939] | 86.4% vs 31.5% [step-1-pmid-33567185] |

### SUSTAIN 10 (T2D head-to-head, 30 weeks)

PMID 31539622, n=577. Mean HbA1c (baseline 8.2%) decreased by 1.7% with semaglutide vs 1.0% with liraglutide (ETD −0.69%; 95% CI −0.82 to −0.56; P&lt;0.0001). Mean body weight (baseline 96.9 kg) decreased by 5.8 kg vs 1.9 kg (ETD −3.83 kg; 95% CI −4.57 to −3.09; P&lt;0.0001) [sustain-10-pmid-31539622].

This is not an obesity-dose comparison with SCALE or STEP 1.

## Cardiovascular Evidence

Do not headline a rounded percent-reduction without the hazard ratio.

**LEADER** (PMID 27295427, NCT01179048): n=9340; median 3.8 years. Primary MACE 608/4668 (13.0%) vs 694/4672 (14.9%); HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.97); P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority, P=0.01 for superiority. CV death 219 (4.7%) vs 278 (6.0%); HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.66–0.93) [leader-pmid-27295427].

**SUSTAIN-6** (PMID 27633186): n=3297; 104 weeks. Primary MACE 108/1648 (6.6%) vs 146/1649 (8.9%); HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95); P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority. CV death rates were similar. Retinopathy complications HR 1.76 (95% CI 1.11–2.78; P=0.02) [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting CVD, overweight or obesity, no diabetes. Primary MACE 569/8803 (6.5%) vs 701/8801 (8.0%); HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90); P&lt;0.001. Mean follow-up 39.8 months [select-pmid-37952131].

## Side Effect Comparison

SCALE: most frequently reported adverse events were mild or moderate nausea and diarrhea; no nausea percent in the abstract [scale-pmid-26132939].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; GI events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% placebo [step-1-pmid-33567185].

SUSTAIN 10: gastrointestinal disorders 43.9% vs 38.3%; AEs leading to premature discontinuation 11.4% vs 6.6% (semaglutide vs liraglutide) [sustain-10-pmid-31539622].
