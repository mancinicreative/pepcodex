## What the evidence actually compares

5-Amino-1MQ is a small-molecule inhibitor of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT), not a peptide or incretin receptor agonist.

The cited 2018 experiment tested 5-Amino-1MQ in diet-induced obese mice and measured body weight and adipose endpoints. The bounded source set does not identify a human therapeutic efficacy trial for this compound. Mouse findings cannot establish human weight-loss benefit or safety. [Neelakantan et al., PMID 29155147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29155147/)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

The distinction is a human randomized-trial evidence gap, not a numerical source-count contest: mazdutide has published controlled weight trials in people, whereas the cited 5-Amino-1MQ result is preclinical. Different mechanisms and species leave no valid efficacy ratio or combination conclusion.

No FDA-approved 5-Amino-1MQ product was identified in the bounded regulatory review. Mazdutide's reported Chinese approvals do not confer US approval. The source for the 5-Amino-1MQ evidence above is [this primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29155147/); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.
