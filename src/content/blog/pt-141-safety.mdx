The adverse-event frequencies, cardiovascular cautions, naltrexone interaction, and labelled limits live on [PT-141 safety](/safety/pt-141-safety). The compound record is [PT-141](/peptides/pt-141). This post does not recap that table.

## What this page is for

Vyleesi (bremelanotide) is an FDA-approved product for hypoactive sexual desire disorder in premenopausal women. The development name PT-141 still appears on research-chem listings. Those listings are not the labelled product, and they are not a second safety dataset.

RECONNECT is the Phase 3 record the label draws on [pmid-31599840]. Registry context for that programme is [NCT01382719](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01382719). How to read frequencies, who the label excludes, and the boxed-style cautions is the job of the [safety page](/safety/pt-141-safety).

## What not to treat as the labelled product

- A vial sold as "PT-141" is not Vyleesi
- Safety numbers from RECONNECT do not transfer to an unregulated listing
- The indication is premenopausal women with HSDD — not a general sexual-function claim

For the beginner name-vs-label split, see [PT-141 vs Vyleesi](/blog/what-is-pt-141).
