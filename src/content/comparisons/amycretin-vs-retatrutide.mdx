## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [Retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide) is Eli Lilly's investigational GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor agonist. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, the retatrutide phase 2 obesity trial, and TRANSCEND-T2D-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse a 36-week early-phase estimated mean into a 48-week phase 2 secondary least-squares mean.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide), [retatrutide vs semaglutide](/compare/retatrutide-vs-semaglutide), and [tirzepatide vs retatrutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-retatrutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ. Do not divide the amycretin 36-week estimated mean by the retatrutide week-48 secondary percent.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### Retatrutide phase 2 obesity (not TRIUMPH)

PMID 37366315, NCT04881760. Phase 2; 338 adults; 48 weeks. The **primary** end point was percentage change in body weight at **24 weeks**. Week 48 change is a **secondary** end point. The abstract reports least-squares means and does not name a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy estimand.

Week 24: −7.2% (1 mg), −12.9% (combined 4 mg), −17.3% (combined 8 mg), **−17.5% (12 mg)**, −1.6% placebo.

Week 48: −8.7% (1 mg), −17.1% (combined 4 mg), −22.8% (combined 8 mg), **−24.2% (12 mg)**, −2.1% placebo.

At week 48, a reduction of 5% / 10% / 15% or more occurred in 100% / 93% / 83% on 12 mg versus 27% / 9% / 2% on placebo. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events, related to assigned amount, and mostly mild to moderate. Heart-rate increases peaked at 24 weeks and declined thereafter [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

This page does not restore a 4 mg 48-week −17.5% row. −17.5% in the abstract is the 12 mg week-24 figure. Combined 4 mg at 48 weeks is −17.1%.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04881760 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 338; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract, not the results module.

### TRANSCEND-T2D-1 (type 2 diabetes phase 3, not obesity)

PMID 42250575, NCT06354660. Phase 3; 537 adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled by diet and exercise; 40 weeks. Primary end point: HbA1c change. **Treatment-regimen** estimand. HbA1c: −1.69% (4 mg), −1.86% (9 mg), −1.94% (12 mg), −0.81% placebo (P&lt;0.0001 vs placebo). Body weight (key secondary): −11.5%, −13.9%, −15.3% versus −2.6% placebo. Discontinuation for adverse events: 2–5% versus 0% placebo [transcend-t2d-1-pmid-42250575].

This is not a TRIUMPH obesity readout.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06354660 as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 537; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06354660` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 42250575 only.

### TRIUMPH (design only)

PMID 41090431 describes four Phase 3 studies (TRIUMPH-1 through TRIUMPH-4) in over 5,800 participants. The primary weight-management end point is percent change in body weight. The abstract publishes **no efficacy percent**. A PubMed search on 2026-09-02 for `"TRIUMPH-1" retatrutide weight` returned only this design paper. A PubMed search the same day for `retatrutide[Title] AND 28.7` returned 0 records. This page does not quote an unpublished TRIUMPH obesity percent [triumph-design-pmid-41090431].

PMID 42608321 is a phase 2 post-hoc lipoprotein and CRP analysis of NCT04881760 and NCT04867785. The apolipoprotein B change (up to −24.2% in that paper) is **not** a body-weight percent. It is not a TRIUMPH efficacy paper [retatrutide-biomarkers-pmid-42608321].

PMID 38858523 is a metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease substudy of the same phase 2 record (NCT04881760). It is not a TRIUMPH obesity readout. This page does not quote its liver-fat figures as a body-weight result [retatrutide-masld-pmid-38858523].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; the retatrutide obesity figure on this page is a phase 2 week-48 secondary least-squares mean.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **Retatrutide phase 2 primary is 24 weeks:** −17.5% is 12 mg at 24 weeks, not 4 mg at 48 weeks. The −24.2% vs −2.1% figure is a week-48 secondary.
5. **TRANSCEND-T2D-1:** treatment-regimen HbA1c and weight in type 2 diabetes, not TRIUMPH obesity.
6. **TRIUMPH:** design paper only. PubMed `retatrutide[Title] AND 28.7` on 2026-09-02 returned 0 records.
7. **H2H:** PubMed `"amycretin" AND "retatrutide"` on 2026-09-02 found reviews only.
