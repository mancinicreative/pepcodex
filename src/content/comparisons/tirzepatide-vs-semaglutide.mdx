## Overview

[Tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide) is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. This page quotes two head-to-head trials and two placebo-controlled obesity programmes. Placebo-controlled rows are not a substitute for the head-to-head.

The brand-name twin of this pair is [Wegovy vs Zepbound](/compare/wegovy-vs-zepbound).

---

## Head-to-head: type 2 diabetes (SURPASS-2)

PMID 34170647, NCT03987919. Open-label, 40 weeks, phase 3. n=1879. Tirzepatide 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg versus semaglutide **1 mg** (not the 2.4 mg obesity dose). Primary end point: change in glycated hemoglobin from baseline to 40 weeks [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

Estimated mean HbA1c change:

| Arm | HbA1c change | Difference vs semaglutide 1 mg |
|-----|--------------|--------------------------------|
| Tirzepatide 5 mg | −2.01 percentage points | −0.15 (95% CI −0.28 to −0.03; P=0.02) |
| Tirzepatide 10 mg | −2.24 percentage points | −0.39 (95% CI −0.51 to −0.26; P&lt;0.001) |
| Tirzepatide 15 mg | −2.30 percentage points | −0.45 (95% CI −0.57 to −0.32; P&lt;0.001) |
| Semaglutide 1 mg | −1.86 percentage points | — |

The abstract reports that tirzepatide at all doses was noninferior and superior to semaglutide on that HbA1c end point. Body-weight least-squares mean estimated treatment differences versus semaglutide were −1.9 kg, −3.6 kg, and −5.5 kg (P&lt;0.001 for all). The abstract does **not** publish absolute mean kilogram change per arm, an HbA1c-below-7% rate, or a ≥10% weight-loss rate; those rows were removed rather than guessed [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

Gastrointestinal events (primarily mild to moderate): nausea 17 to 22% (tirzepatide) vs 18% (semaglutide); diarrhea 13 to 16% vs 12%; vomiting 6 to 10% vs 8%. Hypoglycemia (blood glucose &lt;54 mg per deciliter): 0.6% / 0.2% / 1.7% on tirzepatide 5 / 10 / 15 mg vs 0.4% on semaglutide. Serious adverse events: 5 to 7% vs 3% [surpass-2-pmid-34170647].

## Head-to-head: obesity (SURMOUNT-5)

PMID 40353578, NCT05822830. Phase 3b, open-label; n=751 adults with obesity and without type 2 diabetes; 72 weeks. Maximum tolerated dose of tirzepatide (10 mg or 15 mg) versus maximum tolerated dose of semaglutide (1.7 mg or 2.4 mg). Primary end point: percent change in weight from baseline to week 72 [surmount-5-pmid-40353578].

| Arm | Weight change at 72 weeks | 95% CI |
|-----|---------------------------|--------|
| Tirzepatide (MTD) | −20.2% | −21.4 to −19.1 |
| Semaglutide (MTD) | −13.7% | −14.9 to −12.6 |

Least-squares mean waist circumference: −18.4 cm (95% CI −19.6 to −17.2) vs −13.0 cm (95% CI −14.3 to −11.7) (P&lt;0.001). The abstract states participants on tirzepatide were more likely to reach ≥10%, ≥15%, ≥20%, and ≥25% reductions; it does **not** publish those category percents. The abstract does not label treatment-regimen versus efficacy. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events in both groups [surmount-5-pmid-40353578].

A longer write-up of the same trial is [SURMOUNT-5: tirzepatide vs semaglutide](/blog/surmount-5-tirzepatide-vs-semaglutide).

## Placebo-controlled obesity programmes (not a head-to-head)

These two rows describe each drug against placebo. They are **not** a substitute for SURMOUNT-5.

### STEP 1 (semaglutide 2.4 mg)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961 adults with overweight or obesity and without diabetes; 68 weeks. Primary estimand: effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions. Mean weight change −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% / ≥10% / ≥15%: 86.4% / 69.1% / 50.5% vs 31.5% / 12.0% / 4.9%. Gastrointestinal discontinuation 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2539; 72 weeks; no diabetes. Treatment-regimen estimand. Mean percent change: 5 mg −15.0%, 10 mg −19.5%, 15 mg **−20.9%** vs **−3.1%** placebo (P&lt;0.001 for all). ≥20% weight reduction at 15 mg: **57%** (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo — not a rounded headline above that interval. Adverse-event discontinuation: 4.3% / 7.1% / 6.2% vs 2.6% [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Cardiovascular outcomes

These are separate programmes, not a tirzepatide-versus-semaglutide cardiovascular trial.

### SELECT (semaglutide vs placebo)

PMID 37952131, NCT03574597. n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease and overweight or obesity without diabetes. Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569/8803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8801 (**8.0%**); **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Adverse-event discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2%. This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent [select-pmid-37952131].

### SURPASS-CVOT (tirzepatide vs dulaglutide)

PMID 41406444, NCT04255433. Type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. Modified intention-to-treat: 6586 tirzepatide vs 6579 dulaglutide 1.5 mg. Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, myocardial infarction, or stroke): 801 (12.2%) vs 862 (13.1%); **HR 0.92** (95.3% CI 0.83–1.01); P=0.003 for noninferiority; P=0.09 for superiority. Noninferior, not superior, to dulaglutide. This is not a placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcomes trial and not a semaglutide comparison [surpass-cvot-pmid-41406444].

## What these trials do not settle

- SURMOUNT-5 is open-label and uses a maximum-tolerated-dose design (tirzepatide 10 or 15 mg; semaglutide 1.7 or 2.4 mg), not a single locked dose.
- SURPASS-2 used semaglutide **1 mg** in type 2 diabetes. It is not an obesity head-to-head against 2.4 mg.
- STEP 1 and SURMOUNT-1 remain useful as placebo-controlled magnitudes. They are not a head-to-head.
- SELECT and SURPASS-CVOT used different comparators (placebo vs dulaglutide) and different populations.
