## Overview

Mazdutide is a dual glucagon/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Innovent; also studied as LY3305677). Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). GLORY-1, GLORY-2, the US phase 2 trial, and SURMOUNT-1 are separate programmes. They are not one efficacy ratio.

## Key Facts

| Aspect | Mazdutide | Tirzepatide |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Developer** | Innovent (LY3305677 / IBI362) | Eli Lilly |
| **Class** | Glucagon/GLP-1 dual agonist | GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist |
| **FDA status on this page** | Not FDA-approved | FDA-approved (Mounjaro, Zepbound) |
| **Phase 3 obesity trials quoted here** | GLORY-1 (48 wk) [glory-1-pmid-40421736]; GLORY-2 (60 wk) [glory-2-pmid-42251595] | SURMOUNT-1 (72 wk) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024] |

## Clinical Trial Data

### GLORY-1 (China, treatment-policy)

PMID 40421736, NCT05607680. Phase 3; n=610. Primary end points at week 32 used a treatment-policy estimand. Week 32 mean body-weight change: −10.09% (4 mg), −12.55% (6 mg), +0.45% placebo. At least 5% reduction: 73.9%, 82.0%, 10.5% (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). Week 48: −11.00% (4 mg), −14.01% (6 mg), +0.30% placebo; at least 15% reduction: 35.7%, 49.5%, 2.0% (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). Adverse events leading to discontinuation: 1.5%, 0.5%, 1.0% (4 mg / 6 mg / placebo). Gastrointestinal events were the most frequent adverse events [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

### GLORY-2 (China, 9 mg, 60 weeks)

PMID 42251595, NCT06164873. Phase 3; 461 treated (307 mazdutide, 154 placebo); 60 weeks. Mean body-weight change: −16.65% (95% CI −18.19 to −15.12) vs −1.50% (95% CI −3.43 to 0.43); between-group difference −15.15% (95% CI −17.22 to −13.09; P&lt;0.001). At least 5% reduction: 84.3% vs 33.1%. Discontinuation for adverse events: 2.9% vs 0%. Vomiting 53.1% vs 1.3%; nausea 46.9% vs 3.2%; diarrhea 39.4% vs 6.5% [glory-2-pmid-42251595].

### US phase 2 (efficacy estimand, not treatment-policy)

PMID 42628555, NCT06124807. n=179; 48-week trial; primary analysis at 32 weeks on the efficacy (hypothetical) estimand. The abstract does not report a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy figure. Least-squares mean change at 32 weeks: −7.3% (3–6 mg), −15.6% (10 mg), −18.1% (16 mg) vs −0.9% placebo; estimated treatment differences −6.5% to −17.2% (P&lt;0.0001). Additional reductions at 48 weeks are noted without abstract numbers. Discontinuation for adverse events was 20% at 16 mg [mazdutide-us-p2-pmid-42628555].

Do not treat those 32-week efficacy-estimand percents as interchangeable with GLORY-1 treatment-policy or SURMOUNT-1 treatment-regimen numbers.

### SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2539; 72 weeks; treatment-regimen estimand. Week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg), −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001). At least 20% reduction at 15 mg: 57% (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 6.2% (15 mg) vs 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Side Effects

GLORY-2: vomiting 53.1% vs 1.3%; nausea 46.9% vs 3.2%; diarrhea 39.4% vs 6.5% [glory-2-pmid-42251595].

GLORY-1: gastrointestinal events most frequent; discontinuation 0.5–1.5% vs 1.0% placebo [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation 6.2% (15 mg) vs 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].
