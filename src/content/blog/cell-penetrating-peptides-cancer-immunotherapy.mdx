The cell membrane is a real barrier to drug delivery. Charged compounds and most biologics
cannot cross it, which puts intracellular targets out of reach. Cell-penetrating peptides
(CPPs) are the proposed answer. This page separates the mechanism, which is well described,
from the clinical record, which is thin.

## What We Know

### What a Cell-Penetrating Peptide Is

CPPs are short sequences, typically 5–30 residues, that cross cell membranes while carrying
cargo. Classes are usually grouped by physicochemistry:

| Class | Example | Characteristic |
|---|---|---|
| Cationic | Polyarginine | Rich in arginine and lysine; charge-mediated uptake |
| Amphipathic | Penetratin | Both hydrophobic and charged regions |
| Hydrophobic | Transportan | Lipid membrane interaction |

Entry is described through several routes — direct translocation, endocytosis, and transient
membrane disruption — and most CPPs appear to use more than one depending on structure, cargo
size, cell type and concentration. This plurality is precisely what makes
[mechanism](/glossary/mechanism-of-action) studies difficult and rational design uncertain.

### The One That Reached the Clinic

The clearest verified case is [sulanemadlin](/peptides/sulanemadlin) (ALRN-6924), described
in its discovery paper as the first cell-permeating, stabilised α-helical peptide to enter
clinical development [sulanemadlin-discovery]. It is a stapled peptide that inhibits the
MDMX and MDM2 interaction with p53 — an intracellular protein-protein interaction, which is
exactly the target class CPP chemistry exists to reach.

It has completed a phase 1 trial in patients with solid tumors and lymphomas
[alrn6924-phase1]. That is a genuine milestone for the concept: a peptide designed to get
inside cells, given to patients, taken through dose escalation, published.

It is also one compound. A single phase 1 programme does not establish a delivery platform.

### Peptide Cancer Vaccines — a Related but Distinct Track

Peptide-based cancer vaccines are often discussed alongside CPPs because both involve
delivering peptide material to immune cells. The evidence here is stronger, and it does not
depend on cell-penetrating chemistry.

A personalised peptide-based neoantigen vaccine, [EVX-01](/peptides/evx-01), has a published
dose-escalation study in patients with metastatic melanoma [evx01-dose-escalation]. The
broader approach was established by an earlier study demonstrating that a personal neoantigen
vaccine could be immunogenic in melanoma patients [neoantigen-vaccine-melanoma]. Related
individualised neoantigen work is covered in the
[cancer peptide vaccine briefing](/blog/cancer-peptide-vaccine-breakthrough) and in the
[mRNA-4157 dossier](/peptides/mrna-4157).

The distinction matters. These vaccines work because antigen-presenting cells take up and
present peptide antigen — an existing biological pathway. They are not evidence that CPP
conjugation improves anything.

## What We Don't Know

**Whether CPP delivery beats the alternatives.** The field lacks systematic head-to-head
comparisons of CPP delivery against lipid nanoparticles, viral vectors, exosomes or
electroporation for any specific immunotherapy cargo. Lipid nanoparticles in particular have
a large and successful clinical record that CPPs do not.

**Endosomal escape.** Many CPPs enter by endocytosis and remain trapped in endosomes, never
reaching their target. This is the field's central unsolved technical problem.

**Cell-type selectivity.** Most CPPs have none inherently. A delivery vehicle that enters
every cell is a poor fit for a cytotoxic payload.

**Immunogenicity on repeat administration.** Whether repeated dosing provokes a response
against the carrier itself is not characterised.

**Manufacturing.** Conjugation chemistry, purification, stability and quality control have to
be re-solved for each cargo.

## How Strong Is the Evidence?

**Evidence level: low.**

1. **Preclinical predominance.** Most CPP evidence is cell culture and animal work.
2. **One clinical anchor.** Sulanemadlin's phase 1 is the verified human data point for a
   cell-permeating peptide in oncology on this site.
3. **No approvals.** No CPP-based cancer immunotherapy has regulatory approval as of
   2026-08-17.
4. **Mechanistic gaps.** Entry route, endosomal escape and selectivity remain open.

The honest framing is that CPPs are a delivery *hypothesis* with one clinical proof point and
a large preclinical literature, sitting next to a peptide-vaccine field that has real trial
data and does not rely on them. Many delivery technologies have shown preclinical promise and
failed in development. Whether this one converts will be settled by
[clinical trials](/glossary/clinical-trial) that have mostly not run yet. For the chemistry
that made sulanemadlin possible, see
[peptide stapling and oral delivery](/blog/peptide-stapling-oral-delivery).
