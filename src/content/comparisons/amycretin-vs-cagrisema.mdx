## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [CagriSema](/peptides/cagrisema) is Novo Nordisk's coadministered cagrilintide plus semaglutide. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, and REDEFINE 1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [CagriSema vs semaglutide](/compare/cagrisema-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### REDEFINE 1 (CagriSema, obesity)

PMID 40544433, NCT05567796. Phase 3a; 68 weeks; multicenter; double-blind; placebo-controlled and active-controlled. Adults without diabetes with BMI of 30 or higher, or BMI of 27 or higher with at least one obesity-related complication. Randomised 21:3:3:7 to cagrilintide-semaglutide 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg (n=2,108), semaglutide 2.4 mg alone (n=302), cagrilintide 2.4 mg alone (n=302), or placebo (n=705); 3,417 total. Coprimary end points: relative body-weight change and a reduction of 5% or more from baseline to week 68 for cagrilintide-semaglutide compared with placebo. Effect estimates used the **treatment-policy** estimand (consistent with the intention-to-treat principle).

Estimated mean percent change at week 68: −20.4% with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus −3.0% with placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). Participants receiving cagrilintide-semaglutide were more likely than those receiving placebo to reach weight-loss targets of 5% or more, 20% or more, 25% or more, and 30% or more (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons). The abstract does not publish those responder percents, and it does not publish a weight-change percent for the semaglutide-alone or cagrilintide-alone arms. This page does not invent those figures [redefine-1-pmid-40544433]. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo, mainly transient and mild-to-moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05567796 (acronym REDEFINE 1) as ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING Phase 3 (enrollment 3400 estimated; primary completion 2024-10-30 actual; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT05567796` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40544433 (weight paper quoted here) and PMID 41328546 (REDEFINE 1 blood-pressure secondary analysis). This page does not quote blood-pressure figures from 41328546.

Do not divide REDEFINE 1’s 68-week treatment-policy percent by the amycretin 36-week estimated mean.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; REDEFINE 1 is a 68-week Phase 3a treatment-policy result.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **REDEFINE 1:** treatment-policy −20.4% vs −3.0% at 68 weeks. Active-control arm percents are not in the abstract.
5. **openFDA:** no amycretin, cagrilintide, or CagriSema application as of 2026-09-02.
