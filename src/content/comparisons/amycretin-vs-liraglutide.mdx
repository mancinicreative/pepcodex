## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [Liraglutide](/peptides/liraglutide) is Novo Nordisk's GLP-1 receptor agonist, listed on openFDA as Victoza and Saxenda. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, SCALE, and LEADER are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [liraglutide vs semaglutide](/compare/liraglutide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ. Do not divide the amycretin 36-week estimated mean by SCALE kilograms.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes (NEJM)

PMID 26132939, NCT01272219. 56-week, double-blind trial in 3731 participants without type 2 diabetes (BMI at least 30, or at least 27 with treated or untreated dyslipidemia or hypertension). Randomisation 2:1 to once-daily subcutaneous liraglutide 3.0 mg (2487) or placebo (1244); both groups received lifestyle-modification counseling. Coprimary endpoints were change in body weight and the proportions losing at least 5% and more than 10% of initial body weight.

At week 56, mean change was −8.4±7.3 kg versus −2.8±6.5 kg (difference −5.6 kg; 95% CI −6.0 to −5.1; P&lt;0.001, last-observation-carried-forward imputation). At least 5% loss: 63.2% versus 27.1%. More than 10% loss: 33.1% versus 10.6%. The abstract reports kilograms, not a mean percent, and does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [scale-pmid-26132939]. Most frequently reported adverse events were mild or moderate nausea and diarrhea. Serious events 6.2% versus 5.0%.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT01272219 as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 3731; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract. PubMed `NCT01272219` on 2026-09-02 returned 7 records; this page quotes PMID 26132939.

### LEADER (NEJM)

PMID 27295427, NCT01179048. 9340 participants with type 2 diabetes and high cardiovascular risk; median follow-up 3.8 years. Primary composite: first death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke.

Primary outcome: 608/4668 (13.0%) versus 694/4672 (14.9%); HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.97); P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority; P=0.01 for superiority. Cardiovascular death: 219 (4.7%) versus 278 (6.0%); HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.66–0.93). This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent [leader-pmid-27295427].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT01179048 as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 9341; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; SCALE reports kilograms with LOCF, not a mean percent.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **SCALE:** −8.4 kg vs −2.8 kg at 56 weeks. Do not convert those kilograms into a percent.
5. **LEADER:** quote the hazard ratio 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.97), not a rounded relative-risk headline.
6. **H2H:** PubMed `"amycretin" AND "liraglutide"` on 2026-09-02 found reviews only.
