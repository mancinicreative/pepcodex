## Overview

Amycretin is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. The oral first-in-human trial, the subcutaneous phase 1b/2a trial, STEP 1, and SELECT are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

CagriSema is a two-molecule GLP-1/amylin combination. This page does not quote CagriSema trial percents.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide), and [amycretin vs tirzepatide](/compare/amycretin-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

A per-alias PubMed search for amycretin on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later type 2 diabetes papers that were not fetched this increment.

### STEP 1 (semaglutide, obesity)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1,961 adults with overweight or obesity and no diabetes; 68 weeks; once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (treatment-regimen).

Mean body-weight change at week 68: −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). At least 5% reduction: 86.4% vs 31.5%. At least 10% reduction: 69.1% vs 12.0%. At least 15% reduction: 50.5% vs 4.9%. Kilogram change: −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events; discontinuations due to gastrointestinal events: 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### SELECT (semaglutide, cardiovascular outcomes)

PMID 37952131, NCT03574597. n=17,604 patients aged 45 years or older with preexisting cardiovascular disease and BMI of 27 or greater, without diabetes. Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo. Mean follow-up 39.8 months.

Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569 of 8,803 (6.5%) vs 701 of 8,801 (8.0%); hazard ratio 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation: 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131]. This page does not headline a rounded “20% reduction.”

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; gastrointestinal discontinuations 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

SELECT: adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means; STEP 1 is a 68-week Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **Treatment-regimen leads for semaglutide obesity:** STEP 1 2.4 mg −14.9% vs −2.4%.
5. **SELECT:** HR 0.80 (6.5% vs 8.0%); do not headline 20%.
6. **CagriSema:** named only as a two-molecule design contrast; no CagriSema trial percent is quoted here.
