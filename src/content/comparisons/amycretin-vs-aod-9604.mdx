## Overview

Amycretin is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. AOD-9604 (also written AOD9604) is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the C-terminal fragment of human growth hormone from amino acids 177-191 with an additional N-terminal tyrosine [aod-9604-identity-pmid-25208511]. The amycretin oral first-in-human trial, the amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a trial, and the AOD-9604 rodent papers fetched here are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [AOD-9604](/peptides/aod-9604), and [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Species, durations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

A per-alias PubMed search for `"amycretin"` on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later type 2 diabetes papers that were not fetched this increment.

### AOD-9604: no human obesity RCT percent in this increment

Per-alias PubMed searches on 2026-09-02 (`"AOD-9604"` count 16; `"AOD9604"` count 22; `"AOD 9604"` count 16) and ClinicalTrials.gov `query.term` searches for AOD-9604, AOD9604, and AOD 9604 (0 studies each) did not yield a published human obesity RCT abstract with a body-weight percent. This is a window-dated finding for that scan, not an eternal claim that no human study was ever run.

PMID 15134286 (2004 drug profile) states that Metabolic was developing AOD-9604 for potential obesity treatment and that by February 2002, phase IIa trials were underway. The abstract does not publish enrollment, duration, estimand, or a percent [aod-9604-profile-pmid-15134286].

PMID 16625817 (2006 review) lists a human growth hormone fragment (AOD-9604) among obesity drugs then in clinical development and states that, of the compounds in that review set, only rimonabant had completed phase III. That sentence is about the 2006 set. This increment did not fetch an AOD-9604 obesity Phase 3 publication [aod-9604-review-pmid-16625817].

Two incidental NCT identifiers appeared in later review abstracts that also named AOD-9604 (NCT00267527, CJC 1295 in HIV visceral obesity; NCT03150511, tesamorelin after nerve injury). Those trials are not AOD-9604 studies and are not quoted as AOD-9604 results.

### AOD-9604 rodent papers (not human RCT)

PMID 11146367. Obese Zucker rats. Chronic oral AOD9604 for 19 days reduced body-weight gain versus control (15.8 ± 0.6 g vs 35.6 ± 0.8 g). Adipose tissue lipolytic activity increased. The abstract reports no adverse effect on insulin sensitivity versus intact hGH in that model. That is a rodent weight-gain difference, not a human obesity percent [aod-9604-rat-pmid-11146367].

PMID 11673763. Obese and lean C57BL/6J mice, 14-day infusion. Both hGH and AOD9604 reduced body-weight gain in obese mice, with increased in vivo fat oxidation and plasma glycerol. The abstract states AOD9604 did not induce hyperglycaemia or reduce insulin secretion in that model, did not compete for the hGH receptor, and did not induce cell proliferation unlike hGH [aod-9604-mice-pmid-11673763].

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

Fetched AOD-9604 abstracts in this increment do not publish a human adverse-event table. The rodent papers comment on insulin sensitivity and glycaemia in those models only [aod-9604-rat-pmid-11146367] [aod-9604-mice-pmid-11673763].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means. Fetched AOD-9604 abstracts do not supply a human obesity RCT percent.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **AOD-9604 human note:** a 2004 profile said phase IIa was underway by February 2002 and did not publish a percent. CT.gov alias searches on 2026-09-02 returned 0 studies.
5. **Rodent papers are not Phase 3:** reduced weight gain in obese rats and mice is not a human treatment-regimen result. This page does not invent an AOD-9604 obesity Phase 3.
