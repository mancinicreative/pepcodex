Common-event frequencies, boxed-warning text, and contraindication lists live on [semaglutide safety](/safety/semaglutide-safety). The compound record is [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide). This post does not recap that table.

## What this page is for

Semaglutide is one molecule on three labelled products (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus). They share a class boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors from rodent data, and they do not share identical indicated uses, delivery, or monitoring language. SUSTAIN, PIONEER, and STEP are the trial programmes those labels draw on [pmid-36700417] [pmid-28110911] [pmid-36216945].

How to read the safety page:

- Read frequencies there, not from a blog recap
- Treat the boxed warning as a labelled contraindication in people at risk for MTC or MEN 2 — not as a quantified human cancer rate
- Gastrointestinal effects are the common class finding; pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, retinopathy, and volume-related kidney injury are the labelled serious risks, not a ranked shopping list

For the head-to-head weight result against tirzepatide, see [semaglutide vs tirzepatide](/blog/semaglutide-vs-tirzepatide-2026). That post is a comparison, not a second AE table.
