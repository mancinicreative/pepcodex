**Correction, September 29, 2026:** The original version combined results from different vaccine platforms and described a nonexistent randomized peptide-vaccine result. The trial with 157 randomized participants and 18-month recurrence-free survival estimates of 79% and 62% tested **V940 (mRNA-4157), an mRNA-based individualized therapy**, not EVX-01 or a synthetic-peptide vaccine. Its findings cannot establish EVX-01 efficacy.

Personalized neoantigen therapies use a tumor's mutations to select patient-specific immune targets. EVX-01 packages selected **peptides** with an adjuvant; V940 (mRNA-4157) uses **mRNA** to encode selected neoantigens. Both have been studied alongside pembrolizumab, but the products and trials answer different questions.

## What the EVX-01 studies show

The peer-reviewed EVX-01 dose-escalation study enrolled **12 people with metastatic melanoma receiving anti-PD-1 therapy**. Its primary endpoint was safety and tolerability. Investigators detected EVX-01-specific CD4+ and/or CD8+ T-cell responses in all 12 participants and observed objective tumor responses in **8 of 12** during the combination treatment [Mørk et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782542/). This was a small, open-label study without an anti-PD-1-only comparison group. The observed tumor responses cannot show how much, if any, benefit EVX-01 added.

A separate, single-arm Phase 2 study in checkpoint-inhibitor-naive unresectable or metastatic melanoma enrolled **17 participants**, according to its [official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05309421). A [2025 company release](https://evaxion.ai/investors/press-releases/?id=10646&press_release_pdf=1) and [AACR 2026 sponsor poster](https://evaxion.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/EVX01_AACR2026_Poster_FINAL_20260408_forupload.pdf) reported objective responses in **12 of 16 response-evaluable participants** after pembrolizumab and EVX-01. The trial began with a pembrolizumab run-in, had no randomized pembrolizumab-only arm, and the company said its registered response-conversion primary endpoint was **not formally analyzed** after lower enrollment. These sponsor-reported results are not a controlled estimate of EVX-01's added clinical benefit; the registry had no posted results when checked on September 29, 2026.

## What the V940 study shows

The separate [KEYNOTE-942 randomized Phase 2b study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38246194/) assigned **157 people with completely resected high-risk melanoma** to V940 plus pembrolizumab (**107**) or pembrolizumab alone (**50**). At the reported follow-up, 18-month recurrence-free survival estimates were **79% versus 62%**; the recurrence-or-death hazard-ratio point estimate was **0.561**, with a **95% confidence interval of 0.309–1.017** and two-sided **p=0.053**. This is an mRNA-product result in a post-surgery population. It is neither an EVX-01 result nor evidence that the peptide-vaccine approach as a class produces that effect.

## What remains open

The EVX-01 clinical studies show that vaccine-specific immune responses can be measured in treated participants. Whether adding EVX-01 improves survival, recurrence, or tumor response over checkpoint treatment alone remains unresolved by these single-arm data. The randomized V940 trial provides a different, product-specific comparison in a different melanoma setting. Future controlled studies and independently reported outcomes are needed to assess each approach's clinical effect.
