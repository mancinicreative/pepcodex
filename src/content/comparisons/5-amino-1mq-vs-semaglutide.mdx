## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run and the semaglutide Phase 3 trials are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [5-Amino-1MQ vs tirzepatide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-tirzepatide).

The abstract names 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium as the NNMT inhibitor used with a low-fat diet and describes whole-body adiposity and weight loss in mice relative to diet switch alone. ### STEP 1 (semaglutide, obesity)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. Double-blind; 1,961 adults with BMI of 30 or greater (or ≥27 with at least one weight-related coexisting condition), without diabetes; 2:1 to 68 weeks of once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. Coprimary end points: percentage change in body weight and a reduction of at least 5%. The **primary estimand** assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions.

Mean body-weight change at week 68: **−14.9%** with semaglutide versus **−2.4%** with placebo (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). Weight reductions of 5% or more: 86.4% vs 31.5%; 10% or more: 69.1% vs 12.0%; 15% or more: 50.5% vs 4.9% (P&lt;0.001 for all three comparisons of odds). Kilogram change: −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal discontinuation: 4.5% vs 0.8%. Nausea and diarrhea were the most common adverse events with semaglutide; typically transient and mild-to-moderate [step-1-pmid-33567185].

This page does not collapse STEP 1 into a programme-level percent range. STEP 2, STEP 3, and STEP 4 abstracts were not fetched in this increment and are not quoted.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT03548935 (STEP 1) as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 1,961; hasResults true as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract.

### SELECT (semaglutide, cardiovascular outcomes)

PMID 37952131, NCT03574597. Multicenter, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, event-driven superiority trial. Patients 45 years or older with preexisting cardiovascular disease and BMI of 27 or greater, without diabetes. Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo. n=17,604 (8,803 / 8,801). Mean follow-up 39.8 months.

Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569/8,803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8,801 (**8.0%**); **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation: 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131]. This page does not convert that hazard ratio into a percent-reduction headline and does not invent a SELECT weight-change percent (none is in this abstract).

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT03574597 (SELECT) as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 17,604; hasResults true as of 2026-09-02).
