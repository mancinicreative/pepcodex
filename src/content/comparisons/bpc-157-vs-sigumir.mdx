## Overview

BPC-157 and Sigumir are both studied in the peptide research space.

**BPC-157:** A synthetic peptide derived from human gastric juice protein.

**Sigumir:** A cytamin-class peptide supplement derived from cartilage tissue, part of the Russian bioregulator framework.

## Evidence Comparison

As of September 5, 2026, the [BPC-157 dossier](/peptides/bpc-157) cites 9 publications: 1 human intervention report and 8 preclinical publications. The [human report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40131143/) is an uncontrolled safety pilot in two previously exposed adults, not a randomized efficacy trial. Registrations are excluded from publication counts. This is a cited-source inventory, not an exhaustive literature count or evidence of comparative benefit.
