## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [CagriSema](/peptides/cagrisema) is coadministered cagrilintide plus semaglutide. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run and the REDEFINE Phase 3a trials are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [5-Amino-1MQ vs semaglutide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-semaglutide), [5-Amino-1MQ vs cagrilintide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-cagrilintide), and [CagriSema vs semaglutide](/compare/cagrisema-vs-semaglutide).

### CagriSema — REDEFINE 1 (without diabetes)

PMID 40544433, NCT05567796. Phase 3a, 68-week, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled and active-controlled trial. Adults without diabetes; BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with at least one obesity-related complication. n=3417 randomly assigned (2108 cagrilintide-semaglutide; 302 semaglutide 2.4 mg; 302 cagrilintide 2.4 mg; 705 placebo), plus lifestyle intervention. Effect estimates used the treatment-policy estimand (intention-to-treat).

Treatment-policy mean percent change at week 68: **−20.4%** with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus **−3.0%** placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). The abstract does not publish the cagrilintide-alone or semaglutide-alone percents. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05567796 as ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING Phase 3 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02; lead sponsor Novo Nordisk A/S). This page quotes the NEJM abstract, not a registry results module. A per-alias PubMed search for `"CagriSema"` on 2026-09-02 returned 70 records. This page quotes REDEFINE 1 and REDEFINE 2 and does not dump that list.

A PubMed search for NCT05567796 on 2026-09-02 also returned PMID 41328546 (REDEFINE 1 blood-pressure analysis). That paper is not quoted as a weight-change result.

### CagriSema — REDEFINE 2 (type 2 diabetes)

PMID 40544432, NCT05394519. Phase 3a, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 12 countries. Adults with BMI of 27 or more, glycated hemoglobin 7 to 10%, and type 2 diabetes. n=1206 randomly assigned (904 cagrilintide-semaglutide 2.4 mg each; 302 placebo) for 68 weeks plus lifestyle intervention. Treatment-policy estimand.

Treatment-policy mean change at week 68: **−13.7%** versus **−3.4%** placebo (estimated difference −10.4 percentage points; 95% CI −11.2 to −9.5; P&lt;0.001). Glycated hemoglobin of 6.5% or less: 73.5% versus 15.9%. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 72.5% versus 34.4% [redefine-2-pmid-40544432].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05394519 as COMPLETED Phase 3 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02; lead sponsor Novo Nordisk A/S). This page does not invent a trial-product percent for REDEFINE 2.
