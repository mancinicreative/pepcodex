## Overview

[AOD-9604](/peptides/aod-9604) (also written AOD9604) is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the C-terminal fragment of human growth hormone from amino acids 177-191 with an additional N-terminal tyrosine [aod-9604-identity-pmid-25208511]. [Cagrilintide](/peptides/cagrilintide) is a long-acting amylin analogue. The AOD-9604 rodent papers and early-2000s development notes fetched this run, the Phase 2 cagrilintide monotherapy trial, and REDEFINE 1 (CagriSema) are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs AOD-9604](/compare/amycretin-vs-aod-9604), [5-Amino-1MQ vs AOD-9604](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-aod-9604), [5-Amino-1MQ vs cagrilintide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-cagrilintide), and [cagrilintide vs tirzepatide](/compare/cagrilintide-vs-tirzepatide).

None of those three is a published AOD-9604 human obesity RCT with a body-weight percent.

PMID 15134286 title-matched as a 2004 development profile. It states Metabolic was developing AOD-9604 for the potential treatment of obesity and that by February 2002 phase IIa trials were underway. The abstract publishes **no enrollment and no percent** [aod-9604-profile-pmid-15134286]. That is not a human RCT result and is not an obesity Phase 3.

PMID 11146367 title-matched as a rat paper. Daily oral AOD9604 for 19 days in obese Zucker rats reduced body-weight gain to 15.8 ± 0.6 g versus 35.6 ± 0.8 g in controls. That is rodent weight-gain in grams, not a human RCT percent. The treatment-amount line from the abstract is not copied here [aod-9604-rat-pmid-11146367].

PMID 11673763 names AOD9604 in the abstract as a C-terminal fragment of hGH. In obese mice treated for 14 days, both hGH and AOD9604 reduced body-weight gain and increased fat oxidation. The abstract does **not** publish a body-weight percent. AOD9604 did not compete for the hGH receptor [aod-9604-mice-pmid-11673763].

PMID 16625817 is a 2006 review of then-in-development obesity drugs. It lists a human growth hormone fragment (AOD-9604) among that set. Of those compounds, only rimonabant had completed phase III. That sentence is not an AOD-9604 Phase 3 result [aod-9604-review-pmid-16625817].

PMID 25208511 title-matched as an identity and doping-detection paper. It describes AOD9604 as the C-terminal hGH fragment 177-191 plus an N-terminal tyrosine. It publishes no efficacy percent [aod-9604-identity-pmid-25208511].

### Cagrilintide monotherapy (Phase 2)

PMID 34798060, NCT03856047. Multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled and active-controlled Phase 2 trial. Adults without diabetes; BMI of at least 30 kg/m² or at least 27 kg/m² with hypertension or dyslipidaemia. n=706 randomly assigned (100–102 per cagrilintide arm; 99 liraglutide 3.0 mg; 101 placebo); 26-week treatment period. The paper reports both a trial-product estimand (assuming adherence) and a treatment-policy estimand (regardless of adherence).

Trial-product mean percent weight change at week 26: cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg 6.0%–10.8% versus placebo 3.0% (estimated treatment difference range 3.0–7.8 percentage points; P&lt;0.001). Cagrilintide 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0% (estimated treatment difference 1.8 percentage points; P=0.03). The abstract states that similar reductions were observed with the treatment-policy estimand; it does not publish a separate treatment-policy percent table. This page does not invent a 2.4 mg monotherapy row [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

Gastrointestinal adverse events: 41%–63% on cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg versus 32% placebo, primarily nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT03856047 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 706; hasResults true as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. PubMed on that NCT returned PMID 34798060 only. This page quotes the Lancet abstract, not the registry results module. A per-alias PubMed search for "cagrilintide" on 2026-09-02 returned 97 records. This page quotes the Phase 2 paper and REDEFINE 1 below and does not dump that list.

### CagriSema is not cagrilintide monotherapy

REDEFINE 1 (PMID 40544433, NCT05567796) randomised 3,417 adults without diabetes to cagrilintide-semaglutide, semaglutide 2.4 mg, cagrilintide 2.4 mg, or placebo for 68 weeks (21:3:3:7; 2108 / 302 / 302 / 705). Treatment-policy mean change: **−20.4%** with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus **−3.0%** placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). The abstract does not publish the cagrilintide-alone percent. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05567796 as ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING Phase 3 (enrollment 3400 estimated; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. This page does not quote an unpublished cagrilintide-alone Phase 3 percent.

A PubMed search for NCT05567796 on 2026-09-02 also returned PMID 41328546 (REDEFINE 1 blood-pressure analysis). That paper is not quoted as a weight-change result.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** AOD-9604 has no human obesity RCT percent on this page; cagrilintide Phase 2 is a 26-week trial-product result. PubMed H2H queries returned 0 on 2026-09-02.
2. **AOD-9604:** 2004 profile says phase IIa was underway by February 2002 with **no percent** (PMID 15134286). CT.gov queries returned 0 studies as of 2026-09-02. No invented AOD-9604 obesity Phase 3.
3. **Rodent only:** rat 19-day weight-gain 15.8 ± 0.6 vs 35.6 ± 0.8 g (PMID 11146367); mouse weight-gain reduction with **no percent** (PMID 11673763).
4. **Cagrilintide Phase 2:** trial-product **6.0%–10.8% vs 3.0%** at 26 weeks; 4.5 mg 10.8% vs liraglutide 9.0% (PMID 34798060).
5. **REDEFINE 1:** CagriSema treatment-policy **−20.4% vs −3.0%**; cagrilintide-alone arm named without a percent (PMID 40544433). openFDA: no AOD-9604 application; no cagrilintide application as of 2026-09-02.
