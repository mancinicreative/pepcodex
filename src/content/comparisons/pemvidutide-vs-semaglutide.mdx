## Overview

Pemvidutide is Altimmune's investigational dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist. Semaglutide is Novo Nordisk's GLP-1 receptor agonist. IMPACT, the MASLD liver-fat trials, STEP 1, and SELECT are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [pemvidutide](/peptides/pemvidutide), [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide), and [pemvidutide vs tirzepatide](/compare/pemvidutide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Indications, durations, and estimands differ.

### IMPACT (pemvidutide, MASH)

PMID 41237796, NCT05989711. Phase 2b; 212 randomly assigned after 1,557 screened; 24 weeks. Dual primary endpoints in the intention-to-treat population: MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis, or at least one-stage fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH.

MASH resolution without fibrosis worsening: 18 (20%) of 86 on placebo, 24 (58%) of 41 on 1.2 mg (difference 38 percentage points; 95% CI 21–56; P&lt;0.0001), and 45 (52%) of 85 on 1.8 mg (difference 32 percentage points; 95% CI 19–46; P&lt;0.0001).

Fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH: 24 (28%) of 86 on placebo, 13 (33%) of 41 on 1.2 mg (difference 5 percentage points; 95% CI −13 to 22; P=0.59), and 30 (36%) of 85 on 1.8 mg (difference 8 percentage points; 95% CI −6 to 22; P=0.27). The fibrosis co-primary was not met at 24 weeks.

Adverse events: 32 (78%) of 41 on 1.2 mg, 69 (81%) of 85 on 1.8 mg, 58 (67%) of 86 on placebo. Discontinuations due to adverse events: none of 41 (1.2 mg), one (1%) of 85 (1.8 mg), two (2%) of 86 (placebo) [impact-pmid-41237796].

### MASLD liver-fat trials (not IMPACT)

**12-week MASLD** (PMID 39002641, NCT05006885): 94 randomised and dosed. Paper-stated primary efficacy was relative reduction in liver fat content (LFC) by MRI-PDFF. Week 12 LFC: −46.6% (1.2 mg), −68.5% (1.8 mg), −57.1% (2.4 mg) vs −4.4% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo, all groups). Maximal weight change in the abstract was −4.3% at 1.8 mg (P&lt;0.001). ClinicalTrials.gov lists this record as Phase 1 with a TEAE primary; this page does not treat −4.3% as a confirmatory obesity result [masld-12wk-pmid-39002641].

**24-week MASLD extension** (PMID 41113119, NCT05292911): 64 participants continued their originally assigned dose. Paper-stated primary efficacy was relative LFC reduction at 24 weeks: −56.3%, −75.2%, and −76.4% at 1.2 / 1.8 / 2.4 mg vs −14.0% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo, all groups). At 1.8 mg, 84.6% achieved a 50% LFC reduction and 53.8% reached LFC ≤5%. Body weight was also reduced by 6.2% (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). ClinicalTrials.gov lists this record as Phase 1 with a TEAE primary [masld-24wk-pmid-41113119].

NCT05295875 is a completed Phase 2 obesity trial (actual enrollment 391; primary completion 2023-09-28; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). ### STEP 1 (semaglutide, obesity)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1,961 adults with overweight or obesity and no diabetes; 68 weeks; once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (treatment-regimen).

Mean body-weight change at week 68: −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). At least 5% reduction: 86.4% vs 31.5%. At least 10% reduction: 69.1% vs 12.0%. At least 15% reduction: 50.5% vs 4.9%. Kilogram change: −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events; discontinuations due to gastrointestinal events: 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### SELECT (semaglutide, cardiovascular outcomes)

PMID 37952131, NCT03574597. n=17,604 patients aged 45 years or older with preexisting cardiovascular disease and BMI of 27 or greater, without diabetes. Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo. Mean follow-up 39.8 months.

Primary composite (death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569 of 8,803 (6.5%) vs 701 of 8,801 (8.0%); hazard ratio 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation: 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131]. This page does not headline a rounded “20% reduction.”

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** IMPACT is a 24-week MASH biopsy trial; STEP 1 is a 68-week obesity trial.
2. **IMPACT ITT:** MASH resolution without fibrosis worsening was met; the fibrosis co-primary was not met at 24 weeks.
3. **Treatment-regimen leads for semaglutide obesity:** STEP 1 2.4 mg −14.9% vs −2.4%.
4. **SELECT:** HR 0.80 (6.5% vs 8.0%); do not headline 20%.
5. **MASLD LFC is not an obesity Phase 3 figure:** quote the MRI-PDFF percents from the 12-week and 24-week papers; do not annualize them or collapse them to ~70%.
6. **MOMENTUM:** completed registry record without a PubMed results paper as of 2026-09-02; unpublished percents are not used here.
