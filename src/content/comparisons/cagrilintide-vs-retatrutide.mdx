## Overview

Cagrilintide and Retatrutide are both studied in the peptide research space.

**Cagrilintide:** A long-acting amylin analog developed by Novo Nordisk for weight management.

**Retatrutide:** A triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist in Phase 3 development.

## Evidence Comparison

As of September 5, 2026, the [retatrutide dossier](/peptides/retatrutide) cites 11 publications: 8 human reports, 2 meta-analyses and 1 trial-design paper; no preclinical publication is included in that inventory. Human reports include original trials, substudies, post-hoc analyses and a case report. Registrations and sponsor announcements are excluded.
