## Overview

CagriSema and CT-388 are both studied in the peptide research space.

**CagriSema:** A fixed-dose combination of cagrilintide (amylin analog) and semaglutide (GLP-1 agonist) for chronic weight management.

**CT-388:** Roche's June 2026 update identifies CT-388 as enicepatide, an investigational dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist. [Roche, 1 June 2026](https://www.roche.com/media/releases/med-cor-2026-06-01).
