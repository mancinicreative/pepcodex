TB-500 commonly refers to **N-acetyl-LKKTETQ**, the seven-residue segment corresponding to thymosin beta-4 residues 17–23. FDA's 2026 substance review gives the free-base mass as 889.01 Da and warns that the name has also been used for different salts and active moieties. Full-length thymosin beta-4 is a distinct 43-residue molecule; results from its wound, cardiac, corneal and neurologic experiments cannot be assigned to TB-500. [FDA identity review](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download)

### What the fragment research shows

A 2012 analytical study identified N-acetyl-LKKTETQ in a veterinary preparation and detected it and its metabolites in equine samples. It was a detection study, not evidence of tissue repair in people. In a 2024 metabolism and fibroblast scratch-assay study, the **Ac-LKKTE metabolite**, rather than parent TB-500, showed statistically significant wound-healing activity against control. That result is limited to the assay and does not establish clinical efficacy or the full parent protein's mechanisms for TB-500. [PMID:23084823](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23084823/) · [PMID:38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/)

Full-length thymosin beta-4 has a larger preclinical literature. For example, Bock-Marquette et al. studied cardiac repair pathways in mice; those ILK/Akt results concern the parent molecule. RGN-259 is a full-length thymosin beta-4 ophthalmic development program, not a TB-500 trial. Its small published corneal phase 3 study did not reach the primary endpoint. These studies help explain scientific interest in the parent protein but remain indirect for Ac-LKKTETQ. [PMID:15565145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15565145/) · [PMID:36613994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36613994/)

### Evidence and safety boundary

No controlled human efficacy study of Ac-LKKTETQ was identified. FDA says it has identified no human exposure data for drug products containing the fragment and cites potential immunogenicity from aggregation and peptide-related impurities in compounded products. Its interaction and combination effects with other peptides are unknown. [FDA compounding safety review](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks)
