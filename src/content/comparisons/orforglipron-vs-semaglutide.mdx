## Overview

Orforglipron is a nonpeptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist; semaglutide is a peptide. ATTAIN-1, STEP 1, and OASIS 1 are separate trials. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

This comparison matters because oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonists could dramatically expand access to this drug class.

## Clinical Trial Data

These are separate programmes, not a head-to-head RCT.

**ATTAIN-1** (PMID 40960239, NCT05869903): n=3127 adults with obesity without diabetes; 72 weeks; treatment-regimen estimand. Mean weight change −7.5% (6 mg), −8.4% (12 mg), and −11.2% (36 mg) vs −2.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 for all vs placebo). At 36 mg, ≥10% 54.6% vs 12.9% placebo; ≥15% 36.0% vs 5.9%; ≥20% 18.4% vs 2.8%. AE discontinuation 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7% placebo. GI events most common, mostly mild to moderate [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

**Phase 2** (PMID 37351564, NCT05051579): n=272, 36 weeks. Week 36 mean change ranged from −9.4% to −14.7% across orforglipron dose cohorts vs −2.3% placebo. The abstract does not publish a 36 mg vs 45 mg split or an HbA1c table; this page does not invent one. GI events led to discontinuation in 10–17% across dose cohorts [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564].

**STEP 1** (PMID 33567185): −14.9% vs −2.4% at 68 weeks [step-1-pmid-33567185].

**OASIS 1** (PMID 37385278): oral semaglutide vs placebo, n=667 randomised, 68 weeks. −15.1% vs −2.4% (ETD −12.7 pp; 95% CI −14.2 to −11.3; P&lt;0.0001). GI adverse events 80% vs 46% [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].

This page does not quote a Rybelsus 14 mg −4.4% row: that figure was not in the abstracts fetched 2026-09-02.

## Administration Comparison

ATTAIN-1 used once-daily oral orforglipron. STEP 1 used once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide. OASIS 1 used once-daily oral semaglutide. This page does not copy a fasting or water-volume instruction from memory.

## Side Effect Profile

ATTAIN-1: GI events most common, mostly mild to moderate; AE discontinuation 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7% placebo. No nausea percent in the abstract [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

Phase 2: GI events mild to moderate, primarily during dose escalation; discontinuation 10–17% across dose cohorts [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564].

OASIS 1: GI adverse events 80% vs 46% placebo [oasis-1-pmid-37385278].
