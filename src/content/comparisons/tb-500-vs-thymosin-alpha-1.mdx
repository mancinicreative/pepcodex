## Overview

TB-500 here means Ac-LKKTETQ, the N-acetylated seven-residue fragment, distinct from full-length thymosin beta-4. Thymosin alpha-1 is an immune-modulating peptide with regulatory approval in some countries. Sharing the thymosin name does not make their evidence interchangeable.

### How They Work

**TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment):**

Parent thymosin beta-4 and short sequences containing LKKTETQ have different experimental evidence. Parent actin, angiogenesis, cardiac and hair findings cannot automatically be assigned to N-acetylated Ac-LKKTETQ. [PMID:20179146](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20179146/) The direct 2024 fragment assay found significant wound-healing activity only for a metabolite. [PMID:38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/)


**Thymosin Alpha-1:**
- Isolated from thymus gland
- Enhances T-cell function
- Activates dendritic cells
- Modulates innate and adaptive immunity

### TB-500 Evidence Limitations

The direct 2024 Ac-LKKTETQ assay found significant wound-healing activity only for the Ac-LKKTE metabolite, not Ac-LKKTETQ itself. Its experimental human serum and rat metabolic studies do not establish clinical benefit. [PMID:38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/)

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception:** TB-500 and thymosin alpha-1 are related and have similar effects.

**Reality:** They are distinct molecules. Shared naming does not establish shared clinical effects or interchangeable evidence.

## TB-500: direct evidence and parent context

This comparison uses TB-500 to mean **Ac-LKKTETQ, the N-acetylated seven-residue fragment**, not the 43-residue parent protein or the different Ac-SDKP fragment. Analytical studies identified Ac-LKKTETQ in tested TB-500 material; they do not verify all products using the name. [PMID:22962027](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22962027/) [PMID:20179146](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20179146/)

The TB-500 dossier cites 17 publications: 3 full-length thymosin beta-4 clinical reports, 10 preclinical reports spanning distinct molecular forms, 3 analytical reports and 1 review. The 3 clinical reports do not administer Ac-LKKTETQ. These are cited-publication counts, not independent trials, exhaustive literature totals or evidence of comparative benefit. The Ac-LKKTETQ clinical-benefit grade is **very low**. The [corrected dossier](/peptides/tb-500) identifies each molecular form and publication category.

In the 2024 Ac-LKKTETQ study, only the **Ac-LKKTE metabolite** showed significant wound-healing activity in the fibroblast assay; Ac-LKKTETQ itself did not. Laboratory human serum and treated rats were used for metabolic analysis, not a human treatment trial. Absence of cytotoxicity in that assay does not establish human safety. [PMID:38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/)

The cited parent-compound corneal trial reported four-week healing in 6/10 versus 1/8 participants but missed its primary comparison (p=0.0656); a separate 72-person parent dry-eye trial missed both primary endpoints. Neither establishes Ac-LKKTETQ efficacy. [PMID:36613994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36613994/) [PMID:26056426](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26056426/)

A July 2026 rat tendon study reported some positive findings for its TB-500-labelled substance and **no additional benefit from combining it with BPC-157**. Its exact chemical identity is unresolved from the fetched abstract, which also describes synthetic thymosin beta-4. It is not evidence of Ac-LKKTETQ clinical efficacy or a synergistic combination. [PMID:42542926](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42542926/)

The [May 15, 2026 FDA briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download) lists the free-base mass as **889.01 g/mol** and acetate as **949.1 g/mol**. It states neither evaluated form was an approved-drug component. That advisory briefing does not establish a final compounding decision.

The [NCT07487363 record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07487363) explicitly calls itself fictional/example material and is excluded as evidence of a real trial. Our September 5 review did not verify a conducted human Ac-LKKTETQ trial; this bounded result is not proof of universal absence. See the [TB-500 safety review](/safety/tb-500-safety) for clinical safety and pharmacokinetic limits.

## Summary

- **TB-500:** Very-low Ac-LKKTETQ clinical-benefit evidence; 17 cited publications include 3 parent clinical reports, not fragment treatment trials.
- **Thymosin alpha-1** is an immune modulator approved in many countries for hepatitis and immune conditions
