This post does not recap those tables.

## What this page is for

Neither peptide is approved by a medicines regulator. Human safety is not established. Most of the published work is preclinical; BPC-157 papers cluster in one research group; TB-500 claims are often extrapolated from the parent thymosin β4 literature. There is no Phase 3 programme to harvest an AE table from.

WADA prohibits both in sport [wada-prohibited-list]. FDA review of BPC-157 as a bulk substance for compounding is a regulatory position that the human evidence is insufficient — not a completed safety trial [fda-503a-nominations-2023].

Unregulated listings cannot be assumed to match a trial-grade substance. For the BPC-157 human-tally FAQ, see [Is BPC-157 safe?](/blog/is-bpc-157-safe).
