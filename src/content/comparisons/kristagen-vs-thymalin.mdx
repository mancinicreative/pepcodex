## Overview

Kristagen and Thymalin are both studied in the peptide research space.

**Kristagen:** A synthetic tripeptide (Glu-Asp-Pro, EDP; spelled Crystagen in English-language papers) developed by Russian scientist Vladimir Khavinson, claimed to regulate thymus function and support immune cell differentiation.

**Thymalin:** A thymic peptide complex developed in Russia and used clinically in the former Soviet Union for immunomodulation.
