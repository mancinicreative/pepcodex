## What the name means

Analytical studies identified **Ac-LKKTETQ**, an N-acetylated seven-residue peptide, in tested material called TB-500. One study developed detection in equine samples after administration; it was not a healing trial. Product identity in these samples does not authenticate every item using the name. [PMID:22962027](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22962027/) [PMID:23084823](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23084823/)

Full-length thymosin beta-4 contains 43 residues. **Ac-SDKP is a different, N-terminal fragment**; it is not Ac-LKKTETQ. A review distinguishes these regions and their experimental activities. [PMID:20179146](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20179146/)

The FDA's May 15, 2026 briefing lists **889.01 g/mol for the free base** and **949.1 g/mol for its acetate**, which are distinct chemical forms. [FDA briefing, pages 7–9](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

## Direct fragment evidence

The 2024 Ac-LKKTETQ study examined metabolism in laboratory human serum, enzyme systems and treated rats, plus fibroblast assays. **Only Ac-LKKTE, a metabolite, showed significant wound-healing activity compared with control.** Ac-LKKTETQ itself did not show that significant signal. Absence of cytotoxicity in the tested assay does not establish human safety. Human serum used in a laboratory is not administration to people. [PMID:38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/)

An analytical study also found that tested TB500/TB1000 products were not consistently described by prior labels. Its sample-specific findings cannot establish the contents of all products. [PMID:36482504](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36482504/)

## Related experiments use different substances

These findings describe their specific test systems; they do not demonstrate the efficacy of Ac-LKKTETQ in patients:

- A mouse wound study tested full-length thymosin beta-4 and a synthetic **LKKTETQ** peptide. The abstract does not establish N-terminal acetylation of that peptide. [PMID:12581423](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12581423/)
- A study of cultured human hepatic stellate cells tested the **LKKTETQ 17–23 fragment** against another fragment. Its cellular findings are not clinical evidence for either fragment. [PMID:30063851](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30063851/)
- A December 2025 study investigated an enzyme-responsive hydrogel made with a **chemically modified LKKTETQ-containing precursor**. Its cell and experimental corneal findings concern that formulation, not free Ac-LKKTETQ in patients. [PMID:41359360](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41359360/)
- A report in the February 2026 issue studied TB500 and Ac-SDKP as distinct peptides in cell models and 5×FAD mice. Despite reported behavioral and cellular signals, hippocampal amyloid burden was unchanged. Exact TB500 chemistry needs full-method verification before assigning the result to Ac-LKKTETQ. [PMID:41443105](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41443105/)
- A July 2026 experiment in 32 rats reported improved tendon measures in its TB-500-labelled group and **no additional benefit from combining it with BPC-157**. The abstract also calls the test substance synthetic thymosin beta-4; its molecular identity remains unresolved here. It does not establish fragment efficacy, clinical benefit, or a synergistic interaction. [PMID:42542926](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42542926/)

## Parent-compound evidence

The following findings concern full-length thymosin beta-4 and provide context only:

- A rat skin-wound experiment reported increased re-epithelialization. It is not a skeletal-muscle recovery trial. [PMID:10469335](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10469335/)
- Rat/mouse hair research and cultured follicular-cell findings concern the parent protein. [PMID:14657002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14657002/)
- Cardiac-cell and mouse coronary-ligation experiments reported cell-survival and cardiac-function findings. [PMID:15565145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15565145/)
- A bleomycin mouse lung-injury study reported reduced damage and called for further antifibrotic investigation. [PMID:23320620](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23320620/)
- A 73-person venous-ulcer study reported exploratory efficacy. Its abstract does not substantiate the former claim of healing one month faster than placebo. [PMID:20536470](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20536470/)
- A 72-person dry-eye trial **missed both primary endpoints**, although selected secondary endpoints differed. [PMID:26056426](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26056426/)
- An 18-person neurotrophic keratopathy trial reported four-week healing in **6/10 versus 1/8** participants; the primary comparison was **not statistically significant (p=0.0656)**. Later secondary findings do not change that primary result. [PMID:36613994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36613994/)

## Clinical-trial and safety limits

The three clinical publications cited above do not administer Ac-LKKTETQ. Our September 5, 2026 review combined a June 7–September 5 literature window with unrestricted identity queries and selected cited papers. It did not verify a conducted human Ac-LKKTETQ trial. This scoped finding is not proof that no such research exists anywhere.

One registry result, **NCT07487363**, explicitly describes itself as a fictional study/example record. It is excluded as evidence of a real recruiting trial. [ClinicalTrials.gov record, retrieved September 5, 2026](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07487363).

The FDA briefing reported neither evaluated form as an approved-drug component. It is an advisory evaluation, not a final compounding decision. [FDA briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

The [safety review](/safety/tb-500-safety) explains the limits of fragment assays and parent-compound safety observations. This dossier is factual research information, not a treatment recommendation.
