## Overview

CagriSema is Novo Nordisk's coadministered cagrilintide plus semaglutide. Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. REDEFINE 1 and STEP 1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [cagrisema](/peptides/cagrisema), [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide), and [cagrisema vs tirzepatide](/compare/cagrisema-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not a published head-to-head percent. Indications match (overweight or obesity, no diabetes), but estimands and randomisation structures differ.

### REDEFINE 1 (CagriSema, obesity)

PMID 40544433, NCT05567796. Phase 3a; 68 weeks; multicenter; double-blind; placebo-controlled and active-controlled. Adults without diabetes with BMI of 30 or higher, or BMI of 27 or higher with at least one obesity-related complication. Randomised 21:3:3:7 to cagrilintide-semaglutide 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg (n=2,108), semaglutide 2.4 mg alone (n=302), cagrilintide 2.4 mg alone (n=302), or placebo (n=705); 3,417 total. Coprimary end points: relative body-weight change and a reduction of 5% or more from baseline to week 68 for cagrilintide-semaglutide compared with placebo. Effect estimates used the treatment-policy estimand (consistent with the intention-to-treat principle).

Estimated mean percent change at week 68: −20.4% with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus −3.0% with placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). Participants receiving cagrilintide-semaglutide were more likely than those receiving placebo to reach weight-loss targets of 5% or more, 20% or more, 25% or more, and 30% or more (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons). The abstract does not publish those responder percents, and it does not publish a weight-change percent for the semaglutide-alone arm. This page does not invent either figure [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

### STEP 1 (semaglutide, obesity)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1,961 adults with overweight or obesity and no diabetes; 68 weeks; once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg versus placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (treatment-regimen).

Mean body-weight change at week 68: −14.9% versus −2.4% placebo (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). At least 5% reduction: 86.4% versus 31.5%. At least 10% reduction: 69.1% versus 12.0%. At least 15% reduction: 50.5% versus 4.9%. Kilogram change: −15.3 kg versus −2.6 kg. Nausea and diarrhea were the most common adverse events; discontinuations due to gastrointestinal events: 4.5% versus 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### NCT06131437 (CagriSema versus tirzepatide — not this pair)

NCT06131437 is a completed Phase 3 randomised parallel trial of CagriSema 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg versus tirzepatide 15 mg (actual enrollment 809; week 0 to week 84; masking none). The registered primary is to confirm non-inferiority of CagriSema versus tirzepatide on relative change in body weight. CT.gov `hasResults` is false as of 2026-09-02. PubMed searches on 2026-09-02 for `NCT06131437`, `"REDEFINE 4" AND cagrilintide AND tirzepatide`, and `"REDEFINE-4" AND cagrilintide` each returned 0 records. This page does not quote a percent from that registry record [nct-06131437].

## Side Effect Profile

REDEFINE 1 reported gastrointestinal adverse events in 79.6% of the cagrilintide-semaglutide group versus 39.9% on placebo, mainly transient and mild-to-moderate (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, or abdominal pain named as a class, not as separate percents) [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

STEP 1 reported nausea and diarrhea as the most common adverse events with semaglutide, typically transient and mild-to-moderate. Discontinuations due to gastrointestinal events: 4.5% versus 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185]. The STEP 1 abstract does not publish a matched any-GI percent; this page does not invent one.

This page does not quote a CagriSema discontinuation percent: that figure is not in the REDEFINE 1 PubMed abstract fetched 2026-09-02.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not a published H2H percent:** REDEFINE 1 reported −20.4% vs −3.0% at 68 weeks (treatment-policy) [redefine-1-pmid-40544433]. STEP 1 reported −14.9% vs −2.4% at 68 weeks on 2.4 mg (treatment-regimen) [step-1-pmid-33567185]. Those are different estimands.
2. **REDEFINE 1 had a semaglutide-alone arm (n=302).** The fetched abstract does not name that arm's weight-change percent. This page does not invent it.
3. **Treatment-policy leads for CagriSema:** −20.4% vs −3.0%. This page does not quote an efficacy-estimand or unpublished head-to-head percent.
4. **Treatment-regimen leads for semaglutide obesity:** STEP 1 2.4 mg −14.9% vs −2.4%.
5. **NCT06131437 is versus tirzepatide, not semaglutide:** COMPLETED; `hasResults` false; PubMed 0 as of 2026-09-02; design only [nct-06131437].
6. **GI events in REDEFINE 1:** 79.6% vs 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433]. STEP 1 GI discontinuations 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185]. This page does not invent a matched nausea table.
