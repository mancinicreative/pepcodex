**Correction notice:** An earlier version of this article described a randomized,
double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 180 adults aged 65–85, with influenza
seroconversion of 72% vs 48% and seroprotection of 78% vs 54% after thymulin. Those
figures were not backed by any source on the page. The body cited
`[thymulin-vaccine-study]`, `[immunosenescence-review]`, and
`[vaccine-aging-research]` — none of which existed in `sources[]`. The three PubMed
records that *were* listed are about thymulin/zinc biochemistry and a mouse antibody
study, not a human vaccine RCT. The invented trial, endpoints, and development-path
claims have been removed.

Thymulin is a real peptide. The leap from its biochemistry to "restores vaccine
response in older adults" is not supported by the papers we can verify. The compound
record is in the [thymulin dossier](/peptides/thymulin). Related thymic peptides on
this site: [thymosin alpha-1](/peptides/thymosin-alpha-1) and
[thymalin](/peptides/thymalin).

## What the Verified Papers Cover

### A zinc-dependent thymic hormone

Bach and Dardenne's 1989 review describes thymulin as a zinc-dependent hormone
produced by the thymus [pmid-2657247]. The zinc requirement is not a marketing
detail: the biologically active form is a nonapeptide–zinc complex. A 1994 paper in
*Metal-Based Drugs* maps the same zinc–thymulin interaction [pmid-18476235]. Those
papers are the mechanistic core of the thymulin literature. They do not measure
vaccine seroconversion in elderly adults.

### Antibody production in stressed mice

Okamoto et al. (1993) reported that short-term thymulin administration restored
thymus-dependent antibody production in restraint-stressed mice [pmid-8407057]. That
is an animal immunopharmacology result. It is not a human influenza-vaccine trial.
It does not license percentages, sample sizes, or clinical influenza outcomes.

## What Is Not in This Record

As of 2026-09-01, this page's verified sources do **not** contain:

- A randomized trial of thymulin as a vaccine adjuvant in older adults
- Seroconversion or seroprotection rates in humans
- Clinical influenza-season outcomes attributable to thymulin
- A registered development path (Phase 2b / Phase 3) for that use

Absence of a citation on this page is not proof that no human thymulin study exists
anywhere. It is proof that the specific n=180 RCT previously asserted here was not
tied to a resolving identifier, and that the identifiers we do have do not describe
that study.

High-dose and adjuvanted influenza vaccines, and thymosin alpha-1 in some
jurisdictions, are separate products with their own evidence bases. They are not
evidence that thymulin has completed a vaccine-response RCT.

## How to Read Thymic-Peptide Claims

Thymic peptides are often bundled in popular writing as if one human result applied
to the whole class. That is the error this page made. [Thymulin](/peptides/thymulin),
[thymosin alpha-1](/peptides/thymosin-alpha-1), and [thymalin](/peptides/thymalin)
are different sequences with different regulatory and trial records. A zinc-binding
nonapeptide paper does not become a geriatric vaccine trial when the word "immune"
appears in both abstracts.

If a future human vaccine study of thymulin is published with a PMID or NCT that
drug-matches, that identifier can be added and this page updated. Until then, the
evidence level stays **low**: biochemistry plus animal antibody work, not a
completed human vaccine RCT.
