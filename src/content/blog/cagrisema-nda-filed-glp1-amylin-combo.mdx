Novo Nordisk has submitted a New Drug Application to the FDA for CagriSema, a once-weekly injectable combination of semaglutide and cagrilintide [novo-nda-filing]. If approved, it would be the first obesity treatment combining GLP-1 receptor agonism with amylin analogue activity.

Several different weight-loss percentages circulate for this drug. They come from different trials and different analytical assumptions, and the difference matters more than it looks.

## What the Numbers Actually Are

In REDEFINE 1, the estimated mean change in body weight from baseline to week 68 was **−20.4% with CagriSema versus −3.0% with placebo, on the treatment-policy estimand** — the published primary analysis, which counts every randomised participant regardless of adherence [redefine-1-results].

| Figure | Where it comes from | What it assumes |
|---|---|---|
| **20.4%** | REDEFINE 1, treatment-policy estimand | The published primary result (every randomised participant) |
| (not quoted) | REDEFINE 1 trial-product estimand | The PubMed abstract fetched 2026-09-02 does not report that percent |
| (not quoted) | NCT06131437 (REDEFINE 4) | Registry trial vs tirzepatide; CT.gov hasResults=false as of 2026-09-02 |

The 20.4% figure is the one in the REDEFINE 1 abstract [redefine-1-results]. When a headline quotes a CagriSema weight-loss number without naming the trial and the estimand, it is not reporting a result — it is reporting a choice.

The full compound record, including the trial register and scoring, is in the [CagriSema dossier](/peptides/cagrisema). The components have their own pages: [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) and [cagrilintide](/peptides/cagrilintide).

## Understanding the Dual Mechanism

CagriSema combines two distinct but complementary hormonal pathways.

**Semaglutide** acts as a GLP-1 receptor agonist, mimicking glucagon-like peptide-1. GLP-1 receptors in the brain reduce appetite and increase satiety; pancreatic GLP-1 receptors enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion. The mechanism is well established through years of clinical use as Ozempic and Wegovy.

**Cagrilintide** is a long-acting analogue of amylin, a hormone co-secreted with insulin from pancreatic beta cells in response to meals. Amylin complements insulin by slowing gastric emptying, suppressing glucagon secretion, and promoting satiety through direct effects on the brainstem [amylin-mechanism]. The only previously approved amylin analogue, pramlintide (Symlin), required multiple daily administrations and never achieved wide adoption.

Because GLP-1 acts largely on hypothalamic and reward circuits while amylin acts on brainstem meal-termination circuits, the two are described as reinforcing rather than duplicating each other. REDEFINE 1 tested that directly by carrying both monotherapy arms, and the combination beat each component without reaching the sum of the two [redefine-1-results].

## The REIMAGINE Programme: Type 2 Diabetes

The obesity story is only half the programme. Three phase 3 trials in type 2 diabetes published in June and July 2026, and they answer questions the obesity trials could not.

**REIMAGINE 1** ([NCT06323174](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06323174)) studied 189 adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on diet and exercise over 40 weeks. On the efficacy estimand, HbA1c fell 1.8 percentage points with CagriSema versus 0.1 with placebo, and body weight fell 13.8% versus 1.4% [reimagine-1].

**REIMAGINE 2** ([NCT06065540](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06065540)) is the important one: 2,713 adults with type 2 diabetes across 30 countries over 68 weeks, the first head-to-head of the combination against **its own two components**. On the efficacy estimand, HbA1c fell 1.91 percentage points with CagriSema versus 1.75 with semaglutide alone — an estimated treatment difference of −0.16 points (95% CI −0.27 to −0.05) [reimagine-2]. Statistically superior, and a small absolute margin. This trial also produced the first phase 3 cagrilintide monotherapy comparator data.

**REIMAGINE 3** ([NCT06323161](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06323161)) added CagriSema to stable basal insulin in 274 adults over 40 weeks. On the efficacy estimand, HbA1c fell 2.33 percentage points (2.4 mg each component) and 2.10 points (1.0 mg each) versus 0.66 with placebo, from a mean baseline of 8.8%, with body-weight reductions of 10–12% [reimagine-3].

Note the estimand shift: all three REIMAGINE trials report their primary glycaemic outcome on the **efficacy estimand**, so there is no treatment-policy figure to prefer. Say so when quoting them rather than implying the comparison is like-for-like with REDEFINE 1's treatment-policy number.

## What We Still Do Not Know

**Long-term outcomes.** The REDEFINE trials ran about 68 weeks, standard for obesity drug development. Whether weight loss persists, what happens on discontinuation, and multi-year safety remain to be characterised.

**The tirzepatide comparison is registered, not a PubMed result.** NCT06131437 is an 84-week open-label randomised phase 3 trial of CagriSema versus tirzepatide (actual enrollment 809; primary outcome non-inferiority on relative body-weight change at week 84; status COMPLETED, last update posted 2026-02-06). As of 2026-09-02 CT.gov reports `hasResults` false and a PubMed search for REDEFINE 4 + cagrilintide + tirzepatide returned 0 records, so this page does not quote a percent [nct-06131437].

**Cardiovascular outcomes.** REDEFINE 3 is an event-driven cardiovascular outcomes trial in roughly 7,000 adults; definitive MACE data have not been reported. Semaglutide alone has shown cardiovascular benefit, and whether the combination preserves or improves on it is unconfirmed.

**Special populations.** One gap has closed: a phase 1 study found that renal or hepatic impairment does not affect the pharmacokinetics, safety or tolerability of subcutaneous cagrilintide [cagrilintide-pk].

### Safety Considerations

Tolerability in the trials reflected the gastrointestinal effects common to both GLP-1 agonists and amylin analogues. In REDEFINE 1, gastrointestinal adverse events occurred in 79.6% of the CagriSema group versus 39.9% on placebo, described as mainly transient and mild-to-moderate [redefine-1-results]. Nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea were the most frequent, and most concentrated during escalation.

Whether combining two agents that both slow gastric emptying creates distinct tolerability problems for particular patients is a question real-world experience will answer, not a trial.

## How Strong Is the Evidence?

Strong for weight loss by clinical development standards. The REDEFINE programme comprised large, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials that met their primary endpoints [redefine-1-results], and the REIMAGINE programme has now added three phase 3 diabetes trials including a component-controlled head-to-head [reimagine-2].

The mechanistic rationale rests on decades of GLP-1 and amylin physiology [amylin-mechanism], and REDEFINE 1's monotherapy arms turned that rationale into a measured comparison rather than an assumption.

The limitations are those of any pre-approval dataset. Trial populations were selected and may not represent everyone who would receive the drug; rare adverse events surface post-marketing; and the registered tirzepatide head-to-head (NCT06131437) is not a published percent on this page.

## What's Next

**FDA decision.** The review process typically spans 10–12 months for standard review, 6 months for priority review. A first decision is expected in late 2026.

**Pricing and access.** How Novo prices a combination product will govern accessibility more than the efficacy margin will.

**Market positioning.** CagriSema enters a field that already contains injectable semaglutide, [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), oral semaglutide, and — with published phase 3 data of their own — [orforglipron](/peptides/orforglipron) and [retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide). The [CagriSema versus tirzepatide comparison](/compare/cagrisema-vs-tirzepatide) tracks that matchup against published trials rather than projections. The pairing that already earns clicks is [cagrilintide versus survodutide](/compare/cagrilintide-vs-survodutide).

**Ongoing development.** Novo continues to study cagrilintide in other combinations and populations, and has signalled a higher-dose CagriSema programme.

The CagriSema submission is a genuine milestone in obesity pharmacotherapy: it shows that combining complementary mechanisms produces weight loss previously associated with bariatric surgery. Whether that translates into better long-term health outcomes is the measure that has not been taken yet.
