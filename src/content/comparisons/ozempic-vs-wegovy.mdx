## Overview

[Ozempic](/peptides/semaglutide) and [Wegovy](/peptides/semaglutide) are two brand names for the same active ingredient: semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist. This page is a brand split, not a two-drug comparison. It does not invent a source census, a collapsed SUSTAIN A1C range, or a rounded MACE percent.

Type 2 diabetes brand evidence versus tirzepatide is [Ozempic vs Mounjaro](/compare/ozempic-vs-mounjaro). Obesity brand evidence versus tirzepatide is [Wegovy vs Zepbound](/compare/wegovy-vs-zepbound).

## Type 2 diabetes programme (SUSTAIN)

These trials studied weekly subcutaneous semaglutide in type 2 diabetes. They are not a head-to-head of the two brands.

### SUSTAIN-1 (glycemic, vs placebo)

PMID 28110911, NCT02054897. Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 30-week phase 3a trial. Modified intention-to-treat: 387 of 388 randomised participants received study medication (128 on 0.5 mg, 130 on 1.0 mg, 129 on pooled placebo). Baseline mean HbA1c 8.05%. Primary end point: change in mean HbA1c to week 30 [sustain-1-pmid-28110911].

| Arm | HbA1c change | ETD vs placebo |
|-----|--------------|----------------|
| Semaglutide 0.5 mg | −1.45% (95% CI −1.65 to −1.26) | −1.43% (95% CI −1.71 to −1.15; P&lt;0.0001) |
| Semaglutide 1.0 mg | −1.55% (95% CI −1.74 to −1.36) | −1.53% (95% CI −1.81 to −1.25; P&lt;0.0001) |
| Placebo | −0.02% (95% CI −0.23 to 0.18) | — |

Body weight: −3.73 kg (0.5 mg) and −4.53 kg (1.0 mg) vs −0.98 kg placebo. Nausea 20% / 24% vs 8%; diarrhea 13% / 11% vs 2%. This page does not restore a collapsed SUSTAIN 1–5 “1.0–1.8%” row [sustain-1-pmid-28110911].

### SUSTAIN-6 (cardiovascular, vs placebo)

PMID 27633186, NCT01720446. n=3297 adults with type 2 diabetes on a standard-care regimen; once-weekly semaglutide 0.5 mg or 1.0 mg versus placebo for 104 weeks. The paper hypothesized noninferiority (margin 1.8 for the upper bound of the 95% CI). Primary composite (first cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 108/1648 (**6.6%**) vs 146/1649 (**8.9%**); **HR 0.74** (95% CI 0.58 to 0.95; P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority). This page does not headline a rounded relative-risk percent [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].

Nonfatal myocardial infarction: 2.9% vs 3.9% (HR 0.74; 95% CI 0.51 to 1.08; P=0.12). Nonfatal stroke: 1.6% vs 2.7% (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.38 to 0.99; P=0.04). Rates of death from cardiovascular causes were similar. New or worsening nephropathy was lower with semaglutide. Retinopathy complications (vitreous hemorrhage, blindness, or conditions requiring an intravitreal agent or photocoagulation) were higher (HR 1.76; 95% CI 1.11 to 2.78; P=0.02) [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].

## Obesity programme (STEP)

These trials studied weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg. They are not an Ozempic-versus-Wegovy head-to-head.

### STEP 1 (overweight or obesity, no diabetes)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961 adults without diabetes; 68 weeks; 2:1 randomisation to once-weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg or placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. Inclusion in the abstract: BMI 30 or greater, or 27 or greater with at least one weight-related coexisting condition. The **primary estimand** assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions. Mean body-weight change **−14.9%** vs **−2.4%** (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% 86.4% vs 31.5%; ≥10% 69.1% vs 12.0%; ≥15% 50.5% vs 4.9%. Kilogram change −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg. GI events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8%. The STEP 1 abstract fetched 2026-09-02 does not report a ≥20% rate [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### STEP 2 and STEP 3 (not interchangeable with STEP 1)

| Trial | What the fetched abstract reports |
|-------|-----------------------------------|
| STEP 2 (PMID 33667417, NCT03552757) | n=1210, 68 weeks, overweight or obesity plus type 2 diabetes. Intention to treat. Semaglutide 2.4 mg −9.6% vs placebo −3.4% (ETD −6.2 pp; 95% CI −7.3 to −5.2; P&lt;0.0001). ≥5% 68.8% vs 28.5%. GI events 63.5% (2.4 mg) / 57.5% (1.0 mg) / 34.3% (placebo) [step-2-pmid-33667417] |

GI events 82.8% vs 63.2%; GI discontinuation 3.4% vs 0% [step-3-pmid-33625476] |

## Cardiovascular evidence in obesity without diabetes (SELECT)

Do not headline a rounded relative-risk percent without the hazard ratio.

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, overweight or obesity, no diabetes; once-weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo. Primary MACE 569/8803 (**6.5%**) vs 701/8801 (**8.0%**); **HR 0.80** (95% CI 0.72 to 0.90); P&lt;0.001. Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Permanent discontinuation for adverse events 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

SELECT is not SUSTAIN-6. The populations and trial arms in those abstracts are not the same.

## What these trials do not settle

- There is no Ozempic-versus-Wegovy efficacy head-to-head. They are the same molecule.
- SUSTAIN-1 is a 30-week monotherapy glycemic trial. It is not a SUSTAIN 1–5 range.
- SUSTAIN-6 used 0.5 mg or 1.0 mg in type 2 diabetes. STEP 1 and SELECT used 2.4 mg in people without diabetes.
- This page does not quote PIONEER or an oral semaglutide (Rybelsus) percent. Those papers were not fetched and this page is not about oral semaglutide.
- This increment did not fetch a SUSTAIN 7, STEP 4, or FDA-label year.

## Safety (only what these abstracts report)

This page does not invent a matched nausea table or copy a boxed-warning list from memory.

- SUSTAIN-1: nausea 20% / 24% vs 8%; diarrhea 13% / 11% vs 2% [sustain-1-pmid-28110911].
- SUSTAIN-6: more discontinuations for adverse events, mainly gastrointestinal; retinopathy complications HR 1.76 (95% CI 1.11 to 2.78; P=0.02) [sustain-6-pmid-27633186].
- STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; GI discontinuation 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].
- STEP 2: GI events 63.5% / 57.5% / 34.3% [step-2-pmid-33667417].
- STEP 3: GI events 82.8% vs 63.2%; GI discontinuation 3.4% vs 0% [step-3-pmid-33625476].
- SELECT: AE discontinuation 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].
