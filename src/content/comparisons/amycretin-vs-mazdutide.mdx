## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [Mazdutide](/peptides/mazdutide) is Innovent/Eli Lilly's investigational GLP-1 and glucagon receptor dual agonist. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, GLORY-1, GLORY-2, and the Hsia US phase 2 trial are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [mazdutide vs semaglutide](/compare/mazdutide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, populations, and estimands differ. Do not divide the amycretin 36-week estimated mean by the GLORY-1 week-48 treatment-policy percent.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### GLORY-1 (NEJM)

PMID 40421736, NCT05607680. Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in China. 610 adults randomly assigned 1:1:1 to mazdutide 4 mg, mazdutide 6 mg, or placebo for 48 weeks. The two primary endpoints were percentage change in body weight from baseline and a weight reduction of at least 5% at **week 32**, assessed with a **treatment-policy** estimand (effects regardless of early discontinuation or initiation of new antiobesity therapies).

Week 32 (coprimary): −10.09% (4 mg), −12.55% (6 mg), and +0.45% (placebo); at least 5% loss 73.9%, 82.0%, and 10.5% (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Week 48 (also reported): −11.00% (4 mg), **−14.01%** (6 mg), and +0.30% (placebo); at least 15% loss 35.7%, 49.5%, and 2.0% (P&lt;0.001). Adverse events leading to discontinuation: 1.5% (4 mg), 0.5% (6 mg), and 1.0% (placebo). The most frequently reported adverse events were gastrointestinal and mostly mild to moderate. The abstract does not publish a nausea percent [glory-1-pmid-40421736].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05607680 as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 610; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract. PubMed `NCT05607680` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40421736 only. A PubMed `"GLORY-1"[Title]` search on 2026-09-02 returned 0 records; the paper is quoted from the PMID title match.

### GLORY-2 (JAMA)

PMID 42251595, NCT06164873. Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in Chinese adults with obesity (BMI at least 30), with or without type 2 diabetes. 461 participants received study treatment (307 mazdutide 9 mg, 154 placebo) for 60 weeks. Coprimary outcomes were percentage change in body weight from baseline and a weight reduction of at least 5% at week 60.

At week 60, mean percentage change was **−16.65%** (95% CI −18.19 to −15.12) versus **−1.50%** (95% CI −3.43 to 0.43); between-group difference −15.15% (95% CI −17.22 to −13.09; P&lt;.001). At least 5% loss: 84.3% versus 33.1%. Adverse events leading to discontinuation: 2.9% versus 0%. Most common adverse events: vomiting 53.1% vs 1.3%; nausea 46.9% vs 3.2%; diarrhea 39.4% vs 6.5%. The abstract does **not** name a treatment-policy versus efficacy estimand [glory-2-pmid-42251595]. This page does not collapse GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 into one range.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06164873 as overallStatus UNKNOWN (actual enrollment 462; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract. PubMed `NCT06164873` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 42251595 only.

### Hsia US phase 2 (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology)

PMID 42628555, NCT06124807. US multicentre phase 2; 179 adults without type 2 diabetes randomly allocated to mazdutide 3–6 mg (32), 10 mg (48), 16 mg (51), or placebo (48) for 48 weeks. The **primary endpoint is the 32-week efficacy (hypothetical) estimand**. This is not a treatment-policy number.

At 32 weeks, least-squares mean percentage change: −7.3% (3–6 mg), −15.6% (10 mg), **−18.1%** (16 mg), versus **−0.9%** (placebo). Estimated treatment differences versus placebo ranged from −6.5% to −17.2% (P&lt;0.0001, all comparisons). The abstract reports additional reductions at 48 weeks **without 48-week percents**. Discontinuation for adverse events was most frequent at 16 mg (**20%**), associated primarily with gastrointestinal disorders [hsia-pmid-42628555].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06124807 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 179; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract, not the results module. PubMed `NCT06124807` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 42628555 only.
