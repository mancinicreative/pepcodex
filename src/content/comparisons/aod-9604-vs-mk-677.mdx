## Different studies, different endpoints

AOD-9604 is a modified growth-hormone fragment studied for obesity. MK-677 (ibutamoren) is an oral **nonpeptide ghrelin-receptor agonist** studied for GH-axis effects and other distinct outcomes. Shared growth-hormone context does not make them interchangeable. [FDA AOD-9604 review](https://www.fda.gov/media/183584/download); [FDA ibutamoren review](https://www.fda.gov/media/182087/download).

| Question | AOD-9604 | MK-677 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant cited population | Adults with obesity in the OPTIONS study | Healthy older adults in a body-composition study |
| Main finding | Weight-loss endpoints were not met | Fat-free mass increased; abdominal visceral and total fat did not significantly decline |
| Functional outcome | Not established by the obesity finding | Strength/function did not improve significantly; trial insufficiently powered to rule an effect out |
| FDA status | No approved weight-loss product identified | No approved ibutamoren product identified |

FDA's AOD-9604 review describes the randomized OPTIONS study: **502 of 536 enrolled adults were randomized**. Its weight-loss endpoints at 12 and 24 weeks were not met. This does not establish selective human fat loss or an absence of metabolic risk. [FDA AOD-9604 review](https://www.fda.gov/media/183584/download).

In the **65-person randomized older-adult** MK-677 trial, GH, IGF-1 and fat-free mass increased. Abdominal visceral and total fat did not significantly improve; fasting glucose rose and insulin sensitivity fell. Strength/function did not significantly improve, but the study was too small and short to assess those endpoints definitively. It cannot be ranked against the AOD-9604 obesity trial as if both tested the same population or clinical question. [Nass et al., PMID 18981485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18981485/).

Ibutamoren development continues under the **LUM-201** name: [Phase 3 NCT06948214](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06948214) was recruiting in the September 29, 2026 official registry archive. This is a pediatric growth-hormone-deficiency program, not a weight-loss approval. [FDA ibutamoren review](https://www.fda.gov/media/182087/download).

No direct trial here supports a ranking, combination or treatment recommendation.
