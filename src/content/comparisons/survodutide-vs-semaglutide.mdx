## Overview

Survodutide is a dual glucagon/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Boehringer Ingelheim). Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist (Novo Nordisk). SYNCHRONIZE-1 and STEP 1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio. Related compare: [survodutide vs tirzepatide](/compare/survodutide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head obesity RCTs. Durations and populations differ.

### SYNCHRONIZE-1 (obesity, no diabetes)

PMID 42253238, NCT06066515. Phase 3, n=725 (241 on 3.6 mg, 242 on 6.0 mg, 242 on placebo), 76 weeks. Primary analysis is the treatment-regimen estimand. Mean body-weight change: −12.2% (95% CI −13.6 to −10.8) with 3.6 mg, −13.0% (95% CI −14.4 to −11.6) with 6.0 mg, −5.4% (95% CI −6.9 to −4.0) with placebo. At least 5% weight reduction: 72.6%, 71.9%, and 46.3% (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9%, 89.7%, and 47.9%. No deaths reported [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

CT.gov NCT06066515 (accessed 2026-09-02): COMPLETED; acronym SYNCHRONIZE-1; enrollment 726 actual; hasResults false. Percents are from the paper.

### Phase 2 obesity dose-finding (not SYNCHRONIZE-1)

PMID 38330987, NCT04667377. 387 enrolled; 386 treated; 46 weeks (not a 48-week table). Planned-treatment mean body-weight change: −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), −14.9% (4.8 mg), −2.8% placebo. Gastrointestinal adverse events in 75% of survodutide recipients vs 42% on placebo [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

### STEP 1 (semaglutide 2.4 mg)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961; 68 weeks; no diabetes. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (treatment-regimen). Mean body-weight change: −14.9% vs −2.4% (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). At least 5%/10%/15% reduction: 86.4%/69.1%/50.5% vs 31.5%/12.0%/4.9%. Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

## Type 2 diabetes (16-week trial with open-label semaglutide)

PMID 38095657, NCT04153929. Phase 2; 413 randomised; 16 weeks. The semaglutide arm was open-label at up to 1.0 mg once weekly — not STEP 1’s 2.4 mg obesity dose. HbA1c change (percent units): −0.91% (DG1), −1.46% (DG2), −1.71% (DG3), −1.56% (DG4), −1.63% (DG5), −1.68% (DG6). Open-label semaglutide −1.47%. Bodyweight decreased dose-dependently up to −8.7% (95% CI −10.1 to −7.3; DG6) vs −5.3% with semaglutide. Adverse events: 77.8% on survodutide, 52.5% placebo, 52.0% semaglutide [survodutide-t2d-pmid-38095657].

## Liver-disease programmes

### Phase 2 MASH (not a resolution-percent table)

PMID 38847460, NCT04771273. 293 treated; 48 weeks. Primary end point: histologic improvement in MASH with no worsening of fibrosis — 47% (2.4 mg), 62% (4.8 mg), 43% (6.0 mg) vs 14% placebo (P&lt;0.001 for the quadratic dose-response). At least 30% liver-fat reduction: 63%, 67%, 57% vs 14%. Fibrosis improvement by at least one stage: 34%, 36%, 34% vs 22%. Nausea 66% vs 23%; diarrhea 49% vs 23%; vomiting 41% vs 4% [survodutide-mash-pmid-38847460].

### SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD

PMID 42252333. CT.gov identifier for this trial is **NCT06309992** (COMPLETED; enrollment 218 actual; hasResults false, accessed 2026-09-02). Paper n=216; 48 weeks. Treatment-regimen estimand leads: at least 30% liver-fat reduction 68.5% vs 28.6%; mean body-weight change −8.7% vs −1.4%. Efficacy estimand (labelled): 84.2% vs 24.3%; −12.2% vs −1.0% (P&lt;0.0001) [synchronize-masld-pmid-42252333].

The PubMed XML also listed LIVERAGE NCT06632457. That record is a separate recruiting cirrhosis trial (estimated enrollment 1590) and is not the source of these percents.

## Cardiovascular outcomes

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604 with preexisting cardiovascular disease and overweight or obesity, without diabetes; mean follow-up 39.8 months. 3-point MACE (cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke): 569/8803 (6.5%) vs 701/8801 (8.0%); hazard ratio 0.80 (95% CI 0.72 to 0.90; P&lt;0.001). Adverse events leading to discontinuation: 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].

## Side Effect Profile

SYNCHRONIZE-1 gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9% (3.6 mg), 89.7% (6.0 mg), 47.9% placebo [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

Phase 2 obesity GI events: 75% vs 42% [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

STEP 1: nausea and diarrhea most common; gastrointestinal discontinuation 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** SYNCHRONIZE-1 and STEP 1 differ in duration and placebo response.
2. **Treatment-regimen leads:** SYNCHRONIZE-1 −12.2% and −13.0% vs −5.4%; STEP 1 −14.9% vs −2.4%.
3. **Phase 2 obesity is 46 weeks:** planned-treatment −14.9% at 4.8 mg vs −2.8% placebo.
4. **Same-trial T2D arm is 16 weeks and 1.0 mg semaglutide:** do not read it as STEP 1.
