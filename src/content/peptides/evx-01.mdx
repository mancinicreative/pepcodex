EVX-01 is a **personalized peptide mixture**, not a single fixed-sequence peptide. Evaxion's PIONEER platform selects tumor-specific neoantigens from each person's tumor and normal-tissue data. The selected peptides are formulated with an adjuvant for clinical investigation. EVX-01 is a peptide platform; the related mRNA-4157 page describes a different platform, not a head-to-head comparator. ([First-in-human study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35036074/), [phase 2 design paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36047545/))

### What the peer-reviewed study found

NCT03715985 was a first-in-human, open-label phase 1/2 study of EVX-01 **added to anti-PD-1 treatment**. Safety and tolerability were its primary objective. A 2022 paper described the first five participants; the 2024 dose-escalation publication expanded the report to 12, so those papers do not represent independent cohorts. The first-five-person report recorded vaccine manufacture in 48–55 days and treatment initiation within 60 days of baseline biopsy; this is observed feasibility in that study, not a guaranteed turnaround. In the later report, all 12 had vaccine-specific T-cell responses measured in laboratory assays; 8 of 12 had an observed objective tumor response, including six partial and two complete responses. Without a concurrent anti-PD-1-only arm, that 8/12 observation cannot quantify EVX-01's added clinical benefit. ([2022 interim report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35036074/), [2024 dose-escalation report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782542/), [official study record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03715985))

### What is newer, and how strong is it?

The separate KEYNOTE-D36 phase 2 registration, NCT05309421, describes an open-label, single-arm study in adults with unresectable or metastatic melanoma who were checkpoint-inhibitor naïve. Pembrolizumab preceded the first EVX-01 administration by 12 weeks; the registered primary endpoint concerns improvement in best overall response after that initial treatment. The official record lists **17 actual enrollees**, is marked **active, not recruiting**, and was last posted on **September 22, 2025**. It has no posted results section in the checked API record. ([Trial design paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36047545/), [official study record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05309421))

In an **April 2026 AACR poster**, Evaxion reported 12 objective responses among **16 clinically analyzed** phase 2 participants (75%) and vaccine-specific immune responses in **15 assayed** participants. An individual-neoantigen analysis in **13 of those 15** found **101 immunogenic among 118 tested neoantigens**. These are sponsor-reported conference results, not a peer-reviewed phase 2 outcomes publication or a randomized comparison. Evaxion's October 2025 release explicitly said the registered primary response-conversion endpoint was **not formally analyzed** because enrollment fell below the original plan. Its August 2026 business update discussed planned further follow-up rather than a new published outcome analysis. Do not pool the 12-person phase 1 results with the 16-person phase 2 response subset or treat immune and tumor-response denominators as interchangeable. ([AACR 2026 poster](https://evaxion.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/EVX01_AACR2026_Poster_FINAL_20260408_forupload.pdf), [October 2025 sponsor release](https://evaxion.ai/investors/press-releases/?id=10646&press_release_pdf=1), [August 2026 sponsor update](https://evaxion.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/evaxion-announces-business-update-and-second-quarter-2026))

### Why the response numbers need restraint

These studies show that EVX-01 can be manufactured for individual participants and is associated with measurable neoantigen-specific immune responses. They do **not** establish that it raises response rates, extends survival, or is safer than anti-PD-1 treatment alone. The 2024 publication did not use a randomized control arm; the phase 2 data currently cited are from a sponsor conference poster and company releases. A historical pembrolizumab response rate cannot substitute for a concurrent comparison. ([2024 dose-escalation report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782542/), [official phase 2 record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05309421), [AACR 2026 poster](https://evaxion.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/EVX01_AACR2026_Poster_FINAL_20260408_forupload.pdf))
