## TL;DR

PepCodex already owns the **hardest** moat (machine-verified citations — `qa-pmids.mjs --strict` is build-breaking in prebuild today) and the **reference loop pattern** (`refresh-trials.mjs`: fetch public API → normalize → merge-preserving-curated-fields → stamp date → dry-run/`--apply`). Almost every loop the owner wants can be built by **extending an existing script**, not writing a new pipeline. The gaps are not capability gaps — they are **scheduling** (no `.github/workflows`, loops run only when invoked by hand), **coverage** (30 of 102 dossiers have a source pack), **visible freshness** (no user-facing "last verified" date or changelog; blog dead since 2026-02-18), and **integrity breadth** (only PMIDs are resolve-checked — NCT, DOI, and retraction status are not).

**Verified ground truth (this session):** 102 dossiers · 30 source-packs · 102 scoring blocks (rubric v2.4) · 155 blog posts · **0 GitHub workflows** · prebuild `check = validate-cross-links && qa-seo && qa-scoring && qa-pmids --strict`.

## Competitive bar

The bar PepCodex must clear to be judged "best, most-current" (synthesised from Examine / Drugs.com / DailyMed / UpToDate / Cochrane + 2026 E-E-A-T/YMYL + AI-answer-engine source selection):

1. **Visible freshness** — a real "last verified/reviewed" date + per-page changelog on every page, backed by measurable update velocity. *PepCodex: BELOW bar (date exists in frontmatter, not surfaced).*
2. **Citation integrity at density** — every claim links to a resolving primary source; retracted/superseded sources actively flagged. *PepCodex: AT/ABOVE bar on resolution (the hardest moat); BELOW on retraction-watch + NCT/DOI gating.*
3. **Credentialed review** — named author + named reviewer with verifiable credentials, surfaced via MedicalWebPage `reviewedBy`/`lastReviewed`. *PepCodex: BELOW bar. NOTE: requires a real credentialed reviewer to exist — do not fabricate one.*
4. **Structured data** — MedicalWebPage/Drug/MedicalScholarlyArticle markup feeding Google YMYL + AI engines. *PepCodex: partial (MedicalWebPage-adjacent).*
5. **Discovery velocity** — automated PubMed/CT.gov/openFDA surveillance so new science appears fast, with a public research feed. *PepCodex: BELOW bar (manual).*

**Headline:** above bar on citation integrity (the hardest), below bar on the five *visible* freshness/trust signals — exactly where the candidate loops aim. **Two tactics that VIOLATE the non-negotiables and must be avoided:** (a) auto-publishing any content without the resolve-every-ID gate + human merge; (b) treating community/forum/competitor pages as a citation source (discovery signal only — firewall from claims, strip dosing/sourcing/anecdote).

## Top recommendations — build first (highest impact-per-effort, max reuse, integrity-safe)

1. **Trials freshness all-slug orchestrator** *(Freshness · S · High)* — The single lowest-effort, highest-trust win. `refresh-trials.mjs` already does the whole job for one slug and never fabricates; wrapping it in an all-pack loop + a visible "last synced" date instantly makes the headline tracker honest. Pure reuse, zero new claim surface.
2. **Identifier-integrity gate extension (NCT + DOI) + Retraction watch** *(Integrity · S–M · High)* — Extends the already-build-breaking `qa-pmids.mjs` to close the only remaining integrity holes (NCT/DOI resolution + retraction). Directly hardens the moat that every other content loop depends on, using the existing walk+batch skeleton + the free Retraction Watch CSV.
3. **"Last verified" evidence-freshness heartbeat** *(Freshness · M · High)* — Makes "always up-to-date" *provable* (the axis Examine and undated wikis lose on) and is a YMYL/AI-citation ranking signal. `scoring.lastReviewed` + `DossierLayout` dateModified already exist; the loop just becomes their honest writer (bumps the date only on a real re-verification pass).
4. **CT.gov new-intervention radar** *(Discovery · M · High)* — Highest-leverage breadth expansion at the lowest fabrication risk: NCT IDs can't be hallucinated, the diff target (102 slugs) exists, and a stub needs zero free-text claims. Closes the trials-coverage gap and gives the earliest public signal of a new molecule. Forks `refresh-trials.mjs`.
5. **Daily research-digest blog draft** *(Content/SEO · L · High)* — The owner's flagship ask and the recurring-traffic + newsletter engine. Higher effort and best sequenced *after* loops 1–4 (it consumes their diffs), but it is the loop that converts all the freshness work into a visible, monetizable cadence. Already fully spec'd in `DAILY-BRIEFING-LOOP.md`. Prototype as a manual `/schedule` agent for 1–2 weeks before autonomy.

*Sequencing note: the integrity gate (rec 2) is the prerequisite that makes every content-writing loop safe — keep it ahead of or alongside the daily blog. Recs 1, 2, 4 are near-pure reuse; rec 3 is light glue; rec 5 is the orchestration-heavy payoff.*
