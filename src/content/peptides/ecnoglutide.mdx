### How It Works (Simplified)

Ecnoglutide (XW003) is a long-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist designed for once-weekly subcutaneous injection. It mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1 to suppress appetite, slow gastric emptying, and enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion. Developed by Sciwind Biosciences, it represents China's entry into the competitive GLP-1 obesity therapeutics market.

### Scientific Pathways

**GLP-1 Receptor Activation:** Like other GLP-1 receptor agonists, ecnoglutide binds to and activates GLP-1 receptors in the pancreas, brain, and GI tract. This triggers multiple downstream effects: enhanced insulin secretion (glucose-dependent), suppressed glucagon release, delayed gastric emptying, and central satiety signaling in the hypothalamus.

**Engineering for Duration:** Ecnoglutide incorporates structural modifications to resist DPP-4 enzymatic degradation and extend half-life, enabling once-weekly dosing comparable to semaglutide.

### Clinical Evidence

**Regulatory note (journal, not a label):** Shirley 2026 (PMID 42412371) summarises reported first approvals of subcutaneous ecnoglutide in China for adult T2DM glycaemic control and later for long-term weight management. See `regulatoryStatus` — no NMPA/openFDA/EMA document was retrieved on 2026-09-02.

**Phase 1 DDI papers (not obesity treatment-policy):** Li 2026 (PMID 42310888; n=28 who received at least one dose; open-label single-sequence crossover) reported rosuvastatin AUC GMR 106% (90% CI 94–120%) and digoxin AUC GMR 84% (90% CI 76–94%) with versus without steady-state ecnoglutide. The abstract also states a mean 11.2% body-weight decrease across a 14-week intervention in that healthy-volunteer DDI study — exploratory, not a Phase 3 treatment-policy obesity estimand. Chen 2026 (PMID 42137314; n=28; open-label fixed-sequence crossover) reported metformin and S-/R-warfarin AUC GMRs inside pre-specified 80.00–125.00% no-effect bounds.

**Registered trials fetched 2026-09-02 (no results posted):** oral EVOLVE-2 NCT07281937 (Phase 2, completed, n=206 actual, hasResults false); NCT07553299 (oral weight-maintenance Phase 2, active, estimated n=120); NCT07243171 (oral tablets Phase 1/2, recruiting); NCT07628127 (oral VRB-103 ± VRB-101, Phase 1, recruiting); NCT07734311 (injection, obese participants with knee osteoarthritis, Phase 3, recruiting); NCT07434050 and NCT07387094 (XW003 injection in obese participants with OSA, Phase 3, recruiting); NCT07073417 SLIMMER-UP-SWITCH (XW003 vs semaglutide injection, Phase 2 open-label switch, active, n=163 actual, hasResults false); NCT07143227 (adolescent XW003 injection, Phase 1, completed, n=48 actual, hasResults false).

**Prior Phase 3 obesity figure on this page:** The existing 40-week Chinese obesity Phase 3 body-weight figure was not re-fetched in this increment and is not a new 90-day paper.

### Safety Profile

The safety profile is consistent with the GLP-1 receptor agonist class, with gastrointestinal adverse events (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea) as the most common side effects. No unique safety signals beyond the established GLP-1 class effects have been reported. Safety data is primarily from Chinese patient populations; global generalizability awaits broader trials.

### Important Limitations

- Journal-reported China approvals (PMID 42412371) are not confirmed by an NMPA/openFDA/EMA document fetched 2026-09-02
- No FDA application appears in openFDA drugsfda as of 2026-09-02
- Oral EVOLVE-2 (NCT07281937) and the semaglutide switch trial (NCT07073417) have no posted results
- Safety and DDI data in the new papers are Phase 1 open-label, not Phase 3 treatment-policy
- Limited English-language publication of confirmatory Phase 3 obesity and T2D datasets
