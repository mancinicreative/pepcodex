## Research-chem / peptide-vendor affiliates (explicit)

**Verdict: REJECT.**

Reasons, independently of any one network’s unpublished commission:

1. Live advertising policy: “Advertising for peptide vendors or sources” is in **What We Don’t Accept**.
2. Editorial policy: sourcing/purchasing information is excluded.
3. FDA compounding + unapproved-drug context (BPC-157 Category 2; ibutamoren Category 2; PCAC 2026 proposals against several wellness peptides; compounded GLP-1 quality concerns).
4. Nearby name collisions in the fake clinic file are themselves vendors (`alamopeptides.com`, `northwestpeptides.com`) — proof the topic area is saturated with RUO storefronts.
5. Commission **cannot** be isolated from evidence grading in practice: the highest-RPM SKUs would be the lowest-evidence research peptides.
6. Terms of those programs were **not opened** (authorization boundary). Rates stay **UNVERIFIABLE**. Absence of a published rate is not permission to guess one.

A disclosure (“we may earn a commission”) does **not** legalize a sourcing recommendation the site otherwise bans.

## What “PURSUE” is allowed to mean

Only:

- First-party PepTracker conversion (already the growth constraint’s intended CTA).
- Unpaid citations to primary literature and regulators.
- Later, labeled non-vendor sponsorships **after** the directory fiction is gone and counsel signs the firewall.

Nothing in this matrix authorizes implementation.

## Pre-enrollment tests (if a future CAUTIOUS TEST is approved)

1. Can the public terms be read without an account? If no → UNVERIFIABLE, stop.
2. Does the advertiser sell a peptide, compounding service, or clinic lead? If yes → REJECT.
3. Would the unit appear on a dossier? If yes → default REJECT (grades stay unpaid).
4. Is the disclosure “Sponsored” / “Ad” / “Affiliate” (as applicable) adjacent to the unit, not in a footer only?
5. Does ops have a kill-switch to drop the unit without a deploy that touches evidence copy?
