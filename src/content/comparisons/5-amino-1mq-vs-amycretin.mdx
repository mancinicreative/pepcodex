## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run and the amycretin Lancet trials are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [5-Amino-1MQ vs semaglutide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Species, durations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

ClinicalTrials.gov v2 search for `5-Amino-1MQ`, `5-amino-1-methylquinolinium`, and `NNMT 5-amino` each returned 0 studies (hasResults not applicable).

None of the three PubMed hits is a human obesity RCT. PMID 35013352 is a diet-induced obese (DIO) mouse microbiome paper. The abstract names 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium as the NNMT inhibitor used with a low-fat diet and describes whole-body adiposity and weight loss in mice relative to diet switch alone. The abstract does **not** publish a body-weight percent. This page does not invent one [nnmti-dio-mice-pmid-35013352].

PMID 39067875 title-matched as an NNMT / urothelial bladder cancer CAF paper. The abstract names 5-Amino-1-methylquinolinium iodide in UBC mouse models. It is not an obesity RCT and is not quoted as a weight-change result. PMID 33645410 title did not name 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium; it is not quoted.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

A per-alias PubMed search for `"amycretin"` on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later papers that were not fetched this increment.

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

2. **5-Amino-1MQ:** PubMed street-name aliases returned 0; the chemical-name search returned 3 papers, none a human obesity RCT. The DIO mouse abstract names the compound and publishes **no** percent (PMID 35013352). CT.gov queries returned 0 studies as of 2026-09-02.
