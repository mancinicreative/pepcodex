## Overview

Amycretin is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. Orforglipron is Eli Lilly's oral nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist. The amycretin oral first-in-human trial, the amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a trial, ATTAIN-1, and the orforglipron phase 2 obesity trial are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [orforglipron](/peptides/orforglipron), [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide), and [orforglipron vs semaglutide](/compare/orforglipron-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, populations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew. The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen estimand.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

A per-alias PubMed search for `"amycretin"` on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later type 2 diabetes papers that were not fetched this increment.

### Orforglipron ATTAIN-1 (NEJM)

PMID 40960239, NCT05869903. Phase 3; 3127 adults with obesity without diabetes; 72 weeks. Primary endpoint was percent change in body weight to week 72, **treatment-regimen estimand**, intention-to-treat. Mean change −7.5% (6 mg), −8.4% (12 mg), and −11.2% (36 mg) vs −2.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 for all vs placebo). At 36 mg, ≥10% 54.6% vs 12.9% placebo; ≥15% 36.0% vs 5.9%; ≥20% 18.4% vs 2.8%. Adverse events led to treatment discontinuation in 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7% placebo. Gastrointestinal events were most common and mostly mild to moderate [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05869903 as Phase 3, ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING, actual enrollment 3127, hasResults true as of 2026-09-02. PubMed on that NCT also returned PMID 42577069 (post hoc ≥65-year subgroup of ATTAIN-1 and ATTAIN-2). This page quotes the primary ATTAIN-1 paper only and does not substitute the subgroup percents.

### Orforglipron phase 2 (NEJM)

PMID 37351564, NCT05051579. Phase 2; 272 adults with obesity, or overweight plus at least one weight-related coexisting condition, without diabetes; 36 weeks. Week 26 (primary) mean change ranged from −8.6% to −12.6% across orforglipron groups vs −2.0% placebo. Week 36 (secondary) mean change ranged from −9.4% to −14.7% vs −2.3% placebo. A ≥10% reduction by week 36 occurred in 46–75% vs 9% placebo. Gastrointestinal events were mild to moderate and led to discontinuation in 10–17% across groups [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564]. The abstract does **not** publish a 36 mg vs 45 mg split or an HbA1c table; this page does not invent one.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05051579 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 272; hasResults true as of 2026-09-02). PubMed on that NCT also returned PMID 40481478 (exploratory cardiovascular-biomarker analysis spanning two phase 2 programmes). This page does not quote that paper as the obesity weight result.

### No head-to-head RCT in this increment

A PubMed search for `"amycretin" AND "orforglipron"` on 2026-09-02 returned 4 records (PMIDs 42673585, 41948476, 41054801, 40949933). All four are reviews. None of the titles names amycretin or orforglipron. None is a randomised head-to-head obesity trial. That is a window-dated finding for that scan, not an eternal claim.

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

ATTAIN-1: gastrointestinal events most common, mostly mild to moderate; discontinuation 5.3–10.3% vs 2.7% placebo. No nausea percent in the abstract [attain-1-pmid-40960239].

Phase 2: gastrointestinal events mild to moderate; discontinuation 10–17% across groups [orforglipron-p2-pmid-37351564].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means. ATTAIN-1 is a published phase 3 treatment-regimen result. They are not one trial.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%. Estimand not named.
4. **ATTAIN-1:** treatment-regimen 36 mg week 72 −11.2% vs −2.1%. Phase 2 is quoted as the published week-36 range −9.4% to −14.7% vs −2.3%, not a 36 mg / 45 mg table.
5. **Foundayo:** NDA 220934 ORIG approved 2026-04-01 per Drugs@FDA accessed 2026-09-02. Amycretin had no openFDA application on that date.
