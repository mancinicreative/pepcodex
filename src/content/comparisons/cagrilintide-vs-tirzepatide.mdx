## Overview

Cagrilintide is Novo Nordisk's investigational long-acting amylin analogue. Tirzepatide is Eli Lilly's dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. The Phase 2 cagrilintide monotherapy trial, REDEFINE 1 (CagriSema), and SURMOUNT-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [cagrilintide](/peptides/cagrilintide), [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), and [CagriSema vs tirzepatide](/compare/cagrisema-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations and estimands differ.

### Cagrilintide monotherapy (Phase 2)

PMID 34798060, NCT03856047. n=706 randomly assigned (100–102 per cagrilintide dose; 99 liraglutide 3.0 mg; 101 placebo); 26-week treatment period. The paper reports both a trial-product estimand (assuming adherence) and a treatment-policy estimand (regardless of adherence).

Trial-product mean percent weight change at week 26: cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg 6.0%–10.8% versus placebo 3.0% (estimated treatment difference range 3.0–7.8 percentage points; P&lt;0.001). Cagrilintide 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0% (estimated treatment difference 1.8 percentage points; P=0.03). The abstract states that similar reductions were observed with the treatment-policy estimand; it does not publish a separate treatment-policy percent table. This page does not invent a 2.4 mg row [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

Gastrointestinal adverse events: 41%–63% on cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg versus 32% placebo, primarily nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

### CagriSema is not cagrilintide monotherapy

REDEFINE 1 (PMID 40544433, NCT05567796) randomised 3,417 adults without diabetes to cagrilintide-semaglutide, semaglutide 2.4 mg, cagrilintide 2.4 mg, or placebo for 68 weeks. Treatment-policy mean change: −20.4% with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus −3.0% placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). The abstract does not publish the cagrilintide-alone percent. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

This page does not quote an unpublished CagriSema versus tirzepatide percent.

### SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2,539; 72 weeks; no diabetes. Treatment-regimen mean change at week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg), −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). At least 20% reduction: 50% (10 mg) and 57% (15 mg; 95% CI 53–61) versus 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5 / 10 / 15 mg and placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

### Registered head-to-head (CagriSema, not monotherapy)

NCT06131437 is a completed Phase 3 open comparison of CagriSema 2.4 mg / 2.4 mg versus tirzepatide 15 mg (actual enrollment 809; primary completion 2025-12-08; completion 2026-01-09). ClinicalTrials.gov `hasResults` was false as of 2026-09-02. This page does not quote a percent from that registry record [nct-06131437].

## Side Effect Profile

Phase 2 cagrilintide: gastrointestinal events 41%–63% versus 32% placebo; nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

REDEFINE 1 CagriSema: gastrointestinal events 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation for adverse events 6.2% (15 mg) versus 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. The SURMOUNT-1 abstract does not publish separate nausea or vomiting percents; this page does not invent them.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** Phase 2 cagrilintide monotherapy is 26 weeks; SURMOUNT-1 is 72 weeks.
2. **Monotherapy is a range, not a 2.4 mg row:** trial-product 6.0%–10.8% versus 3.0% placebo; 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0%. The abstract does not publish a treatment-policy percent table.
3. **CagriSema is the combination:** REDEFINE 1 treatment-policy −20.4% versus −3.0%. That figure is not a cagrilintide-alone result.
4. **Treatment-regimen leads for tirzepatide:** SURMOUNT-1 15 mg −20.9% versus −3.1%; ≥20% at 15 mg is 57% (95% CI 53–61).
5. **Registered H2H is CagriSema versus tirzepatide:** NCT06131437 completed, hasResults false, PubMed 0 as of 2026-09-02; no percent quoted.
