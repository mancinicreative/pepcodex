## Overview

Survodutide is a dual glucagon/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Boehringer Ingelheim). Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). SYNCHRONIZE-1, the phase 2 obesity dose-finding trial, SURMOUNT-1, and SURMOUNT-2 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

## Key Facts

| Aspect | Survodutide | Tirzepatide |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Developer** | Boehringer Ingelheim | Eli Lilly |
| **Targets** | GLP-1 + glucagon | GIP + GLP-1 |
| **FDA status on this page** | Investigational | FDA-approved (Mounjaro, Zepbound) |
| **Obesity trial quoted here** | SYNCHRONIZE-1, 76 weeks [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238] | SURMOUNT-1, 72 weeks [surmount-1-pmid-35658024] |

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations and populations differ.

### SYNCHRONIZE-1 (obesity, no diabetes)

PMID 42253238, NCT06066515. Phase 3, n=725 (241 on 3.6 mg, 242 on 6.0 mg, 242 on placebo), 76 weeks. Primary analysis is the treatment-regimen estimand. Mean body-weight change: −12.2% (95% CI −13.6 to −10.8) with 3.6 mg, −13.0% (95% CI −14.4 to −11.6) with 6.0 mg, −5.4% (95% CI −6.9 to −4.0) with placebo. At least 5% weight reduction: 72.6%, 71.9%, and 46.3% (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9%, 89.7%, and 47.9%. No deaths reported [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

### Phase 2 obesity dose-finding (not SYNCHRONIZE-1)

PMID 38330987, NCT04667377. 387 enrolled; 386 treated; 46 weeks (not a 48-week table). Planned-treatment mean body-weight change: −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), −14.9% (4.8 mg), −2.8% placebo. Gastrointestinal adverse events in 75% of survodutide recipients vs 42% on placebo [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

### SURMOUNT-1 and SURMOUNT-2 (tirzepatide)

**SURMOUNT-1** (PMID 35658024, NCT04184622): n=2539; 72 weeks; no diabetes. Treatment-regimen mean change at week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg), −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). At least 5% reduction: 85%, 89%, 91% vs 35% placebo. At least 20% reduction: 50% (10 mg) and 57% (15 mg; 95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5/10/15 mg and placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

**SURMOUNT-2** (PMID 37385275, NCT04657003): n=938 with obesity and type 2 diabetes; 72 weeks. Treatment-regimen body-weight change: −12.8% (10 mg), −14.7% (15 mg), −3.2% placebo. At least 5% reduction: 79–83% vs 32%. Gastrointestinal events were the most frequent adverse events; few led to discontinuation (&lt;5%) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275].

## Type 2 diabetes (16-week survodutide trial)

PMID 38095657, NCT04153929. Phase 2; 413 randomised; 16 weeks. HbA1c change (percent units): −0.91% (DG1), −1.46% (DG2), −1.71% (DG3), −1.56% (DG4), −1.63% (DG5), −1.68% (DG6). Open-label semaglutide −1.47%. Bodyweight decreased dose-dependently up to −8.7% (95% CI −10.1 to −7.3; DG6) vs −5.3% with semaglutide. Adverse events: 77.8% on survodutide, 52.5% placebo, 52.0% semaglutide [survodutide-t2d-pmid-38095657].

## Side Effect Profile

SYNCHRONIZE-1 gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9% (3.6 mg), 89.7% (6.0 mg), 47.9% placebo [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

Phase 2 obesity GI events: 75% vs 42% [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation for adverse events 6.2% (15 mg) vs 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].
