SS-31 is elamipretide, a tetrapeptide studied for binding cardiolipin in mitochondrial membranes. Biochemical and animal findings are a mechanistic basis, but clinical effects vary by condition. [PMID:23813215](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23813215/)

### Product-specific FDA approval

On September 19, 2025, FDA granted **accelerated approval to Forzinity (elamipretide)** to improve muscle strength in adult and pediatric patients with **Barth syndrome weighing at least 30 kg**. Approval relied on knee-extensor muscle strength as an intermediate endpoint; a confirmatory trial is required to verify clinical benefit. This approval does not cover general mitochondrial support, longevity, heart failure, age-related macular degeneration or primary mitochondrial myopathy. The Forzinity label reports serious hypersensitivity reactions and frequent injection-site reactions. It describes the approved product, not the quality or safety of other SS-31 preparations. [FDA approval announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome) · [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2025/215244Orig1s000Lbl.pdf)

### What the trials establish

In the 12-person randomized, placebo-controlled Barth syndrome crossover phase, **neither prespecified primary endpoint** (6-minute walk distance and symptom score) was met. Open-label extension and natural-history comparisons later showed favorable signals, but their design limits causal certainty. FDA's approval was narrower than a general treatment claim. [Barth randomized trial, PMID:33077895](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33077895/) · [FDA trial snapshot](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshots-forzinity)

The 218-person MMPOWER-3 randomized trial in primary mitochondrial myopathy found no significant benefit on either primary endpoint at 24 weeks: six-minute walk distance or total fatigue. The study therefore does not support extending the Barth indication to that disease. [PMID:37268435](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37268435/)

Interactions with other mitochondrial supplements and peptides listed in older versions of this page have not been established as beneficial or safe combinations.
