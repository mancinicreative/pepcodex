## Safety Overview

Tirzepatide has been evaluated in the SURPASS (type 2 diabetes) and SURMOUNT (obesity) phase 3
programmes. The three published trials cited on this page enrolled 475 adults with type 2 diabetes
(SURPASS-5, [PMID 35133415](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35133415/)), 2,539 adults with obesity
(SURMOUNT-1, [PMID 35658024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35658024/)), and 783 adults in the
open-label lead-in of SURMOUNT-4, of whom 670 were randomised
([PMID 38078870](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38078870/)).

The adverse reaction rates below are taken from the FDA prescribing information rather than from any
single trial, because the label pools the placebo-controlled weight-management trials.

## Common Adverse Reactions

From Table 2 of the [Zepbound prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=487cd7e7-434c-4925-99fa-aa80b1cc776b)
— reactions occurring in at least 2% of treated adults and more often than placebo, in the
placebo-controlled chronic weight management trials:

| Adverse reaction | Placebo (N=958) | 5 mg (N=630) | 10 mg (N=948) | 15 mg (N=941) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nausea | 8% | 25% | 29% | 28% |
| Diarrhea | 8% | 19% | 21% | 23% |
| Vomiting | 2% | 8% | 11% | 13% |
| Constipation | 5% | 17% | 14% | 11% |
| Abdominal pain | 5% | 9% | 9% | 10% |
| Dyspepsia | 4% | 9% | 9% | 10% |
| Fatigue | 3% | 5% | 6% | 7% |

Trial-level rates differ from the pooled label figures. SURPASS-5 (PMID 35133415), cited below,
reports nausea at 13%–18% and diarrhoea at 12%–21% across tirzepatide arms. Read the label table
as the pooled safety population and the trial papers as individual studies; they are not
interchangeable.

The label reports gastrointestinal adverse reactions in 56% of treated patients at each strength
versus 30% on placebo, and states that "the majority of nausea, vomiting, and/or diarrhea events
occurred during dose escalation and decreased over time." Gastrointestinal reactions led to
discontinuation in 1.9%, 3.3%, and 4.3% of the 5 mg, 10 mg, and 15 mg groups, versus 0.5% on placebo.
In SURMOUNT-1, adverse events of any type caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, and 6.2% of the
tirzepatide groups versus 2.6% on placebo.

## Serious Warnings

### Boxed Warning: Risk of Thyroid C-Cell Tumors

The label's boxed warning states: "In rats, tirzepatide causes thyroid C-cell tumors. It is unknown
whether ZEPBOUND causes thyroid C-cell tumors, including medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), in humans
as the human relevance of tirzepatide-induced rodent thyroid C-cell tumors has not been determined."
This is a rat finding. The label does not claim it has been shown in people.

### Other Warnings and Precautions

The current Zepbound label (DailyMed SPL, published 6 May 2026) lists these under Warnings and
Precautions, sections 5.2 through 5.10:

- **5.2 Severe gastrointestinal adverse reactions** — reported more often on Zepbound (5 mg 1.7%, 10 mg 2.5%, 15 mg 3.1%) than placebo (1%); not recommended in patients with severe gastroparesis
- **5.3 Acute kidney injury due to volume depletion** — monitor renal function in patients reporting reactions that could cause volume depletion
- **5.4 Acute gallbladder disease** — reported in clinical trials; investigate if cholecystitis is suspected
- **5.5 Acute pancreatitis** — reported in clinical trials; discontinue promptly if suspected, and do not restart if confirmed
- **5.6 Hypersensitivity reactions** — anaphylaxis and angioedema reported postmarketing
- **5.7 Hypoglycemia** — risk increases with concomitant insulin or an insulin secretagogue
- **5.8 Diabetic retinopathy complications** in patients with type 2 diabetes — monitor those with a history of retinopathy
- **5.9 Pulmonary aspiration during general anesthesia or deep sedation**
- **5.10 Never share a Zepbound KwikPen between patients**

**Suicidal behavior and ideation is no longer on this label.** The label's own Recent Major
Changes table records it as "Suicidal Behavior and Ideation (Removed) 02/2026". Pages still
listing it as a current warning — including earlier versions of this one — are describing a
warning the FDA has withdrawn.

## Who Should NOT Use Tirzepatide

The label lists two contraindications:

- Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2)
- Known serious hypersensitivity to tirzepatide or any of the excipients

## Special Populations

- **Pregnancy:** the label states that "weight loss offers no benefit to a pregnant patient and may cause fetal harm," and advises discontinuing when pregnancy is recognised
- **Lactation:** there are no data on tirzepatide in animal or human milk, or on effects on the breastfed infant
- **Pediatric:** safety and effectiveness have not been established under 18 years of age
- **Renal impairment:** the label recommends no adjustment, including in end-stage renal disease, and reports no change in tirzepatide pharmacokinetics; renal function should still be monitored if reactions could cause volume depletion
- **Oral contraceptives:** the label warns that tirzepatide may reduce the efficacy of oral hormonal contraceptives because of delayed gastric emptying
