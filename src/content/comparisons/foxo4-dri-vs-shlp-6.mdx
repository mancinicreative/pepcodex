## What was studied

| Question | FOXO4-DRI | SHLP-6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experimental focus | FOXO4-p53 signaling and apoptosis in senescent-cell models | Mitochondria-derived peptide tested for effects on cell viability and apoptosis |
| Direct finding cited here | Senescent-cell and selected mouse outcomes | Increased apoptosis in mouse NIT-1 beta cells and human 22Rv1 prostate-cancer cells in culture |
| Selective senescent-cell clearance in people | Not established | Not established; the cited SHLP-6 experiment did not use a senescence-clearance model |
| Human longevity or safety outcome | Not established | Not established |

The original FOXO4-DRI study reported apoptosis in experimental senescent-cell systems and changes in selected measures in naturally aged and accelerated-aging mice. It was published in *Cell* in 2017. These model results do not establish human healthspan improvement or universal selectivity. [Baar et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28340339/)

SHLP-6 was described with the small humanin-like peptide family in a 2016 *Aging* paper. In that paper, SHLP-6 increased an apoptosis readout in NIT-1 and 22Rv1 cultured cells. The study's deeper metabolic and animal experiments focused on SHLP-2 and SHLP-3; their findings cannot be assigned to SHLP-6. The 22Rv1 cells were human-derived, but no person received SHLP-6 in that experiment. A pro-apoptotic cell-line result alone does not establish senolytic action, cancer treatment or longevity benefit. [Cobb et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27070352/)

## How to interpret the difference

The cited papers do not directly compare FOXO4-DRI with SHLP-6 or test a combination. They do not establish that the compounds are compatible or synergistic. Model context also matters for FOXO4-DRI: a pulmonary-hypertension mouse study reported worse pulmonary measures after senescent-cell removal, including a FOXO4-DRI comparison in one mouse model. This does not quantify a human risk. [Born et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36515093/)

Evidence for **human longevity outcomes is very low for each peptide**. This comparison describes research and is not medical advice or guidance for self-treatment.
