## Overview

[AOD-9604](/peptides/aod-9604) (also written AOD9604) is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the C-terminal fragment of human growth hormone from amino acids 177-191 with an additional N-terminal tyrosine [aod-9604-identity-pmid-25208511]. [CagriSema](/peptides/cagrisema) is coadministered cagrilintide plus semaglutide. The AOD-9604 rodent papers and early-2000s development notes fetched this run and the REDEFINE Phase 3a trials are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs AOD-9604](/compare/amycretin-vs-aod-9604), [5-Amino-1MQ vs AOD-9604](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-aod-9604), [5-Amino-1MQ vs CagriSema](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-cagrisema), and [CagriSema vs semaglutide](/compare/cagrisema-vs-semaglutide).

PMID 15134286 title-matched as a 2004 development profile. It states Metabolic was developing AOD-9604 for the potential treatment of obesity and that by February 2002 phase IIa trials were underway. The abstract publishes **no enrollment and no percent** [aod-9604-profile-pmid-15134286]. That is not a human RCT result and is not an obesity Phase 3.

PMID 11146367 title-matched as a rat paper. Daily oral AOD9604 for 19 days in obese Zucker rats reduced body-weight gain to 15.8 ± 0.6 g versus 35.6 ± 0.8 g in controls. That is rodent weight-gain in grams, not a human RCT percent. The treatment-amount line from the abstract is not copied here [aod-9604-rat-pmid-11146367].

PMID 11673763 names AOD9604 in the abstract as a C-terminal fragment of hGH. In obese mice treated for 14 days, both hGH and AOD9604 reduced body-weight gain and increased fat oxidation. The abstract does **not** publish a body-weight percent. AOD9604 did not compete for the hGH receptor [aod-9604-mice-pmid-11673763].

PMID 16625817 is a 2006 review of then-in-development obesity drugs. It lists a human growth hormone fragment (AOD-9604) among that set. Of those compounds, only rimonabant had completed phase III. That sentence is not an AOD-9604 Phase 3 result [aod-9604-review-pmid-16625817].

PMID 25208511 title-matched as an identity and doping-detection paper. It describes AOD9604 as the C-terminal hGH fragment 177-191 plus an N-terminal tyrosine. It publishes no efficacy percent [aod-9604-identity-pmid-25208511].

### CagriSema — REDEFINE 1 (without diabetes)

PMID 40544433, NCT05567796. Phase 3a, 68-week, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled and active-controlled trial. Adults without diabetes; BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with at least one obesity-related complication. n=3417 randomly assigned (2108 cagrilintide-semaglutide; 302 semaglutide 2.4 mg; 302 cagrilintide 2.4 mg; 705 placebo), plus lifestyle intervention. Effect estimates used the treatment-policy estimand (intention-to-treat). The enrollmentInfo field was absent from the ClinicalTrials.gov payload returned this run; n=3417 is from the journal abstract.

Treatment-policy mean percent change at week 68: **−20.4%** with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus **−3.0%** placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). The abstract does not publish the cagrilintide-alone or semaglutide-alone percents. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05567796 as ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING Phase 3 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02; lead sponsor Novo Nordisk A/S). This page quotes the NEJM abstract, not a registry results module. A per-alias PubMed search for "CagriSema" on 2026-09-02 returned 70 records. This page quotes REDEFINE 1 and REDEFINE 2 and does not dump that list.

A PubMed search for NCT05567796 on 2026-09-02 also returned PMID 41328546 (REDEFINE 1 blood-pressure analysis). That paper is not quoted as a weight-change result.

### CagriSema — REDEFINE 2 (type 2 diabetes)

PMID 40544432, NCT05394519. Phase 3a, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 12 countries. Adults with BMI of 27 or more, glycated hemoglobin 7 to 10%, and type 2 diabetes. n=1206 randomly assigned (904 cagrilintide-semaglutide 2.4 mg each; 302 placebo) for 68 weeks plus lifestyle intervention. Treatment-policy estimand.

Treatment-policy mean change at week 68: **−13.7%** versus **−3.4%** placebo (estimated difference −10.4 percentage points; 95% CI −11.2 to −9.5; P&lt;0.001). Glycated hemoglobin of 6.5% or less: 73.5% versus 15.9%. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 72.5% versus 34.4% [redefine-2-pmid-40544432].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05394519 as COMPLETED Phase 3 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02; lead sponsor Novo Nordisk A/S). This page does not invent a trial-product percent for REDEFINE 2.
