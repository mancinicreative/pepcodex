## What the term means

A contraindication identifies a reason a particular treatment could be harmful in a specified situation. The [NCI definition](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/contraindication) concerns that treatment and circumstance; it does not quantify the probability of harm.

[MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002314.htm) distinguishes two terms:

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Absolute contraindication** | A restriction associated with potentially life-threatening harm. |
| **Relative contraindication** | A circumstance requiring caution, where a favorable balance of benefit and risk may justify use. |

These definitions explain terminology; they do not determine whether a medicine is appropriate for an individual.

## Contraindications and warnings are different label sections

FDA prescribing information separates contraindications from warnings and precautions. A warning should not automatically be renamed a relative contraindication. FDA guidance also allows a contraindication to reflect an anticipated risk supported by evidence; it need not mean that the harm has already been observed in humans. See the [FDA labeling guidance, sections II and III](https://www.fda.gov/media/71866/download).

## Why the specific product matters

The following examples describe the cited US labels, checked on September 5, 2026. They are not a complete safety comparison.

| Product | What section 4 lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Wegovy (semaglutide), injection and tablets** | Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), MEN 2, and a previous serious hypersensitivity reaction involving semaglutide or product ingredients. [Label, section 4](https://www.novo-pi.com/wegovy.pdf) |
| **Byetta (exenatide), injection** | Prior severe hypersensitivity to exenatide or product ingredients, and a history of exenatide-induced immune-mediated thrombocytopenia. MTC/MEN 2 is not listed in this section. [Label, section 4](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=53d03c03-ebf7-418d-88a8-533eabd2ee4f) |

The Wegovy warning describes thyroid C-cell tumors in rodents and uncertainty about human relevance. It does not establish tumor recurrence in humans. [Label, boxed warning and section 5.1](https://www.novo-pi.com/wegovy.pdf)

The difference between these labels does not establish that either medicine is suitable for a particular person.

## Pregnancy wording also depends on indication

Wegovy discusses pregnancy in section 8, separately from contraindications. Its June 2026 highlights distinguish weight and cardiovascular indications from MASH; the MASH indication applies to injection. [Label, highlights and section 1](https://www.novo-pi.com/wegovy.pdf)
