## What the evidence actually compares

Both mazdutide and survodutide activate GLP-1 and glucagon receptors, but they are distinct molecules with separate dosing regimens and trial programs. Shared targets do not imply interchangeable efficacy or safety.

The published SYNCHRONIZE-1 phase 3 trial studied survodutide versus placebo in adults with obesity or overweight and a complication, excluding diabetes. Its week-76 weight results used a treatment-regimen estimand. A separate SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD study measured liver-fat and weight outcomes; MRI liver-fat changes are not biopsy-proven steatohepatitis resolution. [SYNCHRONIZE-1, PMID 42253238](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42253238/) · [SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD, PMID 42252333](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42252333/)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

Mazdutide GLORY-2 enrolled Chinese adults with obesity, including some with type 2 diabetes; SYNCHRONIZE-1 excluded diabetes and had different follow-up and estimand. Their placebo-controlled results cannot establish which dual agonist is superior. No cited randomized head-to-head trial resolves that question.

Survodutide remained investigational in the reviewed sources; no FDA approval for either agent was verified. Innovent separately reports Chinese approvals of mazdutide for specified adult uses. The source for the Survodutide evidence above is [this primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42253238/); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.
