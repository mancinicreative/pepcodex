## Distinct preclinical questions

MOTS-c is encoded in mitochondrial 12S rRNA and has metabolic model findings. SHLP-6 is a small humanin-like peptide from the 16S region. Its sequence and formulation require care: a 2023 genetics analysis predicted a canonical human 20-residue sequence, while older page metadata called it 22 residues. [MOTS-c discovery, PMID 25738459](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25738459/) · [SHLP-6 genetics, PMID 37675281](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37675281/)

| Evidence question | MOTS-c | SHLP-6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human observations | Endogenous exercise, diabetes and sleep-apnea measurements | Exercise-associated circulating levels and placental transcripts |
| Direct intervention | Mouse/cell research; native Phase 2 registration without outcome | Cell and zebrafish experiments, preliminary mouse abstract; no cited human administration |
| Therapeutic-claim grade | **Low** | **Very low** |

MOTS-c mouse experiments have tested glucose-related and age-related physical measures. A 2026 C26 mouse/cell cachexia paper found partial skeletal-muscle preservation with preventive exposure but no preservation of total body or fat mass. Human observations of endogenous MOTS-c, including a 2026 study with no arterio-venous exercise difference, are not administered-peptide effects. [PMID 33473109](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33473109/) · [PMID 41520850](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41520850/) · [PMID 42266945](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42266945/)

The original SHLP-6 paper reported DNA-fragmentation apoptosis in cultured NIT-1 and 22Rv1 cells; it did not show selective cancer-cell or senescent-cell removal. A later study found FPR1/FPR2 signaling in engineered cells, without showing that this drives the apoptosis result. Zebrafish oxidative-stress experiments test still different endpoints. [PMID 27070352](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27070352/) · [PMID 39294930](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39294930/) · [PMID 40292418](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40292418/) · [PMID 40915070](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40915070/)

One human exercise study measured endogenous SHLP-6 in ten men; a placental study measured **SHLP-6 mRNA**, not administered peptide or SHLP-6 protein in that cohort. [PMID 32271093](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32271093/) · [PMID 42453115](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42453115/)

[NCT07505745](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07505745) registers native MOTS-c Phase 2 intervention without a cited outcome; [NCT03998514](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03998514) studied the distinct CB4211 analog. Neither establishes a SHLP-6 combination or human safety. [FDA compounding safety review](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks)

This page is an evidence summary, not a therapeutic recommendation.
