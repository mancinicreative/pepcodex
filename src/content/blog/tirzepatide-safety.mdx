Gastrointestinal frequencies, the thyroid C-cell boxed warning, and the Mounjaro-versus-Zepbound population notes live on [tirzepatide safety](/safety/tirzepatide-safety). The compound record is [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide). This post does not recap that table.

## What this page is for

Tirzepatide is one molecule on two labelled products. SURPASS and SURMOUNT are the trial programmes those labels draw on [pmid-35133415] [pmid-35658024] [pmid-38078870]. The safety page is where frequencies and the labelled split belong:

- **Shared:** class boxed warning (rodent thyroid C-cell tumors); gastrointestinal events as the common finding; pancreatitis and gallbladder disease as labelled serious risks
- **Not shared:** indicated use, and several Use-in-Specific-Populations paragraphs (pediatric data is established for Mounjaro's diabetes indication and is not established for Zepbound)

Read the product the prescription names. Do not copy a Zepbound paragraph onto Mounjaro, or the reverse.
