## What the evidence actually compares

CagriSema is coadministered cagrilintide (an amylin analogue) and semaglutide (a GLP-1 agonist). It is a two-drug combination, not one new dual-agonist molecule. Mazdutide is one GLP-1/glucagon dual agonist.

The published phase 3 REDEFINE 1 trial randomized adults with overweight or obesity to the combination, its components or placebo and evaluated weight at 68 weeks. REIMAGINE trials study type 2 diabetes populations and different endpoints. These combination-specific results must not be imported into the monotherapy evidence for cagrilintide or semaglutide. [REDEFINE 1, PMID 40544433](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40544433/) · [REIMAGINE 2, PMID 42251859](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251859/)

Mazdutide is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Its published GLORY-1 and GLORY-2 weight trials enrolled Chinese adults; GLORY-2 included some adults with type 2 diabetes, whereas the separate US phase 2 trial enrolled adults without it. The trials used different follow-up and analysis methods. [GLORY-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approvals for specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes uses. The reviewed source is a [sponsor exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), not an independently retrieved Chinese label. No US FDA mazdutide approval was verified; an [FDA letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026) identifies one US seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs.

## What cannot be inferred

Mazdutide's GLORY weight studies were conducted mainly in Chinese adults with different entry criteria and endpoint timing. REDEFINE 1 is not a direct mazdutide comparator, so a trial-percentage ranking would be unsupported.

[Novo Nordisk reports](https://www.novonordisk.com/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=916470) filing a US obesity application for CagriSema in December 2025. Filing is not approval; no FDA approval decision was verified. No FDA mazdutide approval was verified. Innovent separately reports specified Chinese mazdutide approvals. The combination efficacy evidence above is [REDEFINE 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40544433/); regulatory claims about mazdutide are supported by the linked sponsor filing and FDA letter. No combination-use claim follows from these separate studies.
