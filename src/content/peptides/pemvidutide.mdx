**Key Research:** The published IMPACT phase 2b trial reported more MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis at 24 weeks, but did not meet its separate conventional-histology fibrosis co-primary endpoint ([PMID:41237796](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41237796/)). MOMENTUM obesity results are sponsor-reported and identified by its [trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05295875); the registration is not a peer-reviewed results paper.

### MASH and alcohol use disorder updates

In the published IMPACT Phase 2b trial of 212 adults with biopsy-confirmed MASH and F2–F3 fibrosis, MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis at 24 weeks was more frequent with pemvidutide than placebo. The separate co-primary endpoint of fibrosis improvement without worsening of MASH was **not statistically significant** in the conventional histology analysis ([PMID:41237796](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41237796/)). [Altimmune's 48-week update](https://ir.altimmune.com/node/17411) described changes in non-invasive fibrosis markers and further weight reduction, but these are sponsor toplines and do not turn the missed 24-week biopsy endpoint into a positive one. Altimmune says the PERFORMA Phase 3 MASH trial began in July 2026 ([August 2026 corporate update](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and)); trial registration is a study plan, not an efficacy result.

In RECLAIM (NCT06987513), a separate Phase 2 trial in about 100 adults with moderate-to-severe alcohol use disorder, [Altimmune reported on 28 July 2026](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-positive-topline-results-reclaim-phase-2) a week-24 mean reduction of 4.20 heavy drinking days per week from baseline with pemvidutide versus 2.75 with placebo (difference 1.45 days, p=0.0014). The company also reported a two-level WHO risk-drinking reduction in 64.4% versus 34.8% and zero heavy drinking days during weeks 21–24 in 42.2% versus 17.4%. These are **sponsor-reported toplines**, with no peer-reviewed RECLAIM paper or posted registry results table verified here. FDA Fast Track for AUD and Breakthrough Therapy for MASH are development designations, not approval or an agency determination of efficacy.

### Important Limitations

- Phase 3 PERFORMA (NCT07795164) has begun, but there is no Phase 3 efficacy result
- RECLAIM AUD results are sponsor toplines pending detailed peer review and registry posting
- A missing ClinicalTrials.gov results table does not negate the sponsor's RECLAIM topline, but limits independent detail
- RESTORE ALD (NCT07009860) active, not recruiting — no results
- No head-to-head comparisons with approved agents
- Long-term safety and durability beyond the published Phase 2 window unknown
- Registrational and Phase 2 programs remain Altimmune-sponsored
