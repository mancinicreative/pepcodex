## Overview

[Wegovy](/peptides/semaglutide) is a semaglutide brand. [Zepbound](/peptides/tirzepatide) is a tirzepatide brand. Both products named here are FDA-approved prescription medicines. This page quotes STEP 1 and SURMOUNT-1 as separate placebo-controlled obesity trials, then SURMOUNT-5 as the head-to-head. It does not collapse those programmes into one invented percent range.

## Parallel obesity trials (not a head-to-head)

STEP 1 and SURMOUNT-1 used different drugs, durations, and randomisation schemes. Do not subtract one trial’s percent from the other and call that a head-to-head.

### STEP 1 (Wegovy / semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961 adults without diabetes; 68 weeks. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions. Mean body-weight change −14.9% with once-weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg vs −2.4% with placebo (ETD −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). ≥5% 86.4% vs 31.5%; ≥10% 69.1% vs 12.0%; ≥15% 50.5% vs 4.9%. The STEP 1 abstract fetched 2026-09-02 does not report a ≥20% rate. GI events led to discontinuation in 4.5% vs 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

### SURMOUNT-1 (Zepbound / tirzepatide vs placebo)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2539 adults without diabetes; 72 weeks. **Treatment-regimen estimand.** Mean percent change at week 72: 5 mg −15.0%, 10 mg −19.5%, 15 mg −20.9%, placebo −3.1% (P&lt;0.001 for all vs placebo). ≥5% at 5/10/15 mg 85% / 89% / 91% vs 35% placebo. ≥20% at 15 mg **57%** (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5 / 10 / 15 mg / placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## SURMOUNT-5 (head-to-head)

PMID 40353578, NCT05822830. Phase 3b, open-label; n=751 adults with obesity and without type 2 diabetes; 72 weeks. Maximum tolerated dose of tirzepatide (10 mg or 15 mg) vs maximum tolerated dose of semaglutide (1.7 mg or 2.4 mg). Least-squares mean percent weight change −20.2% (95% CI −21.4 to −19.1) with tirzepatide vs −13.7% (95% CI −14.9 to −12.6) with semaglutide (P&lt;0.001). Waist circumference −18.4 cm vs −13.0 cm. The abstract states participants on tirzepatide were more likely to reach ≥10%, ≥15%, ≥20%, and ≥25% reductions; it does **not** publish those category percents. The abstract does not label treatment-regimen vs efficacy. GI events were the most common adverse events in both groups [surmount-5-pmid-40353578].

## Cardiovascular evidence (SELECT)

Do not headline a rounded relative-risk percent without the hazard ratio.

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, overweight or obesity, no diabetes; once-weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg vs placebo. Primary MACE 569/8803 (6.5%) vs 701/8801 (8.0%); HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90); P&lt;0.001. Mean follow-up 39.8 months. Permanent discontinuation for adverse events 16.6% vs 8.2% [select-pmid-37952131].
