**Correction notice:** An earlier version of this briefing reported topline TRIUMPH-1 phase 3 results — including 28.3% mean weight loss at the highest maintenance level, threshold-response rates, adverse-event percentages, and discontinuation rates. Those figures could not be traced to any published trial report or registry results posting, and they have been removed. As of 17 August 2026, no TRIUMPH efficacy or safety results appear in PubMed or on ClinicalTrials.gov. This briefing now describes the verified state of the retatrutide evidence.

## What TRIUMPH Is

[Retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide) is Eli Lilly's investigational triple agonist, targeting the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Its phase 3 registrational program, TRIUMPH, consists of four multicenter, randomized, double-blind trials enrolling over 5,800 participants, evaluating weekly subcutaneous retatrutide against placebo for obesity and two related complications — obstructive sleep apnea and knee osteoarthritis. Two weight-management trials (TRIUMPH-1 and TRIUMPH-2) carry nested OSA and OA protocols, TRIUMPH-3 studies a population with established cardiovascular disease, and TRIUMPH-4 is a stand-alone knee osteoarthritis trial [triumph-program-design]. TRIUMPH-1 is registered as NCT05929066 [triumph-1-registration].

## What Is Published, as of 17 August 2026

**No TRIUMPH results.** A search of PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov on 17 August 2026 returns no TRIUMPH efficacy or safety results — only the program design paper, the trial registrations, and adjacent methodology records. Weight-loss percentages circulating for TRIUMPH-1 cannot be traced to a primary publication or a registry results posting, and this site does not report them.

**Phase 2 obesity data — the verified benchmark.** The published obesity evidence for retatrutide is the phase 2 trial in the New England Journal of Medicine: 24.2% mean weight loss at 48 weeks at the 12 mg maintenance level, with 93% of participants achieving at least 10% weight loss and 83% achieving at least 15%. Gastrointestinal adverse events were dose-related and the most common [nejm-retatrutide-phase2].

**One published phase 3 — in type 2 diabetes.** TRANSCEND-T2D-1, published in The Lancet on 6 June 2026, is the first phase 3 trial of retatrutide to report. In 537 adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled by diet and exercise, HbA1c fell 1.69, 1.86, and 1.94 percentage points at the 4, 9, and 12 mg levels versus 0.81 with placebo at 40 weeks, on the treatment-regimen estimand — the analysis that keeps every randomized participant in. Body weight, a key secondary endpoint, fell 11.5%, 13.9%, and 15.3% across the three levels versus 2.6% with placebo [transcend-t2d-1]. This trial sits outside the TRIUMPH basket, and a diabetes population selected for glycemic control does not answer the obesity question TRIUMPH was built to answer.

## The Triple-Agonist Mechanism

Retatrutide's mechanism involves simultaneous activation of three metabolic receptors [nejm-retatrutide-phase2]:

**GLP-1 receptor:** enhances glucose-dependent insulin secretion, reduces appetite, and slows gastric emptying.

**GIP receptor:** augments GLP-1 effects on insulin secretion and may enhance central appetite suppression.

**Glucagon receptor:** increases energy expenditure through hepatic effects and thermogenesis — the key differentiator from [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), which activates only the GLP-1 and GIP receptors, and from [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide), a GLP-1-only agonist.

## What Comes Next

The TRIUMPH obesity trials listed as completed on ClinicalTrials.gov have not posted results, and Eli Lilly has not published a confirmed regulatory submission date. Until a TRIUMPH trial appears in a peer-reviewed journal or posts registry results, the phase 2 NEJM data and TRANSCEND-T2D-1 are the retatrutide efficacy record. For the knee osteoarthritis arm of the program, see [the TRIUMPH-4 status review](/blog/retatrutide-triumph-4-weight-loss-pain-relief); for the planned head-to-head against tirzepatide, see [the direct-comparison review](/blog/retatrutide-direct-comparison).
