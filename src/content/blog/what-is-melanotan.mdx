## Melanotan I vs II: Which Page Owns the Record

The ranking definition is the [Melanotan guide](/guide/what-is-melanotan) — that URL already earns PAGE impressions. The I-series dossier is [melanotan-I](/peptides/melanotan-i) (the URL slug is not the display name). This page yields the "what is it" job and only maps I vs II vs PT-141.

Do not treat "Melanotan" as one compound. Melanotan I and Melanotan II are different records. Unapproved tanning peptides are not Vyleesi.

## Key Facts

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **FDA Approved** | NO |
| **Legal to Sell** | NO (in most countries) |
| **Safety Established** | NO |
| **WADA Status** | Prohibited |

## Why Do People Use It?

People seek Melanotan II for:
- Tanning without sun exposure
- Perceived protection from UV damage

## Why Is It Dangerous?

### Regulatory Warnings Have Been Issued Because:
- **Mole changes** — can darken existing moles
- **New mole formation** — may develop new moles
- **Melanoma concerns** — theoretical cancer risk
- **Cardiovascular effects** — blood pressure changes
- **Unknown long-term effects** — no proper studies

## The Difference from PT-141

[PT-141](/peptides/pt-141) (Vyleesi) was developed from Melanotan II but:
- Is selective for one receptor
- Has minimal tanning effect
- Went through proper clinical trials
- Received FDA approval

The I-series record is a separate dossier: [melanotan-I](/peptides/melanotan-i).

## Important Warning

Melanotan II is:
- Sold illegally online
- Unregulated (unknown purity)
- Associated with serious adverse events
- Explicitly warned against by health authorities
