## Overview

FOXO4-DRI and SHLP-6 are discussed here as research compounds.

The FOXO4-DRI inventory reviewed on September 5, 2026 contains 13 unique research/review publications, assigned once to a primary category: one patient-observation paper, 11 preclinical papers and one review. A linked correction notice is disclosed separately and excluded from that count. The patient-observation paper also includes cell experiments; it does not report giving FOXO4-DRI to people. Laboratory human tissue stays in the preclinical category. These counts describe publications, not independent trials or comparative efficacy. This dated update covers FOXO4-DRI; the neighboring compound's retained claims were not reverified in this review. [PMID:42391447](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42391447/)

**FOXO4-DRI:** The original Cell study described a 46-residue D-retro-inverso construct with a reported molecular weight of 5,358.2 Da. It demonstrated FOXO4-p53 binding interference and selective activity in particular cell and mouse experiments; these results do not establish universal selectivity or human benefit. [PMID:28340339](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28340339/) [PMID:40593617](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40593617/)

**SHLP-6:** A 22-amino acid mitochondria-derived peptide with the unique property of being pro-apoptotic—the opposite of its sister peptides humanin and SHLP-2.

## FOXO4-DRI evidence limits

Senescent-cell removal did not improve chondrogenic potential in cultured human chondrocytes. A separate study reported unfavorable pulmonary vascular changes in mouse models exposed to FOXO4-DRI; results from its ABT263 rat experiment should not be attributed to FOXO4-DRI. These findings show why a common link to apoptosis cannot establish comparative benefit or combination safety. [PMID:33996787](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33996787/) [PMID:36515093](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36515093/)

The reviewed FOXO4-DRI publications do not establish safety or efficacy of a combination with SHLP-6. Separate mechanisms do not demonstrate compatibility or synergy.

## FOXO4-DRI source-quality note

The inventory includes a 2022 bleomycin-induced lung-fibrosis paper with a 2024 correction. The correction reports that a Figure 6B FACS panel for PBS + FOXO4-DRI was misplaced in the TGF-beta + FOXO4-DRI group. The authors state that their conclusion is unchanged; this review does not independently validate the corrected results. The notice is excluded from the 13 research/review works. [PMID:35510614](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35510614/) [Correction, DOI:10.1111/jcmm.18502](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcmm.18502) [PMID:39166551](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39166551/)
