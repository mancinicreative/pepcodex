## Overview

[Cagrilintide](/peptides/cagrilintide) is Novo Nordisk's investigational long-acting amylin analogue. [Semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) is an approved GLP-1 receptor agonist. This page quotes Lau Phase 2 for cagrilintide monotherapy, STEP 1 for semaglutide 2.4 mg, and REDEFINE 1 for coadministered CagriSema versus placebo. Those are different designs, durations, and estimands. Do not divide one trial's percent by another.

## Mechanism

Cagrilintide is described in Lau 2021 as a long-acting amylin analogue [lau-pmid-34798060]. Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist in STEP 1 and SELECT [step-1-pmid-33567185] [select-pmid-37952131]. This page does not quote half-life hours.

## Cagrilintide monotherapy (Lau Phase 2)

PMID 34798060, NCT03856047. n=706 randomly assigned; 26-week treatment. Adults without diabetes, BMI at least 30 kg/m² or at least 27 kg/m² with hypertension or dyslipidaemia. Doses 0.3, 0.6, 1.2, 2.4, or 4.5 mg once weekly, plus a liraglutide 3.0 mg active-control arm [lau-pmid-34798060].

The abstract publishes the **trial-product** estimand (assumes adherence) as the numbered range: mean percentage weight reductions 6.0%–10.8% with cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg versus 3.0% with placebo (estimated treatment difference 3.0%–7.8%; P&lt;0.001). Cagrilintide 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0% (estimated treatment difference 1.8%; P=0.03). The abstract says **similar** reductions were observed with the **treatment-policy** estimand and does **not** publish a second percent table. This page does not invent a per-dose row that the abstract does not give [lau-pmid-34798060].

Gastrointestinal adverse events 41%–63% versus 32% placebo; nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [lau-pmid-34798060].

## Semaglutide (STEP 1)

PMID 33567185, NCT03548935. n=1961; 68 weeks; no diabetes. Once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg versus placebo, plus lifestyle intervention. The primary estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue interventions (**treatment-regimen**) [step-1-pmid-33567185].

Mean change in body weight −14.9% versus −2.4% placebo (estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points; 95% CI −13.4 to −11.5; P&lt;0.001). Weight reductions of 10% or more: 69.1% versus 12.0%. Of 15% or more: 50.5% versus 4.9% (P&lt;0.001 for those odds comparisons). Gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation in 4.5% versus 0.8% [step-1-pmid-33567185].

STEP 1 is not a cagrilintide trial. Do not treat 68-week STEP 1 and 26-week Lau Phase 2 as one head-to-head.

## CagriSema (REDEFINE 1)

PMID 40544433 names both cagrilintide and semaglutide. NCT05567796. Phase 3a, 68 weeks, n=3417 without diabetes. Randomization 21:3:3:7 to cagrilintide 2.4 mg plus semaglutide 2.4 mg, semaglutide 2.4 mg alone, cagrilintide 2.4 mg alone, or placebo. Effect estimates used the **treatment-policy** estimand [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

Estimated mean percent change in body weight at week 68: **−20.4%** with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus **−3.0%** with placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). Gastrointestinal adverse events 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

The abstract assigns 302 participants to semaglutide alone and 302 to cagrilintide alone. It does **not** publish those arms' percent weight change. This page does not invent them.

## Cardiovascular evidence (semaglutide)

Cagrilintide Phase 2 and REDEFINE 1 as fetched here are weight trials, not cardiovascular-outcomes trials.

**SELECT** (PMID 37952131, NCT03574597): n=17,604; preexisting cardiovascular disease, overweight or obesity, no diabetes. Primary MACE 569/8803 (6.5%) versus 701/8801 (8.0%); HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90; P&lt;0.001). Mean follow-up 39.8 months [select-pmid-37952131].

### Key takeaways

1. **Not one head-to-head.** Lau and STEP 1 differ in duration and estimand. REDEFINE 1's abstract does not publish the monotherapy-arm percents.
2. **Combination result on this page is REDEFINE 1 vs placebo**, not an unpublished CagriSema-versus-semaglutide percent from that abstract.
3. **REDEFINE 4** is CagriSema versus tirzepatide. Design only until a results record or paper is posted.
4. **SELECT** is the cardiovascular paper quoted here for semaglutide. Quote the hazard ratio, not a rounded relative-risk headline.
