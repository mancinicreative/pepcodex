Mazdutide is a dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist with robust Phase 3 clinical trial data. Its mechanisms are well-characterized in human studies.

### GLORY-2 — the 9 mg strength (2026)

GLORY-2, published in JAMA on 4 August 2026, tested the higher 9 mg strength that Innovent had submitted to the NMPA as a supplementary application ([PMID:42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/), NCT06164873). It was a double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial at 27 Chinese hospitals, running from December 2023 to November 2025, and it randomised 461 adults with obesity 2:1 to once-weekly mazdutide 9 mg or placebo for 60 weeks alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity. The participants were young — mean age 33.9 years — and 16.1% had type 2 diabetes.

Both co-primary outcomes favoured mazdutide. Mean body-weight change at week 60 was -16.65% (95% CI -18.19 to -15.12) against -1.50% (95% CI -3.43 to 0.43) on placebo, a between-group difference of -15.15% (95% CI -17.22 to -13.09). At least 5% weight reduction was reached by 84.3% against 33.1%.

The tolerability figures deserve equal weight. Vomiting was reported by 53.1% of the mazdutide group against 1.3% on placebo, nausea by 46.9% against 3.2%, and diarrhoea by 39.4% against 6.5%. Most events were mild to moderate, and 2.9% of the mazdutide group discontinued treatment because of an adverse event against none on placebo.

This trial was conducted entirely in China, in a population younger and lighter than the typical Western obesity trial cohort, which limits how far the result generalises.

### US Phase 2 (Hsia) — efficacy estimand only

Hsia et al. ([PMID:42628555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/), NCT06124807) is a US multicentre randomised placebo-controlled Phase 2 trial in 179 adults with obesity or overweight without type 2 diabetes (48 weeks). The paper names the primary analysis as percentage body-weight change at 32 weeks on the **efficacy (hypothetical) estimand**. The abstract does not report a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy figure, so none is stated here.

On that efficacy estimand, least-squares mean change was −7.3% (3-6 mg), −15.6% (10 mg) and −18.1% (16 mg) versus −0.9% with placebo; estimated treatment differences versus placebo ranged from −6.5% to −17.2%. Additional reductions at 48 weeks were noted without abstract numbers. Discontinuation due to adverse events was 20% at the highest strength, mainly gastrointestinal. This US Phase 2 result is not interchangeable with the China Phase 3 GLORY figures, and it is not a head-to-head against [semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide) or [survodutide](/peptides/survodutide).

### Important Limitations

- Approval is limited to China, per Innovent's own announcements (NMPA); not FDA- or EMA-approved
- Both published phase 3 efficacy trials (GLORY-1, GLORY-2) enrolled only Chinese adults, and every mazdutide phase 3 registration found on ClinicalTrials.gov on 17 August 2026 recruits in China; no phase 3 result in another population was located. The US Hsia Phase 2 result is on the efficacy (hypothetical) estimand only
- Long-term safety data beyond 60 weeks is limited
- Head-to-head data vs tirzepatide not yet available
- Optimal patient selection criteria still being refined
- GI tolerability requires careful dose titration
