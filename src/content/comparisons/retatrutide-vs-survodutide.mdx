## Overview

Retatrutide is a triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist (Eli Lilly). Survodutide is a dual glucagon/GLP-1 receptor agonist (Boehringer Ingelheim). The retatrutide phase 2 obesity trial, SYNCHRONIZE-1, the survodutide phase 2 obesity dose-finding trial, the phase 2 MASH biopsy trial, and SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD are separate programmes. This page does not collapse a 48-week phase 2 secondary into a 76-week phase 3 treatment-regimen figure.

Dossiers: [retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide), [survodutide](/peptides/survodutide). Related compares: [tirzepatide vs retatrutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-retatrutide), [survodutide vs tirzepatide](/compare/survodutide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, populations, and endpoints differ.

### Retatrutide phase 2 obesity (not TRIUMPH)

PMID 37366315, NCT04881760. Phase 2; n=338; 48 weeks. Primary end point: percentage change in body weight at **24 weeks**. Week 48 change is a secondary end point. The abstract reports least-squares means and does not name a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy estimand.

Week 24: −7.2% (1 mg), −12.9% (combined 4 mg), −17.3% (combined 8 mg), −17.5% (12 mg), −1.6% placebo.

Week 48: −8.7% (1 mg), −17.1% (combined 4 mg), −22.8% (combined 8 mg), −24.2% (12 mg), −2.1% placebo.

At week 48, a reduction of 5% / 10% / 15% or more occurred in 100% / 93% / 83% on 12 mg vs 27% / 9% / 2% on placebo. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events, dose-related, and mostly mild to moderate [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

This page does not restore a 4 mg 48-week −17.5% row. −17.5% in the abstract is the 12 mg week-24 figure.

### TRIUMPH (design only)

PMID 41090431 describes four phase 3 studies (TRIUMPH-1 through TRIUMPH-4) in over 5,800 participants. The primary weight-management end point is percent change in body weight. The abstract publishes no efficacy percent. A PubMed search on 2026-09-02 for `TRIUMPH-1[Title] AND retatrutide` returned 0 records; `TRIUMPH[Title] AND retatrutide` returned this design paper and a 2024 correspondence. This page does not quote an unpublished TRIUMPH obesity percent [triumph-design-pmid-41090431].

### SYNCHRONIZE-1 (survodutide obesity, no diabetes)

PMID 42253238, NCT06066515. Phase 3, n=725 (241 on 3.6 mg, 242 on 6.0 mg, 242 on placebo), 76 weeks. Primary analysis is the treatment-regimen estimand. Mean body-weight change: −12.2% (95% CI −13.6 to −10.8) with 3.6 mg, −13.0% (95% CI −14.4 to −11.6) with 6.0 mg, −5.4% (95% CI −6.9 to −4.0) with placebo. At least 5% weight reduction: 72.6%, 71.9%, and 46.3% (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Gastrointestinal symptoms: 80.9%, 89.7%, and 47.9%. No deaths reported [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].

ClinicalTrials.gov for NCT06066515 on 2026-09-02: COMPLETED; enrollment 726; `hasResults` false. The published paper is the source for the percents above.

### Phase 2 obesity dose-finding (not SYNCHRONIZE-1)

PMID 38330987, NCT04667377. 387 enrolled; 386 treated; **46 weeks** (not a 48-week table). Planned-treatment mean body-weight change: −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), −14.9% (4.8 mg), −2.8% placebo. Gastrointestinal adverse events in 75% of survodutide recipients vs 42% on placebo [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].

## Liver-disease programmes

### Survodutide phase 2 MASH (biopsy)

PMID 38847460, NCT04771273. Phase 2; 48 weeks; 293 treated; biopsy-confirmed MASH with fibrosis F1–F3. Primary end point: histologic improvement in MASH with no worsening of fibrosis. 2.4 mg 47%, 4.8 mg 62%, 6.0 mg 43%, placebo 14% (P&lt;0.001 for the quadratic dose-response curve). Decrease in liver fat of at least 30%: 63% / 67% / 57% vs 14%. Fibrosis improvement of at least one stage: 34% / 36% / 34% vs 22%. Nausea 66% vs 23%; diarrhea 49% vs 23%; vomiting 41% vs 4% [survodutide-mash-p2-pmid-38847460].

The abstract does not publish a single “highest-dose MASH resolution” percent that this page can headline. This increment’s PubMed search for NCT04771273 returned this trial and a later mediation analysis (PMID 42545725), not a different resolution table.

### SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD (phase 3, liver fat — not a MASH-resolution headline)

PMID 42252333, NCT06309992. n=216 with obesity and at-risk MASLD; 48 weeks; survodutide 6.0 mg vs placebo. ClinicalTrials.gov on 2026-09-02: COMPLETED; enrollment 218; `hasResults` false. The published paper is the source for the percents below. **Treatment-regimen** (leads): at least 30% reduction in MRI-PDFF liver fat 68.5% vs 28.6% (P&lt;0.0001); mean body-weight change −8.7% vs −1.4% (P&lt;0.0001). Efficacy estimand (labelled): 84.2% vs 24.3% for the liver-fat end point; −12.2% vs −1.0% for weight [synchronize-masld-pmid-42252333].

This is not a TRIUMPH paper and not a MASH histologic-resolution trial.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** 48-week phase 2 secondaries are not interchangeable with 76-week treatment-regimen figures.
2. **Retatrutide primary is 24 weeks:** 12 mg −17.5% vs −1.6%; 48-week 12 mg −24.2% vs −2.1% is secondary [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].
3. **Survodutide phase 3 obesity is SYNCHRONIZE-1:** treatment-regimen −12.2% and −13.0% vs −5.4% at 76 weeks [synchronize-1-pmid-42253238].
4. **Phase 2 obesity is 46 weeks:** planned-treatment −14.9% at 4.8 mg vs −2.8% placebo [survodutide-p2-pmid-38330987].
5. **MASH biopsy (phase 2):** improvement in MASH without worsening of fibrosis 47% / 62% / 43% vs 14% [survodutide-mash-p2-pmid-38847460]. SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD treatment-regimen liver-fat response is 68.5% vs 28.6% [synchronize-masld-pmid-42252333].
6. **TRIUMPH:** design only as of the 2026-09-02 PubMed search; no obesity percent quoted.
