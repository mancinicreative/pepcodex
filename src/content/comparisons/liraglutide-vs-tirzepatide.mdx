## Overview

Liraglutide and Tirzepatide represent two generations of incretin-based therapy. Liraglutide (Saxenda for obesity, Victoza for diabetes) is a first-generation daily GLP-1 agonist, while Tirzepatide (Zepbound for obesity, Mounjaro for diabetes) is a dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist. Both are FDA-approved for weight management and type 2 diabetes.

This comparison helps understand the evolution from single to dual agonist therapy and the clinical implications.

### Liraglutide Mechanism

1. **GLP-1 Receptor Activation**
   - Appetite suppression
   - Delayed gastric emptying
   - Glucose-dependent insulin secretion
   - Reduced glucagon

2. **Clinical Effects**
   - SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes: −8.4 kg vs −2.8 kg at 56 weeks [scale-pmid-26132939]
   - Glycemic effects are not quoted here from that obesity-trial abstract
   - Cardiovascular outcomes: LEADER [leader-pmid-27295427]

### Tirzepatide Mechanism

1. **GLP-1 + GIP Dual Agonism**
   - Combined receptor activation
   - Dual GIP and GLP-1 activity
   - Enhanced insulin response
   - Weight-change results from SURMOUNT-1 and SURMOUNT-2

2. **Clinical Effects**
   - SURMOUNT-1 15 mg: −20.9% vs −3.1% at 72 weeks (treatment-regimen) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]
   - SURMOUNT-2 (obesity + T2D) 15 mg: −14.7% vs −3.2% at 72 weeks (treatment-regimen) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275]

## Weight Loss Efficacy

These are separate programmes, not a head-to-head RCT.

### Liraglutide (SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes)

PMID 26132939 (n=3731, 56 weeks, no type 2 diabetes): mean change −8.4 kg vs −2.8 kg placebo (difference −5.6 kg; 95% CI −6.0 to −5.1). At least 5% weight loss: 63.2% vs 27.1%. More than 10% weight loss: 33.1% vs 10.6%. The abstract reports kilograms, not a percent mean change; this page does not convert kilograms into a mean percent [scale-pmid-26132939]. SCALE Diabetes and SCALE Maintenance rows previously listed here were not re-fetched this increment and were removed.

### Tirzepatide (SURMOUNT)

| Trial | Result |
|-------|--------|
| SURMOUNT-1 5 mg | −15.0% vs −3.1% placebo at 72 weeks (treatment-regimen) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024] |
| SURMOUNT-1 10 mg | −19.5% vs −3.1% placebo at 72 weeks [surmount-1-pmid-35658024] |
| SURMOUNT-1 15 mg | −20.9% vs −3.1% placebo at 72 weeks [surmount-1-pmid-35658024] |
| SURMOUNT-2 (T2D) 15 mg | −14.7% vs −3.2% placebo at 72 weeks (treatment-regimen) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275] |

SURMOUNT-1 ≥5% at 15 mg: 91% (95% CI 88–94) vs 35% placebo. ≥20% at 15 mg: 57% (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% placebo. The SURMOUNT-1 abstract does not publish a ≥10% percent; this page does not invent one [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

### Cross-trial, not head-to-head

Do not divide −20.9% by a SCALE kilogram change. Units, duration (56 vs 72 weeks), and populations differ.

## Diabetes and cardiovascular outcomes

This page does not quote a liraglutide or tirzepatide HbA1c delta: those percents were not in the SCALE, SURMOUNT-1, SURMOUNT-2, LEADER, or SURPASS-CVOT abstracts fetched 2026-09-02.

**LEADER** (PMID 27295427, NCT01179048): n=9340; median follow-up 3.8 years. Primary MACE 608/4668 (13.0%) vs 694/4672 (14.9%); HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.97); P&lt;0.001 for noninferiority, P=0.01 for superiority [leader-pmid-27295427].

**SURPASS-CVOT** (PMID 41406444, NCT04255433): modified ITT 6586 vs 6579 after 13,299 randomised (134 excluded). Primary 3-point MACE 801 (12.2%) vs 862 (13.1%) on dulaglutide; HR 0.92 (95.3% CI 0.83–1.01); P=0.003 for noninferiority, P=0.09 for superiority. Noninferior, not superior, to dulaglutide [surpass-cvot-pmid-41406444].

## Side Effect Profile

SCALE: most frequently reported adverse events with liraglutide were mild or moderate nausea and diarrhea; serious events 6.2% vs 5.0% placebo. No nausea percent in the abstract [scale-pmid-26132939].

LEADER: the most common adverse events leading to discontinuation of liraglutide were gastrointestinal; no percent in the abstract [leader-pmid-27295427].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; AE discontinuation 6.2% on 15 mg vs 2.6% placebo. No nausea/vomiting percents in the abstract [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

SURMOUNT-2: gastrointestinal events most frequent, mostly mild to moderate, few leading to discontinuation (&lt;5%) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275].

This page does not invent a matched nausea-percent table.

## Administration

SCALE used once-daily subcutaneous liraglutide. SURMOUNT-1 used once-weekly subcutaneous tirzepatide. This page does not convert those trial schedules into injections per month [scale-pmid-26132939] [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial only:** SCALE −8.4 kg vs −2.8 kg at 56 weeks [scale-pmid-26132939]. SURMOUNT-1 15 mg −20.9% vs −3.1% at 72 weeks [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. This page does not compute a relative-efficacy ratio.
2. **Different mechanisms:** GLP-1 only vs GIP + GLP-1
3. **Schedule in the cited trials:** once daily (SCALE) vs once weekly (SURMOUNT-1)
4. **GI percents not interchangeable:** abstracts name GI events; they do not publish a matched nausea table
5. **Both FDA-approved** for obesity and type 2 diabetes (Saxenda/Victoza; Zepbound/Mounjaro)
6. **LEADER:** primary MACE HR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.97) vs placebo [leader-pmid-27295427]
7. **SURPASS-CVOT:** tirzepatide noninferior, not superior, to dulaglutide (HR 0.92; 95.3% CI 0.83–1.01) [surpass-cvot-pmid-41406444]
