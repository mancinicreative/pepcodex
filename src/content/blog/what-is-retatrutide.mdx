## How to Read Retatrutide Before Approval

The beginner definition is the [retatrutide guide](/guide/what-is-retatrutide). The compound record is [retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide). How to read TRIUMPH enrolment lives on [retatrutide Phase 3 enrolment](/blog/retatrutide-phase3-enrollment). This page is investigational vs approved — not a news tick and not a second definition.

Retatrutide is not approved. Phase 2 is the published efficacy record cited below. Phase 3 is ongoing. Do not treat a Phase 2 figure as a label claim.

| Fact | Detail |

## How Is It Different?

| Medication | Receptors |
|------------|-----------|
| Semaglutide | GLP-1 only |
| Tirzepatide | GIP + GLP-1 |
| **Retatrutide** | GIP + GLP-1 + Glucagon |

## Why Add Glucagon?

The glucagon receptor may:
- Increase calorie burning
- Enhance fat breakdown
- Boost metabolic rate

## When Might It Be Available?

Retatrutide is in Phase 3 trials (TRIUMPH program). If successful, potential approval could come in 2026-2027, but this is not guaranteed.

## Current Status

You **cannot** get retatrutide outside of clinical trials. It is not approved or available for prescription.
