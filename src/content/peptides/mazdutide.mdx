Mazdutide (IBI362; LY3305677) activates the GLP-1 and glucagon receptors. Its
clinical record includes published randomized trials in adults with obesity,
overweight, or type 2 diabetes. The findings below refer to studied products,
populations, endpoints, and follow-up periods; they do not establish outcomes
for products sold outside regulated channels.

## Weight-management evidence

The Chinese GLORY-1 phase 3 trial reported greater weight reduction with
mazdutide than placebo at 48 weeks. In the separate GLORY-2 phase 3 trial,
Chinese adults with obesity, including a minority with type 2 diabetes, had a
mean weight change at week 60 of -16.65% with mazdutide and -1.50% with placebo.
GLORY-2 reported vomiting, nausea, and diarrhea more often with mazdutide, and
adverse-event discontinuation was 2.9% versus 0%. [GLORY-1, PMID
40421736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2, PMID
42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/)

A Chinese phase 2 trial in 80 adults with obesity without diabetes reported a
24-week weight change of -12.78% versus +1.80% with placebo. A distinct US phase
2 trial randomized 179 adults with obesity or overweight without type 2
diabetes. Its primary 32-week efficacy-estimand analysis reported mean changes
of -7.3%, -15.6%, and -18.1% across study groups versus -0.9% with placebo;
adverse-event discontinuation was highest in the top-dose group at 20%. The
shorter analysis, different estimand, and smaller US sample prevent a direct
ranking against the Chinese studies. [Chinese phase 2, PMID
41875890](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41875890/) · [US phase 2, PMID
42628555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

## Type 2 diabetes evidence

An earlier Chinese phase 2 randomized trial studied adults with type 2 diabetes
inadequately controlled with diet and exercise or stable metformin. In a
separate phase 3 placebo-controlled trial of 320 Chinese adults, mean HbA1c
changes at week 24 were -1.57% and -2.15% in the two mazdutide groups versus
-0.14% with placebo. Another phase 3 trial randomized 731 Chinese adults with
type 2 diabetes receiving background oral medicines; mazdutide improved the
28-week HbA1c and weight endpoints versus dulaglutide in that study. [Phase 2,
PMID 37943529](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37943529/) · [Placebo trial,
PMID 41407859](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407859/) · [Dulaglutide
trial, PMID 41407860](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407860/)

A **June 2026 conference abstract**, rather than a full trial report, gives
DREAMS-3 results from 349 Chinese adults with early type 2 diabetes and obesity.
It reports that mazdutide was superior to semaglutide 1 mg on the proportion
reaching both HbA1c below 7% and at least 10% weight loss at week 32; the
abstract also reports more nausea and vomiting with mazdutide. The open-label
design, selected population, semaglutide dose, and abstract-only reporting
limit broader comparative claims. This is a distinct trial, not a second
publication of DREAMS-1 or DREAMS-2. [ADA 2026 abstract,
DOI 10.2337/db26-1226-OR](https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/75/Supplement_1/1226-OR/167125/1226-OR-Mazdutide-vs-Semaglutide-in-Chinese-Adults)

## Limits and safety

Gastrointestinal adverse events recur across the trial program. A 2026
systematic review pooled nine randomized trials and 2,292 participants, but
the authors graded several obesity comparisons as very-low certainty because
of heterogeneity and few trials. Its participants overlap with primary trial
reports; they are not an additional independent trial population. A mouse and
hepatocyte study of liver disease cannot establish a human liver indication.
[Review, PMID 42410325](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42410325/) · [Mouse
and cell study, PMID 41901218](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41901218/)

## Regulatory scope

An Innovent filing reports Chinese NMPA approval for chronic weight management
in adults meeting the filing's BMI/comorbidity criteria and for glycemic control
in adults with type 2 diabetes. This is sponsor-reported regulatory status;
the review did not retrieve an independent NMPA label. The FDA's March 2026
warning letter calls mazdutide products offered by a cited US seller unapproved
new drugs. Neither the Chinese report nor a trial registration implies FDA or
EMA approval. [Innovent filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf) ·
[FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026)
