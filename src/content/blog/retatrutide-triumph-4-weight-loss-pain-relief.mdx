**Correction notice:** An earlier version of this article reported TRIUMPH-4 results, including 28.7% weight loss and improvements on WOMAC pain and function scores. Those figures could not be traced to any published trial report or registry posting, and they have been removed. This article now describes the verified status of TRIUMPH-4 and the retatrutide evidence that actually exists.

## What TRIUMPH-4 Is

TRIUMPH-4 is the stand-alone knee osteoarthritis trial within Eli Lilly's Phase 3 TRIUMPH registrational program for [retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide). The program consists of four multicenter, randomized, double-blind trials enrolling over 5,800 participants, evaluating weekly subcutaneous retatrutide against placebo for obesity and two related complications — obstructive sleep apnea and knee osteoarthritis. It uses a basket design: two weight-management trials (TRIUMPH-1 and TRIUMPH-2) with OSA and OA protocols nested within, one weight-management trial in a population with cardiovascular disease (TRIUMPH-3), and the stand-alone knee OA trial, TRIUMPH-4 [triumph-program-design].

## What Is Published, as of 17 August 2026

**No TRIUMPH efficacy results.** A dateless PubMed search for the TRIUMPH trials on 17 August 2026 returns no efficacy or safety readouts — only the program design paper and adjacent methodology records. No TRIUMPH-4 weight-loss percentage, pain score, or function outcome has appeared in a verified publication, and figures circulating for this trial — including the 28.7% weight-loss number this article previously carried — cannot be traced to a primary source.

**Phase 2 obesity data.** The verified obesity efficacy benchmark for retatrutide remains the phase 2 trial published in the New England Journal of Medicine, which reported 24.2% mean weight loss at 48 weeks at the highest maintenance level. Gastrointestinal adverse events were dose-related and the most common adverse events [nejm-retatrutide-phase2].

**One published phase 3 — in diabetes.** The first phase 3 trial of retatrutide to report anywhere is TRANSCEND-T2D-1, published in The Lancet on 6 June 2026. In 537 adults with type 2 diabetes over 40 weeks, HbA1c fell 1.69 to 1.94 percentage points across the three strengths versus 0.81 with placebo on the treatment-regimen estimand, and body weight — a secondary endpoint — fell up to 15.3% [transcend-t2d-1]. That trial sits outside the TRIUMPH basket and does not address the osteoarthritis question.

## Why an Obesity Drug Is Being Tested in Knee Osteoarthritis

The rationale for TRIUMPH-4 is mechanistic and clinical rather than speculative. Obesity is an established risk factor for knee osteoarthritis: excess body weight increases compressive load on weight-bearing joints, and adipose tissue is metabolically active in ways that may affect joint tissue beyond mechanics. Weight loss typically improves osteoarthritis symptoms, which is why a weight-management drug with large expected effect sizes is being evaluated directly in an OA population, with OA symptom measures built into the trial design [triumph-program-design].

Whether retatrutide's effect on OA symptoms will be proportional to its weight-loss effect, and whether any benefit extends to joint structure rather than symptoms, are open questions that only the trial's publication can answer.

## The Triple Agonist Mechanism

Retatrutide activates three receptors rather than one ([semaglutide](/peptides/semaglutide)) or two ([tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide)):

**GLP-1 receptor:** appetite suppression, glucose-dependent insulin secretion, delayed gastric emptying.

**GIP receptor:** amplifies GLP-1 effects on appetite and insulin secretion, with additional effects on adipose tissue.

**Glucagon receptor:** increases energy expenditure and promotes hepatic fat oxidation — the component that distinguishes retatrutide from tirzepatide. A mechanistic rationale, however, is not evidence of a clinical result.

## What to Watch

- **TRIUMPH publications** — the completed TRIUMPH weight-management trials and TRIUMPH-4 itself have not published efficacy results; their readouts will replace speculation with data
- **The head-to-head** — TRIUMPH-5 compares retatrutide directly with tirzepatide and has not reported; our [retatrutide vs tirzepatide status review](/blog/retatrutide-direct-comparison) tracks it
- **Regulatory status** — retatrutide is investigational and not approved by the FDA for any indication
