## Molecules and evidence questions

Mazdutide (IBI362; LY3305677) is a GLP-1/glucagon receptor dual agonist. Tirzepatide activates GLP-1 and GIP receptors. The second receptor target is different, but that mechanism does not supply a clinical efficacy or safety ranking. [Mazdutide US phase 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/) · [Mounjaro FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0)

| Evidence question | Mazdutide | Tirzepatide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receptor targets | GLP-1 and glucagon | GLP-1 and GIP |
| Cited weight program | Chinese GLORY-1/2 phase 3; separate US phase 2 | SURMOUNT randomized trials, including adults without and with type 2 diabetes |
| Cited diabetes program | Chinese DREAMS-1 placebo-controlled and DREAMS-2 dulaglutide-controlled trials | SURPASS trials, including the dulaglutide-controlled cardiovascular-outcomes trial |
| US product status | No FDA approval verified | Mounjaro and Zepbound have distinct FDA-approved uses |
| China status in this review | Innovent reports specified adult weight-management and type 2 diabetes approvals | No China product-label conclusion drawn from US labels |

The status row relies on [Innovent's Hong Kong exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), the [FDA letter regarding one US mazdutide seller](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026), and the US [Mounjaro](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0) and [Zepbound](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=487cd7e7-434c-4925-99fa-aa80b1cc776b&version=38) labels. Innovent is a sponsor source; an independent Chinese product label was not retrieved in this bounded review.

## Weight trials: keep controls, populations and timing attached

GLORY-1 enrolled Chinese adults with overweight or obesity, treated for 48 weeks, and used a week-32 primary weight endpoint. GLORY-2 enrolled Chinese adults with obesity, including some with type 2 diabetes. At week 60, GLORY-2 reported mean weight changes of -16.65% for mazdutide 9 mg and -1.50% for placebo among participants who received study treatment. A separate US phase 2 trial enrolled 179 adults without type 2 diabetes and used a 32-week efficacy or hypothetical estimand. These three results are not interchangeable estimates. [GLORY-1, PMID 40421736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) · [GLORY-2, PMID 42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [US phase 2, PMID 42628555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

SURMOUNT-1 randomized adults with obesity or overweight plus a complication **without type 2 diabetes** to tirzepatide or placebo and evaluated weight at week 72. The Zepbound label also describes a separate study in adults with type 2 diabetes. Those are within-tirzepatide comparisons, not a trial against mazdutide. A difference between a SURMOUNT percentage and a GLORY percentage is not an estimated treatment difference: geography, entry criteria, follow-up, dose and statistical estimands vary. [SURMOUNT-1, PMID 35658024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35658024/) · [Zepbound label, clinical studies](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=487cd7e7-434c-4925-99fa-aa80b1cc776b&version=38)

## Diabetes and cardiovascular outcomes are separate questions

Chinese DREAMS-1 tested mazdutide against placebo in type 2 diabetes; DREAMS-2 tested it against dulaglutide. Neither directly compared with tirzepatide. SURPASS-CVOT instead studied tirzepatide against dulaglutide in adults with type 2 diabetes and established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. It found **noninferiority** for its composite cardiovascular outcome, not a head-to-head cardiovascular benefit over mazdutide. Mounjaro's US label includes a cardiovascular risk-reduction indication in specified adults with type 2 diabetes at high risk; that approved product claim cannot be transferred to mazdutide. [DREAMS-1, PMID 41407859](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407859/) · [DREAMS-2, PMID 41407860](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407860/) · [SURPASS-CVOT, PMID 41406444](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41406444/) · [Mounjaro label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0)

## Regulatory and safety limits

Mounjaro's US label covers glycemic control in adults and children aged 10 years or older with type 2 diabetes, plus reduction of major adverse cardiovascular events in specified high-risk adults with type 2 diabetes. Zepbound's separate US label covers long-term weight reduction for qualifying adults and moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. These are product- and population-specific indications. The Zepbound label advises against coadministration with another tirzepatide product or any GLP-1 receptor agonist; no mazdutide-plus-tirzepatide efficacy or safety conclusion follows from the separate trials. [Mounjaro label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0) · [Zepbound label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=487cd7e7-434c-4925-99fa-aa80b1cc776b&version=38)

Both programs report gastrointestinal adverse events, but differing study designs and ascertainment prevent a reliable cross-trial rate ranking. Tirzepatide's US labels carry a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors in rodents and a contraindication for a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. Mazdutide does not have an FDA product label from which to infer equivalent wording or event frequencies. [GLORY-2, PMID 42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) · [Mounjaro label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0)
