## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [MariTide](/peptides/maritide) (maridebart cafraglutide; AMG 133) is Amgen's investigational peptide-antibody conjugate that combines GLP-1 receptor agonism with GIP receptor antagonism [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887] [amg133-discovery-pmid-41941715]. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, and the MariTide Phase 2 trial are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [MariTide vs semaglutide](/compare/maritide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ. Do not divide the amycretin 36-week estimated mean by the MariTide 52-week treatment-policy range.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; the abstract reports 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. The abstract reports 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Novo Nordisk A/S. PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### MariTide Phase 2 (NEJM)

PMID 40549887, NCT05669599. Phase 2, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial with multiple assigned arms in two cohorts. Primary end point: percent change in body weight from baseline to week 52. The abstract reports 592 participants enrolled.

Obesity cohort (465 participants): mean percent change at week 52 on the **treatment-policy estimand** (intention-to-treat approach) ranged from −12.3% (95% CI −15.0 to −9.7) to −16.2% (95% CI −18.9 to −13.5) with maridebart cafraglutide versus −2.5% (95% CI −4.2 to −0.7) with placebo.

Obesity-diabetes cohort (127 participants): week-52 treatment-policy mean percent change ranged from −8.4% (95% CI −11.0 to −5.7) to −12.3% (95% CI −15.3 to −9.2) versus −1.7% (95% CI −2.9 to −0.6) with placebo. Glycated hemoglobin change on the treatment-policy estimand in that cohort was −1.2 to −1.6 percentage points versus 0.1 percentage points with placebo.

This page quotes those published ranges. It does **not** collapse either range into one headline percent and does **not** invent a per-arm table the abstract omitted [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887]. Gastrointestinal adverse events were common; the abstract states they were less frequent with escalation and a lower starting amount. No unexpected safety signals were reported in the abstract.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05669599 as completed Phase 2 (hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Amgen. Primary outcome on the registry is percent change from baseline to week 52 in body weight. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT05669599` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40549887 only.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means with no estimand named in the abstract; the quoted MariTide figures are Phase 2 treatment-policy week-52 ranges.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **MariTide Phase 2:** treatment-policy obesity cohort −12.3% to −16.2% vs −2.5% at week 52. Do not collapse the range.
5. **Phase 3 MariTide:** NCT06858878 is design-only as of 2026-09-02 (hasResults false; PubMed 0).
