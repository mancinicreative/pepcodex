## Overview

Maridebart cafraglutide (MariTide, AMG 133) is Amgen's investigational peptide-antibody conjugate: a GLP-1 receptor agonist paired with a GIP receptor antagonist. Tirzepatide is Eli Lilly's dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. The Phase 2 maridebart trial, SURMOUNT-1, and SURMOUNT-2 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [maritide](/peptides/maritide), [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), and [maritide vs semaglutide](/compare/maritide-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations and estimands differ.

### Maridebart cafraglutide Phase 2

PMID 40549887, NCT05669599. n=592 enrolled as two cohorts. Primary end point: percent change in body weight from baseline to week 52. The paper reports the **treatment-policy estimand** (intention-to-treat). Arms included 140, 280, or 420 mg every 4 weeks, 420 mg every 8 weeks, and 420 mg every 4 weeks with 4-week or 12-week dose escalation. The abstract publishes a range across maridebart groups, not a per-arm percent table.

Obesity cohort (465 participants; no diabetes): mean percent change at week 52 ranged from −12.3% (95% CI −15.0 to −9.7) to −16.2% (95% CI −18.9 to −13.5) versus −2.5% (95% CI −4.2 to −0.7) placebo.

Obesity-diabetes cohort (127 participants): −8.4% (95% CI −11.0 to −5.7) to −12.3% (95% CI −15.3 to −9.2) versus −1.7% (95% CI −2.9 to −0.6) placebo. Mean change in glycated haemoglobin on the treatment-policy estimand: −1.2 to −1.6 percentage points versus +0.1 percentage points placebo.

Gastrointestinal adverse events were common; the abstract states they were less frequent with dose escalation and a lower starting dose. It does not publish nausea or vomiting percents [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887].

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT05669599 as completed (actual enrollment 592; completion 2025-12-16) with `hasResults` false as of 2026-09-02.

### Phase 1 (no percent in the abstract)

PMID 38316982, NCT04478708. Phase 1 single- and multiple-ascending-dose study in participants with obesity. The abstract reports an acceptable safety and tolerability profile, dose-dependent weight loss, and maintenance of weight loss in multiple-ascending-dose cohorts for up to 150 days after the last dose. The abstract does not publish a percent. This page does not invent one [maritide-p1-pmid-38316982].

### SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide, no diabetes)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. n=2,539; 72 weeks. **Treatment-regimen** estimand (effects regardless of discontinuation). Mean change at week 72: −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg), −3.1% placebo (P&lt;0.001 vs placebo). At least 20% reduction: 50% (10 mg) and 57% (15 mg; 95% CI 53–61) versus 3% placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, 6.2%, and 2.6% (5 / 10 / 15 mg and placebo) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

### SURMOUNT-2 (tirzepatide, obesity with type 2 diabetes)

PMID 37385275, NCT04657003. n=938; 72 weeks. **Treatment-regimen** estimand. Least-squares mean change at week 72: −12.8% (10 mg), −14.7% (15 mg), −3.2% placebo (estimated differences −9.6 and −11.6 percentage points; all P&lt;0.0001). Body-weight reduction of 5% or higher: 79–83% versus 32% placebo. Few gastrointestinal events led to discontinuation (&lt;5%) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275].

### Registered Phase 3 (design only)

MARITIME-1 (NCT06858839) is Phase 3, active not recruiting, actual enrollment 3,853, estimated primary completion 2027-01-21. ClinicalTrials.gov `hasResults` was false as of 2026-09-02. PubMed `NCT06858839` and `"MARITIME-1" maridebart` each returned 0 records on 2026-09-02 [nct-06858839].

MARITIME-2 (NCT06858878) is Phase 3, active not recruiting, actual enrollment 1,105, estimated primary completion 2027-01-21. `hasResults` false. PubMed `NCT06858878` and `"MARITIME-2" maridebart` each returned 0 records on 2026-09-02 [nct-06858878].

## Side Effect Profile

Phase 2 maridebart: gastrointestinal events common; the abstract does not publish nausea, vomiting, or discontinuation percents [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation for adverse events 6.2% (15 mg) versus 2.6% placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. The SURMOUNT-1 abstract does not publish separate nausea or vomiting percents; this page does not invent them.

SURMOUNT-2: gastrointestinal events most frequent; few events led to discontinuation (&lt;5%) [surmount-2-pmid-37385275].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** Phase 2 maridebart is 52 weeks (treatment-policy); SURMOUNT-1 is 72 weeks (treatment-regimen).
2. **Phase 2 is a range, not a single arm:** obesity cohort −12.3% to −16.2% versus −2.5% placebo; obesity-diabetes −8.4% to −12.3% versus −1.7%. The abstract does not publish a per-dose table.
3. **Treatment-regimen leads for tirzepatide:** SURMOUNT-1 15 mg −20.9% versus −3.1%; ≥20% at 15 mg is 57% (95% CI 53–61). SURMOUNT-2 15 mg −14.7% versus −3.2% in type 2 diabetes.
4. **Phase 1 abstract has no percent:** NCT04478708 reports dose-dependent weight loss without a published figure in the abstract.
5. **Phase 3 is registered, unpublished:** MARITIME-1 and MARITIME-2 `hasResults` false and PubMed 0 as of 2026-09-02; no Phase 3 percent quoted.
