## The short definition

Tesamorelin is a synthetic GHRH analog that stimulates growth hormone release. Its approved U.S. use is reduction of excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated lipodystrophy. ([FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf))

## Three distinctions that matter

| Question | Evidence-based answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is it a general weight-loss medicine? | No. Weight-loss management is excluded from the label. |
| Does a visceral-fat result establish every body-composition claim? | No. The pivotal endpoint and studied HIV population define what was demonstrated. |
| Are Egrifta WR and Egrifta SV interchangeable? | No. The label says they are not substitutable. |

Sources: [current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=839334d3-8c1d-4c26-9036-2ab524a6ea75) and [pivotal trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20101189/).

## Reading the research

**Evidence grade: high for the approved visceral-fat endpoint in the studied population.** One pivotal study reported a six-month visceral-fat change of -10.9% with tesamorelin versus -0.6% with placebo. The extension showed loss of the initial improvement after switching to placebo. Those findings do not establish a lasting cure or a general anti-aging benefit. ([Trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20101189/))

Safety and eligibility are separate questions from whether an endpoint improved. The current label includes contraindications and glucose, IGF-1, fluid-retention and allergy warnings. ([FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf))
