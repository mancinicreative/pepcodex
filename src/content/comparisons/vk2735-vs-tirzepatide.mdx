## Overview

[VK2735](/peptides/vk2735) is an investigational dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. [Tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide) is an approved dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist (openFDA: Mounjaro NDA 215866; Zepbound NDA 217806). This page quotes VENTURE (13-week subcutaneous VK2735) and SURMOUNT-1 (72-week subcutaneous tirzepatide). Those are not one head-to-head RCT.

For another VK2735 cross-trial page, see [VK2735 vs Semaglutide](/compare/vk2735-vs-semaglutide).

## Mechanism

VENTURE and the Phase 1 registry title describe VK2735 as a dual GLP-1/GIP (GIP/GLP-1) receptor agonist [venture-pmid-41508550] [vk2735-p1-nct05203237]. SURMOUNT-1 describes tirzepatide as a GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. This page does not quote a half-life or amino-acid count from memory.

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations differ (13 vs 72 weeks).

### VENTURE (subcutaneous VK2735, no diabetes)

PMID 41508550, NCT06068946. Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging, weekly subcutaneous VK2735. Adults with obesity, or overweight plus at least one weight-related comorbidity; diabetes excluded. Conducted August 2023–February 2024. Primary endpoint: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 13. CT.gov enrollment 176 actual; hasResults false (results quoted from the journal abstract, not a CT.gov results module) [venture-pmid-41508550].

Abstract-reported mean weight reduction: 9.2 kg (2.5 mg; 9.1%) to 14.6 kg (15 mg; 14.7%); placebo 1.8 kg (1.7%). At least 5% reduction: 93% (130/140) on active treatment vs 12% (4/34) on placebo. The abstract does not name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand and does not publish a ≥10% percent or a per-arm nausea table [venture-pmid-41508550].

### Phase 1 subcutaneous VK2735 (design only)

NCT05203237. Phase 1 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled single- and multiple-ascending-dose study in healthy adults and otherwise healthy adults with increased BMI. Enrollment 92 actual. Completed. hasResults **false**. PubMed search `NCT05203237` on 2026-09-02 returned **0** records. This page does not quote a Phase 1 percent [vk2735-p1-nct05203237].

### Venture Oral Dosing (design only)

NCT06828055. Phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 13-week dose-finding study of an oral VK2735 formulation in adults with obesity, or overweight plus at least one weight-related comorbidity. Enrollment 280 actual. Completed (primary completion 2025-06-24 actual). hasResults **false**. PubMed searches `NCT06828055` and `"VK2735"` on 2026-09-02 found **no** oral-trial publication (the only VK2735 paper was VENTURE, subcutaneous). This page does not quote an oral percent [venture-oral-nct06828055].

### SURMOUNT-1 (subcutaneous tirzepatide, no diabetes)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. Phase 3; n=2539; 72 weeks including a 20-week dose-escalation period. Coprimary: percent weight change and ≥5% reduction. **Treatment-regimen estimand** (effects regardless of discontinuation; intention-to-treat). Mean change at week 72: 5 mg −15.0% (95% CI −15.9 to −14.2); 10 mg −19.5% (95% CI −20.4 to −18.5); 15 mg **−20.9%** (95% CI −21.8 to −19.9); placebo **−3.1%** (95% CI −4.3 to −1.9); P&lt;0.001 vs placebo for all. ≥5% at 5/10/15 mg: 85%/89%/91% vs 35% placebo. ≥20% at 10 mg 50% (95% CI 46–54) and at 15 mg **57%** (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% (95% CI 1–5) placebo. Adverse events led to discontinuation in 4.3%/7.1%/6.2% vs 2.6% placebo. Most common adverse events were gastrointestinal, mostly mild to moderate during dose escalation. The abstract does not publish a nausea-percent table [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Safety

VENTURE: common adverse events were gastrointestinal and decreased in reported frequency after titration to steady state. No nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, or discontinuation percent in the abstract fetched 2026-09-02 [venture-pmid-41508550].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common; discontinuation for adverse events 4.3%/7.1%/6.2% vs 2.6% placebo. No nausea-percent table in the abstract fetched 2026-09-02 [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

This page does not invent a matched GI-incidence table.

## Formulations and development status

VENTURE and VANQUISH 1/2 use subcutaneous VK2735. NCT06828055 is a completed oral-formulation Phase 2 without a PubMed results paper as of 2026-09-02. SURMOUNT-1 used once-weekly subcutaneous tirzepatide. This page does not quote an oral tirzepatide obesity trial.

Phase 3 for subcutaneous VK2735 is registered and active-not-recruiting (VANQUISH 1 and 2). This page does not invent an FDA-submission or approval year.
