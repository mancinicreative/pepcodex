## How to Read Tesamorelin Safety Evidence

Frequency tables, contraindications, and monitoring lists are the [tesamorelin safety page](/safety/tesamorelin-safety) — that URL already earns PAGE impressions. This post is not a second Egrifta side-effect table.

Tesamorelin is an FDA-approved GHRH analog ([tesamorelin dossier](/peptides/tesamorelin)). The label is for a specific HIV-lipodystrophy indication. Research-use or anti-aging claims do not inherit that AE table.

## What the Label Is For

The frequency rows — injection-site reactions, arthralgia, edema, hyperglycemia — live on [tesamorelin safety](/safety/tesamorelin-safety). Read them there. This page only flags the job split: approved-indication safety is not a research-chem brochure.

## Where Warnings Live

Contraindications (active malignancy, disrupted hypothalamic-pituitary axis, hypersensitivity, pregnancy) and monitoring lists are on the safety page and the prescribing information, not restated as a second table here.

## Special Considerations

- Active malignancy is a labelled contraindication; the prescribing information directs discontinuation if malignancy is diagnosed during treatment
- Not for general anti-aging use
- Requires ongoing prescription/monitoring
