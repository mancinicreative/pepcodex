## Overview

Amycretin is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic ERR pan-agonist described as an exercise-mimetic chemical probe. The amycretin oral first-in-human trial and the amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a trial are human programmes. The SLU-PP-332 papers quoted here are mouse studies. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [SLU-PP-332](/peptides/slu-pp-332), and [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Species, durations, populations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed on that NCT returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew. The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen estimand.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed on that NCT returned PMID 40550231 only.

A per-alias PubMed search for `"amycretin"` on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later papers that were not fetched this increment.

### SLU-PP-332 mouse exercise-capacity paper (ACS Chemical Biology)

PMID 36988910. The abstract names SLU-PP-332 as a synthetic ERR pan-agonist with highest potency for ERRα. In a skeletal-muscle cell line it increased mitochondrial function and cellular respiration. In mice it increased type IIa oxidative skeletal-muscle fibers and enhanced exercise endurance; the abstract states that ERRα activation was critical for that endurance effect [slu-pp-332-pmid-36988910]. The published abstract does **not** report a human RCT percent, a body-weight percent, or an obesity Phase 3 result.

### SLU-PP-332 mouse metabolic-syndrome paper (JPET)

PMID 37739806. The abstract names SLU-PP-332. Diet-induced obese or ob/ob mice were administered SLU-PP-332. The abstract reports increased energy expenditure and fatty-acid oxidation, decreased fat-mass accumulation, reduced obesity, and improved insulin sensitivity in those mouse models [slu-pp-332-pmid-37739806]. The published abstract does **not** report a numeric weight-change percent and does **not** report a human RCT. This page does not invent a ~ percent or an obesity Phase 3 programme.

Other `"SLU-PP-332"` hits fetched this increment (titles that name the compound) were a scaffold SAR paper (PMID 41850449) and two in-vitro metabolite / doping-control papers (PMID 41688415, PMID 41588687). None of those abstracts publishes a human RCT percent. They are not quoted as efficacy results.

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-class, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase human estimated means. SLU-PP-332 results quoted here are mouse studies. They are not one trial.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%. Estimand not named.
4. **SLU-PP-332:** fetched abstracts name the compound in mouse exercise-capacity and metabolic-syndrome models. No human RCT percent. No obesity Phase 3 invented here.
5. **openFDA:** neither compound had a drugsfda application on 2026-09-02. PubMed H2H and CT.gov SLU-PP-332 searches on that date returned zero records.
