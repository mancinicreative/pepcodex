The structured profile lives on [AOD-9604 safety](/safety/aod-9604-safety). The compound record is [AOD-9604](/peptides/aod-9604). This post does not recap a trial-result table, and it does not attach a journal identifier to Phase 2b efficacy or safety outcomes.

## What this page records

AOD-9604 is a modified fragment of human growth hormone that was studied for fat loss. It does not have an FDA-approved indication for weight loss.

Registered AOD9604 studies can be found via ClinicalTrials.gov [nct-aod-9604]. A registry search is not a published result paper. A brand name on an unregulated listing is not proof of identity or purity.

AOD-9604 is prohibited in sport [wada-prohibited-list].

## What this page will not do

It will not reconstruct primary-endpoint percentages from an unverified citation. It will not treat a research-chem listing as a labelled product. Long-term effects of chronic use are unknown; an unapproved agent may delay evaluation for treatments that have published Phase 3 data and labeling.

Regulatory approval is the bar that tests whether early signals hold up in controlled human trials. This page records the absence of an approved obesity indication and the WADA listing.
