## Overview

CagriSema and tirzepatide represent different pharmaceutical strategies for enhanced weight loss. CagriSema combines two separate peptides (semaglutide and cagrilintide) into a single injection, while tirzepatide is a single molecule with dual receptor activity. Both aim to achieve greater efficacy than GLP-1 monotherapy.

This comparison is significant as it represents competition between the two major players in metabolic medicine: Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly.

## Key Facts

| Aspect | CagriSema | Tirzepatide |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Developer** | Novo Nordisk | Eli Lilly |
| **Approach** | Combination product | Single molecule |
| **Targets** | GLP-1 + Amylin | GIP + GLP-1 |
| **FDA Status** | Not FDA-approved | FDA approved |

## Mechanism Comparison

| Aspect | CagriSema | Tirzepatide |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **GLP-1 Activity** | Semaglutide (agonist) | Built-in GLP-1 agonism |
| **Second Mechanism** | Cagrilintide (amylin analog) | GIP agonism |
| **Receptor Targets** | GLP-1R + AMY-R | GIPR + GLP-1R |
| **Unique Feature** | Amylin-based satiety | Novel dual agonist |

### CagriSema Components

**Semaglutide:**
- GLP-1 receptor agonist
- Same as Wegovy/Ozempic
- Reduces appetite, slows gastric emptying

**Cagrilintide:**
- Long-acting amylin analog
- Enhances satiety signaling
- Slows gastric emptying (additive to GLP-1)
- Novel mechanism not previously combined with GLP-1

### Tirzepatide Mechanism

- Single molecule with dual activity
- GIP receptor agonism (primary)
- GLP-1 receptor agonism (secondary)
- Synergistic effects on insulin and appetite

## Clinical Trial Data

### CagriSema Phase 3 (REDEFINE)

| Trial | Result |
|-------|--------|
| REDEFINE 1 | −20.4% weight change at 68 weeks vs −3.0% placebo (treatment-policy estimand) |
| REDEFINE 2 (T2D) | −13.7% weight change at 68 weeks vs −3.4% placebo (treatment-policy estimand), N=1,206 |
| NCT06131437 | Completed open-label CagriSema vs tirzepatide (n=809 actual); CT.gov `hasResults` false as of 2026-09-02 — no percent quoted |

**Note:** REDEFINE 1 and REDEFINE 2 were both published in the *New England Journal of Medicine* in
2025. Both figures above are the treatment-policy estimand — the result across all randomised
participants regardless of adherence — which is what the published papers report. Sponsor
communications sometimes quote a higher efficacy-estimand figure that models full adherence.

REDEFINE 2 enrolled adults with type 2 diabetes and a BMI of 27 or more, so its smaller weight
change is not directly comparable to REDEFINE 1: weight loss is consistently lower in populations
with type 2 diabetes across every drug in this class.

## Side Effect Profile

REDEFINE 1 reported gastrointestinal adverse events in 79.6% of the CagriSema group versus 39.9% on placebo, mainly transient and mild-to-moderate (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, or abdominal pain named as a class, not as separate percents) [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

SURMOUNT-1 reported gastrointestinal events as the most common adverse events with tirzepatide, mostly mild to moderate, primarily during dose escalation. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 6.2% of participants on 15 mg versus 2.6% on placebo [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. The SURMOUNT-1 abstract does not publish separate nausea/vomiting percents; this page does not invent them.

This page does not quote a CagriSema discontinuation percent: that figure is not in the REDEFINE 1 PubMed abstract fetched 2026-09-02.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not interchangeable:** REDEFINE 1 reported −20.4% vs −3.0% at 68 weeks (treatment-policy) [redefine-1-pmid-40544433]. SURMOUNT-1 reported −20.9% vs −3.1% at 72 weeks on the 15 mg arm (treatment-regimen) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024]. Those are different trials, durations, and estimands.
2. **Different mechanisms:** CagriSema adds amylin; tirzepatide adds GIP to GLP-1
3. **Tirzepatide is available:** FDA-approved (Zepbound / Mounjaro)
4. **CagriSema is investigational:** REDEFINE 1/2 are published; it is not FDA-approved
5. **Head-to-head is registered, not published:** NCT06131437 is an 84-week open-label randomised trial (actual enrollment 809; COMPLETED; CT.gov `hasResults` false as of 2026-09-02), so this page does not quote a percent [nct-06131437]
6. **GI events in REDEFINE 1:** 79.6% vs 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433]. This page does not invent a tirzepatide GI percent for a side-by-side.
7. **Choice is clinical:** this page does not rank the two by list price.
