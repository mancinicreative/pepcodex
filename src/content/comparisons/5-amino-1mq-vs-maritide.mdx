## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [MariTide](/peptides/maritide) (maridebart cafraglutide; AMG 133) is Amgen's investigational peptide-antibody conjugate that combines GLP-1 receptor agonism with GIP receptor antagonism [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887] [amg133-discovery-pmid-41941715]. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run and the MariTide Phase 2 trial are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [5-Amino-1MQ vs semaglutide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-semaglutide) and [MariTide vs semaglutide](/compare/maritide-vs-semaglutide).

### MariTide Phase 2 (NEJM)

PMID 40549887, NCT05669599. Phase 2, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial with multiple assigned arms in two cohorts. Primary end point: percent change in body weight from baseline to week 52. The abstract reports 592 participants enrolled.

Obesity cohort (465 participants): mean percent change at week 52 on the **treatment-policy estimand** (intention-to-treat approach) ranged from −12.3% (95% CI −15.0 to −9.7) to −16.2% (95% CI −18.9 to −13.5) with maridebart cafraglutide versus −2.5% (95% CI −4.2 to −0.7) with placebo.

Obesity-diabetes cohort (127 participants): week-52 treatment-policy mean percent change ranged from −8.4% (95% CI −11.0 to −5.7) to −12.3% (95% CI −15.3 to −9.2) versus −1.7% (95% CI −2.9 to −0.6) with placebo. Glycated hemoglobin change on the treatment-policy estimand in that cohort was −1.2 to −1.6 percentage points versus 0.1 percentage points with placebo.

This page quotes those published ranges. It does **not** collapse either range into one headline percent and does **not** invent a per-arm table the abstract omitted [maritide-p2-pmid-40549887]. Gastrointestinal adverse events were common; the abstract states they were less frequent with escalation and a lower starting amount. No unexpected safety signals were reported in the abstract.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05669599 as completed Phase 2 (hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Amgen. Primary outcome on the registry is percent change from baseline to week 52 in body weight. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT05669599` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40549887 only.

This increment’s CT.gov responses did not return an enrollment-count field on the registry records; the 592 / 465 / 127 figures above are from the journal abstract.
