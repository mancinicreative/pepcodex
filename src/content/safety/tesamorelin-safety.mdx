## Safety is specific to population and formulation

The pivotal safety evidence concerns people with HIV and excess abdominal fat. The pooled report followed a placebo-controlled phase with an extension, not an indefinite comparison. **Evidence grade: moderate for the observed trial safety profile.** Approval does not establish safety for unrelated uses. ([Pooled trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20554713/))

## Contraindications in the label

The Egrifta WR label lists active malignancy, specified hypothalamic-pituitary disruption, pregnancy and hypersensitivity to tesamorelin or its formulation ingredients. These are formal contraindications, distinct from precautions requiring clinical judgment. ([FDA label, section 4](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf))

## Risks and unanswered questions

| Label topic | What the evidence summary should convey |
| --- | --- |
| IGF-1 | Levels rise; consequences of prolonged elevation remain uncertain. |
| Glucose | Glucose intolerance or diabetes may develop. |
| Fluid retention | Swelling and musculoskeletal symptoms can occur. |
| Hypersensitivity and injection reactions | Both appear in the label; they are different event categories. |
| Cardiovascular outcomes | Long-term cardiovascular safety is not established. |

These statements summarize the [current prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=839334d3-8c1d-4c26-9036-2ab524a6ea75); they are not a complete list or individual risk estimate.

## Formulations and interpretation

WR and SV are not substitutable. Their instructions and ingredients must not be conflated. Clinical trial adverse-event percentages also cannot be transferred directly to another population or compared casually with another drug's studies. ([FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/022505s020lbl.pdf))
