## The identity distinction

TB-500 commonly denotes **N-acetyl-LKKTETQ**, a seven-residue fragment corresponding to thymosin beta-4 residues 17–23. Full-length thymosin beta-4 is a 43-residue protein; its human or animal trial results cannot be assigned to the fragment. LL-37 is the active 37-residue human cathelicidin peptide processed from hCAP18. [FDA TB-500 identity review](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download) · [LL-37 identity, PMID 8681941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8681941/)

| Evidence question | TB-500 fragment | Native LL-37 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct selected evidence | Analytical and cell-assay fragment work; no cited human treatment trial | Three published topical wound-trial populations; separate tiny melanoma registry |
| Therapeutic-claim grade | **Very low** for human repair | **Low** for broad wound or infection benefit |
| Key limit | Parent Tβ4 trials do not transfer | Topical results do not establish systemic benefit |

A 2012 TB-500 study detected the fragment in a veterinary preparation and equine samples, a detection result rather than tissue repair. A 2024 fibroblast scratch assay found statistically significant activity for the **Ac-LKKTE metabolite**, not parent TB-500. Full-length thymosin beta-4 corneal and cardiac findings are indirect for the fragment. [PMID 23084823](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23084823/) · [PMID 38382158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382158/) · [parent Tβ4 corneal trial, PMID 36613994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36613994/)

The 34-person LL-37 venous-ulcer pilot reported selected healing signals. A later 148-person phase IIb trial did not find significant healing benefit for the full cohort; its large-ulcer subgroup finding was post hoc. Diabetic-foot-ulcer LL-37 cream improved granulation measures but not measured aerobic bacterial colonization. [LL-37 topical pilot, PMID 25041740](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25041740/) · [larger phase IIb trial, PMID 34687253](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34687253/) · [diabetic-foot-ulcer trial, PMID 37480520](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37480520/)

An intratumoral LL-37 melanoma record posts four-person results, without establishing antitumor efficacy or general safety. A separate engineered *L. lactis* product expressing LL-37 had no significant overall viral-negative-time result; it is not a native peptide wound trial. [NCT02225366](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02225366) · [PMID 37605995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37605995/)

FDA reports no identified human drug-product exposure data for the TB-500 fragment and insufficient safety information for compounded LL-37. Neither safety conclusion can be inferred from natural origin or parent-protein experience. [FDA TB-500 review](https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download) · [FDA compounding safety review](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks)

The selected evidence contains no direct TB-500-versus-LL-37 or combination trial. This is an educational comparison, not treatment guidance.
