## What is being compared?

Mazdutide (IBI362; LY3305677) is a dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist.
Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist used in distinct regulated products.
Receptor differences are a mechanism description, not proof that one treatment
has greater clinical benefit. The comparison below keeps the trial drug,
population, endpoint and regulatory jurisdiction attached to each claim.
[Mazdutide US phase 2, PMID 42628555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/) ·
[FDA Wegovy label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215256s031,218316s00lbl.pdf)

The regulatory row is supported by [Innovent's Hong Kong exchange filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf), the [FDA letter concerning one US mazdutide seller](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026), and the distinct [Ozempic](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/209637s038lbl.pdf), [Rybelsus/Ozempic tablet](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/213051Orig1s030lbl.pdf), and [Wegovy](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215256s031,218316s00lbl.pdf) FDA labels. The FDA letter identifies that seller's mazdutide products as unapproved new drugs; it is not a global approval record.

## Weight evidence without a cross-trial ranking

GLORY-1 was a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial in Chinese adults with obesity
or overweight, with 48 weeks of treatment and a primary weight endpoint at
week 32. GLORY-2 was a separate Chinese phase 3 trial in adults
with obesity, including some with type 2 diabetes. At week 60, GLORY-2 reported
mean weight changes of -16.65% with mazdutide 9 mg and -1.50% with placebo.
Its stated efficacy analysis included participants who received study
treatment. The published US phase 2 study enrolled 179 adults with obesity or overweight
without type 2 diabetes and reported 32-week weight results under an efficacy
or hypothetical estimand. Those US results do not automatically reproduce the
Chinese phase 3 findings. [GLORY-1, PMID 40421736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40421736/) ·
[GLORY-2, PMID 42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) ·
[US phase 2, PMID 42628555](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42628555/)

STEP 1 randomized 1,961 adults with overweight or obesity without diabetes
to semaglutide 2.4 mg injection or placebo and reported 68-week mean weight
reductions of 14.9% and 2.4%, respectively, under the primary treatment-policy
estimand, regardless of treatment discontinuation or rescue intervention.
This is a **within-STEP-1** comparison, just
as the GLORY-2 figures compare its own randomized groups. Different entry
criteria, durations, products and analysis methods make a subtraction between
GLORY-2 and STEP 1 an unsupported head-to-head estimate.
[STEP 1, PMID 33567185](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33567185/) ·
[GLORY-2, PMID 42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/)

## Diabetes evidence and the limited direct comparison

Published Chinese mazdutide trials report HbA1c outcomes against placebo in
DREAMS-1 and against dulaglutide in DREAMS-2. These are distinct populations
and comparators, and neither is a semaglutide comparison.
[DREAMS-1, PMID 41407859](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407859/) ·
[DREAMS-2, PMID 41407860](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41407860/)

A **June 2026 conference abstract** for the open-label DREAMS-3 trial reports
a direct comparison in 349 selected Chinese adults with early type 2 diabetes
and obesity. Its 32-week endpoint was the proportion reaching both HbA1c below
7% **and** at least 10% weight loss. The abstract reports superiority for
mazdutide versus semaglutide 1 mg on that composite and more nausea and
vomiting with mazdutide. This abstract-only, comparator-specific result cannot
be recast as a general weight-loss, safety or product superiority claim; a full
results paper was not identified in the bounded source review.
[DREAMS-3 abstract, DOI 10.2337/db26-1226-OR](https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/75/Supplement_1/1226-OR/167125/1226-OR-Mazdutide-vs-Semaglutide-in-Chinese-Adults)

## Product approvals and outcome limits

Innovent reports Chinese NMPA approval of mazdutide for adult chronic
weight-management and type 2 diabetes indications subject to criteria in its
filing. This is sponsor-reported here; the bounded review did not obtain an
independent Chinese product label. No FDA approval for mazdutide was verified.
The FDA warning letter concerns products sold by a named US seller and should
not be stretched into a finding about every jurisdiction.
[Innovent filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1125/2025112500387.pdf) ·
[FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-sciences-721805-03312026)

Semaglutide's US status depends on the **specific product and indication**.
Ozempic and Rybelsus have type 2 diabetes indications. Wegovy has labeled
weight and selected cardiovascular risk-reduction uses. Wegovy **injection**
also has accelerated approval for adults with noncirrhotic MASH and F2-F3
fibrosis based on histologic endpoints; continued approval may depend on
confirmatory clinical benefit. This MASH indication does not extend to Wegovy
tablets or to all semaglutide products. The separate SELECT trial measured
cardiovascular events in adults with overweight or obesity, established
cardiovascular disease and no diabetes; the cited mazdutide weight trials do
not supply an equivalent cardiovascular-outcomes comparison.
[FDA Ozempic label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/209637s038lbl.pdf) ·
[FDA Rybelsus/Ozempic tablet label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/213051Orig1s030lbl.pdf) ·
[FDA Wegovy label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/215256s031,218316s00lbl.pdf) ·
[SELECT, PMID 37952131](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37952131/)

Gastrointestinal adverse events were reported in the cited weight and
DREAMS-3 studies, but rates and discontinuations cannot be ranked across
different trials and products. No combination-safety or efficacy conclusion
for mazdutide plus semaglutide follows from these separate studies.
[GLORY-2, PMID 42251595](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251595/) ·
[STEP 1, PMID 33567185](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33567185/) ·
[DREAMS-3 abstract](https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/75/Supplement_1/1226-OR/167125/1226-OR-Mazdutide-vs-Semaglutide-in-Chinese-Adults)
