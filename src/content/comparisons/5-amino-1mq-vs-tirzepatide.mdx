## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [Tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide) is a glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, listed on openFDA as Mounjaro and Zepbound. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run and SURMOUNT-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [5-Amino-1MQ vs semaglutide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-semaglutide) and [tirzepatide vs semaglutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-semaglutide).

### SURMOUNT-1 (NEJM)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622. Phase 3, double-blind trial in 2539 adults with BMI of 30 or more, or 27 or more plus at least one weight-related complication, excluding diabetes. Randomisation 1:1:1:1 to once-weekly subcutaneous tirzepatide 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg, or placebo, for 72 weeks including a 20-week escalation period. Coprimary end points were percent change in weight and a reduction of 5% or more. The **treatment-regimen estimand** assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation in the intention-to-treat population.

At week 72, mean percent change was −15.0% (95% CI −15.9 to −14.2) with 5 mg, −19.5% (95% CI −20.4 to −18.5) with 10 mg, −20.9% (95% CI −21.8 to −19.9) with 15 mg, and −3.1% (95% CI −4.3 to −1.9) with placebo (P&lt;0.001 for all comparisons with placebo). Weight reduction of 5% or more: 85% (95% CI 82–89), 89% (95% CI 86–92), and 91% (95% CI 88–94) versus 35% (95% CI 30–39). Reduction of 20% or more at 10 mg: 50% (95% CI 46–54); at 15 mg: 57% (95% CI 53–61) versus 3% (95% CI 1–5) placebo. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3%, 7.1%, and 6.2% versus 2.6% placebo. The most common adverse events were gastrointestinal and were mostly mild to moderate during escalation. The abstract does not publish a nausea-percent table [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04184622 as completed Phase 3 (hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Eli Lilly and Company. Primary completion 2022-04-01 (actual). Figures above are from the journal abstract. PubMed `NCT04184622` on 2026-09-02 returned 22 records; this page quotes PMID 35658024. The 58-hit `"SURMOUNT-1"` list was not dumped.
