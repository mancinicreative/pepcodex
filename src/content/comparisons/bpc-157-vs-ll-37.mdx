## What is being compared?

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15-residue gastric-peptide fragment investigated for tissue injury and mucosal protection. LL-37 is a 37-residue peptide processed from human cathelicidin precursor hCAP18; endogenous wound expression and laboratory antimicrobial activity are not themselves treatment outcomes. [FDA BPC-157 briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download) · [LL-37 identity, PMID 8681941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8681941/)

| Evidence question | BPC-157 | LL-37 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human intervention | Three small full reports plus two abstract-only controlled groups in FDA's reviewed inventory | Three published native topical wound-trial populations; a separate four-person intratumoral registry cohort |
| Therapeutic-claim grade | **Low**: heterogeneous and incompletely reported human work | **Low**: condition-specific, mixed topical results |
| What remains unproved? | Tendon, muscle, general gut-healing efficacy and long-term safety | General wound, infection, systemic and cancer benefit |

## What the studies actually found

FDA's 2026 BPC-157 review distinguishes an abstract-only phase 1 tolerability group and ulcerative-colitis controlled group from three full primary reports: a retrospective knee series, uncontrolled interstitial-cystitis pilot and two-person intravenous pilot. The controlled ulcerative-colitis abstract is too incomplete to establish efficacy or safety; none validates tendon or muscle repair in people. [FDA briefing](https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download) · [knee report, PMID 34324435](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34324435/) · [cystitis pilot, PMID 39325560](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39325560/) · [IV pilot, PMID 40131143](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40131143/)

A 34-person venous-ulcer pilot reported selected healing signals with native topical LL-37. A later 148-person trial found no significant full-population healing benefit, although a large-ulcer subgroup analysis was post hoc. A separate diabetic-foot-ulcer cream trial found improved granulation measures without a significant change in measured aerobic bacterial colonization. These outcomes do not establish broad antimicrobial treatment. [LL-37 topical pilot, PMID 25041740](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25041740/) · [larger phase IIb trial, PMID 34687253](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34687253/) · [diabetic-foot-ulcer trial, PMID 37480520](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37480520/)

The completed [NCT02225366 melanoma record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02225366) posts results for only four intratumoral LL-37 participants; it is not an additional wound population or proof of antitumor benefit. An oral COVID-19 trial tested engineered *L. lactis* expressing LL-37, not purified peptide; its overall viral-negative-time result was nonsignificant. [PMID 37605995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37605995/)

Neither mechanism nor one peptide's source count provides a head-to-head efficacy ranking. FDA reports insufficient safety information for compounded preparations of both peptides, with substance-specific concerns. [FDA compounding safety review](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks)

This selected-source comparison is educational and does not provide treatment or combination guidance.
