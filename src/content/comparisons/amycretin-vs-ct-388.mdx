## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [CT-388](/peptides/ct-388) is Carmot Therapeutics' investigational signaling-biased dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist (ClinicalTrials.gov also lists enicepatide as another name on later records). The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, and the published CT-388 Phase 1 study are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [CT-388 vs semaglutide](/compare/ct-388-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, phases, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### CT-388 Phase 1 (Molecular Metabolism)

PMID 41319798, NCT04838405. Phase 1; double-blind; randomised; placebo-controlled. The paper describes CT-388 as a unimolecular peptide-based dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist that is cAMP signal-biased at both receptors. The clinical portion used single-administration groups (0.5–7.5 mg) or four once-weekly administrations (5–12 mg) in otherwise healthy participants with overweight or obesity. CT-388 was generally well tolerated; most treatment-emergent adverse events were mild or moderate. Glycemic parameters improved during fasting conditions and an oral glucose tolerance test.

The mean percent change in bodyweight from baseline to day 29 was −4.7% to −8.0% across CT-388 arms versus −0.5% with placebo. The abstract does **not** publish a per-arm percent table and does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [ct388-pmid-41319798]. This page does not collapse that range into one headline percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04838405 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 129; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). Lead sponsor: Carmot Therapeutics, Inc. Primary outcome on the registry is incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events; change in mean body weight is a secondary outcome. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT04838405` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 41319798 only.

Do not divide the CT-388 day-29 range by the amycretin 36-week estimated mean.
