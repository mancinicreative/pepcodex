## Overview

[5-Amino-1MQ](/peptides/5-amino-1mq) is the research name used on this site for 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, a small-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. [Retatrutide](/peptides/retatrutide) is Eli Lilly's investigational GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor agonist. The mouse NNMT-inhibitor papers fetched this run, the retatrutide phase 2 obesity trial, and TRANSCEND-T2D-1 are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [5-Amino-1MQ vs semaglutide](/compare/5-amino-1mq-vs-semaglutide), [retatrutide vs semaglutide](/compare/retatrutide-vs-semaglutide), and [tirzepatide vs retatrutide](/compare/tirzepatide-vs-retatrutide).

### Retatrutide phase 2 obesity (not TRIUMPH)

PMID 37366315, NCT04881760. Phase 2; 338 adults; 48 weeks. The **primary** end point was percentage change in body weight at **24 weeks**. Week 48 change is a **secondary** end point. The abstract reports least-squares means and does not name a treatment-regimen or treatment-policy estimand.

Week 24: −7.2% (1 mg), −12.9% (combined 4 mg), −17.3% (combined 8 mg), **−17.5% (12 mg)**, −1.6% placebo.

Week 48: −8.7% (1 mg), −17.1% (combined 4 mg), −22.8% (combined 8 mg), **−24.2% (12 mg)**, −2.1% placebo.

At week 48, a reduction of 5% / 10% / 15% or more occurred in 100% / 93% / 83% on 12 mg versus 27% / 9% / 2% on placebo. Gastrointestinal events were the most common adverse events, related to assigned amount, and mostly mild to moderate. Heart-rate increases peaked at 24 weeks and declined thereafter [retatrutide-p2-pmid-37366315].

This page does not restore a 4 mg 48-week −17.5% row. −17.5% in the abstract is the 12 mg week-24 figure. Combined 4 mg at 48 weeks is −17.1%.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT04881760 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 338; hasResults **true** as of 2026-09-02). Figures above are from the journal abstract, not the results module. A per-alias PubMed search for `"retatrutide"` on 2026-09-02 returned 176 records. This page quotes the phase 2 paper, TRANSCEND-T2D-1, and the TRIUMPH design paper below and does not dump that list.

### TRANSCEND-T2D-1 (type 2 diabetes phase 3, not obesity)

PMID 42250575, NCT06354660. Phase 3; 537 adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled by diet and exercise; 40 weeks. Primary end point: HbA1c change. **Treatment-regimen** estimand. HbA1c: −1.69% (4 mg), −1.86% (9 mg), −1.94% (12 mg), −0.81% placebo (P&lt;0.0001 vs placebo). Body weight (key secondary): −11.5%, −13.9%, −15.3% versus −2.6% placebo. Discontinuation for adverse events: 2–5% versus 0% placebo [transcend-t2d-1-pmid-42250575].

This is not a TRIUMPH obesity readout.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06354660 as completed Phase 3 (actual enrollment 537; hasResults **false** as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06354660` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 42250575 only.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** 5-Amino-1MQ has no human obesity RCT on this page; the retatrutide obesity figure is a phase 2 week-48 secondary least-squares mean. PubMed H2H queries returned 0 on 2026-09-02.
2. **5-Amino-1MQ:** street-name aliases returned 0; the chemical-name search returned 3 papers, none a human obesity RCT. The DIO mouse abstract names the compound and publishes **no** percent (PMID 35013352). CT.gov queries returned 0 studies as of 2026-09-02.
3. **Retatrutide phase 2 primary is 24 weeks:** −17.5% is 12 mg at 24 weeks, not 4 mg at 48 weeks. The −24.2% vs −2.1% figure is a week-48 secondary (PMID 37366315).
4. **TRANSCEND-T2D-1:** treatment-regimen HbA1c and key-secondary weight in type 2 diabetes, not TRIUMPH obesity (PMID 42250575).
5. **TRIUMPH:** design paper only. PubMed `retatrutide[Title] AND 28.7` and `retatrutide[Title] AND 28.3` on 2026-09-02 returned 0 records (PMID 41090431).
6. **openFDA:** no 5-Amino-1MQ application; no retatrutide application as of 2026-09-02.
