## What a Peptide-Drug Conjugate Is

A peptide-drug conjugate (PDC) has three parts:

**Targeting peptide.** A short sequence — typically 10 to 20 amino acids — that binds a receptor overexpressed on tumor cells. Somatostatin receptors and integrins are among the most-used targets.

**Linker.** The chemical connection between peptide and payload. It has to survive circulation and release the payload inside the tumor cell. Linker chemistry is where most of these programmes succeed or fail.

**Cytotoxic payload.** A chemotherapy agent potent enough that it would be unacceptably toxic given systemically.

## PDCs Versus Antibody-Drug Conjugates

The comparison to antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) is the usual framing, and the trade-offs are genuine:

| Property | Peptide-drug conjugate | Antibody-drug conjugate |
|---|---|---|
| Size | Small; better tissue penetration | Large; limited penetration into dense tumors |
| Half-life | Short | Long |
| Manufacturing | Chemical synthesis | Biologic production |
| Immunogenicity | Generally lower | Higher potential |
| Payload capacity | Low (few molecules per peptide) | Higher |

The short half-life cuts both ways: it reduces systemic exposure but also narrows the window during which drug reaches the tumor, which is why dosing schedule is a recurring problem in PDC development.

## The Modality Is Real

Bicyclic peptide-drug conjugates including BT5528 and zelenectide pevedotin are in clinical development, and radiolabeled somatostatin-analog peptides are already an established part of neuroendocrine tumor care. The category is not speculative.

What is unreliable is the layer of unattributed efficacy claims around it.

## How to Check a PDC Efficacy Claim

1. **Get the compound's actual name and sponsor.** A claim about "a novel PDC" with no compound name cannot be checked and should not be believed.
2. **Find the NCT number.** Search ClinicalTrials.gov by intervention. If no registration exists, no trial exists.
3. **Check whether results are posted or published.** "Presented at an oncology conference" is not a citation; conference abstracts are indexed and retrievable.
4. **Check trial design.** A single-arm response rate is not comparable to "historical outcomes with chemotherapy," and framing it that way overstates the finding.
