## Overview

[Amycretin](/peptides/amycretin) is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. [VK2735](/peptides/vk2735) is Viking Therapeutics' investigational dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. The oral first-in-human amycretin trial, the subcutaneous amycretin phase 1b/2a trial, and VENTURE are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

Related pages: [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide) and [VK2735 vs semaglutide](/compare/vk2735-vs-semaglutide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT05369390` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550229 only.

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. Most treatment-emergent adverse events were gastrointestinal and mild to moderate.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). PubMed `NCT06064006` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 40550231 only.

### VENTURE (subcutaneous VK2735, 13 weeks)

**VENTURE** (PMID 41508550, NCT06068946): Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, weekly subcutaneous VK2735 in adults with obesity or overweight and at least one weight-related comorbidity. Participants with diabetes were ineligible. Primary endpoint: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 13. Mean weight reduction ranged from 9.2 kg (2.5 mg; 9.1%) to 14.6 kg (15 mg; **14.7%**); placebo 1.8 kg (**1.7%**). Across active-treatment groups, ≥5% loss occurred in 93% (130/140) vs 12% (4/34) placebo. Common adverse events were gastrointestinal. The abstract does **not** name a treatment-regimen versus efficacy estimand [venture-pmid-41508550].

CT.gov: COMPLETED; enrollment 176 actual; primary completion 2024-02-27 actual; hasResults **false**. Figures above are from the journal abstract, not a results module. PubMed `NCT06068946` on 2026-09-02 returned PMID 41508550 only.

Do not divide VENTURE’s 13-week percent by the amycretin 36-week estimated mean.

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means; VENTURE is a 13-week Phase 2 result with no estimand named in the abstract.
