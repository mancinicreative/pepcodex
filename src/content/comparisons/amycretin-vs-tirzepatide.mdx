## Overview

Amycretin and tirzepatide are dual-pathway incretin medicines, not the same molecule. Amycretin is a unimolecular GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist ([amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229] [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]). Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist studied for obesity in SURMOUNT-1 ([surmount-1-pmid-35658024]). This page is a cross-trial comparison. It is not a head-to-head randomised trial.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [tirzepatide](/peptides/tirzepatide), and [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide).

## Efficacy

These are separate programmes. Do not divide one percent by the other.

### Oral amycretin (phase 1)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390: first-in-human, n=144 across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D was a 12-week intervention; change in bodyweight to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The abstract fetched 2026-09-02 does not publish a percent bodyweight change [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

### Subcutaneous amycretin (phase 1b/2a)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006: n=125 randomised (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative bodyweight change was a **secondary** endpoint. The paper reports estimated mean change versus placebo (not labelled as a Phase 3 treatment-regimen estimand) [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]:

| Part | Maintenance arm | Week | Estimated mean vs placebo |
|------|-----------------|------|---------------------------|
| B | 60 mg | 36 | −24.3% vs −1.1% |
| C | 20 mg | 36 | −22.0% vs 1.9% |
| D | 5 mg | 28 | −16.2% vs 2.3% |
| E | 1.25 mg | 20 | −9.7% vs 2.0% |

Parts A–D P&lt;0.0001; Part E P=0.0003. A large number of participants withdrew; many discontinuations were unrelated to adverse events [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

### Tirzepatide (SURMOUNT-1)

PMID 35658024, NCT04184622: phase 3, n=2539 adults with obesity (no diabetes), 72 weeks including 20-week dose escalation. The **treatment-regimen** estimand assessed effects regardless of treatment discontinuation in the intention-to-treat population [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

| Arm | Mean percent weight change at 72 weeks |
|-----|----------------------------------------|
| 5 mg | −15.0% (95% CI −15.9 to −14.2) |
| 10 mg | −19.5% (95% CI −20.4 to −18.5) |
| 15 mg | −20.9% (95% CI −21.8 to −19.9) |
| Placebo | −3.1% (95% CI −4.3 to −1.9) |

P&lt;0.001 for all tirzepatide doses versus placebo. ≥5% at 15 mg: 91% (95% CI 88–94) vs 35% (30–39) placebo. ≥20% at 15 mg: 57% (95% CI 53–61) vs 3% (1–5) placebo. ≥20% at 10 mg: 50% (46–54) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Side effects

Oral amycretin: 364 treatment-emergent events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; all mild or moderate; most common were gastrointestinal (180 of 364 events) [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous amycretin: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate and resolved by end of study. High withdrawal rate, including discontinuations unrelated to adverse events. The abstract does not publish a matched nausea-percent table [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

SURMOUNT-1: gastrointestinal events most common, mostly mild to moderate, mainly during dose escalation. Adverse events caused discontinuation in 4.3% (5 mg), 7.1% (10 mg), 6.2% (15 mg), and 2.6% (placebo). The abstract does not publish nausea or vomiting percents [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].

## Summary

- **Amycretin** is an investigational unimolecular GLP-1/amylin agonist. Oral phase 1 did not publish a bodyweight percent in the abstract fetched 2026-09-02. Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a reported estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1% at week 36 on the 60 mg part (secondary endpoint) [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229] [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].
- **Tirzepatide** SURMOUNT-1 15 mg: −20.9% vs −3.1% at 72 weeks (treatment-regimen). ≥20% at 15 mg: 57% (95% CI 53–61) [surmount-1-pmid-35658024].
- Different second targets (amylin vs GIP). Cross-trial only.
- Amycretin is not FDA-approved. Tirzepatide is approved as Mounjaro and Zepbound.
