## Different evidence questions

Tesamorelin is a growth hormone-releasing hormone analog. Its labeled indication is reduction of **excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV and lipodystrophy**. The pivotal randomized trial measured VAT, while FDA labeling says the drug is not for weight-loss management and that long-term cardiovascular safety has not been established. [FDA prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=839334d3-8c1d-4c26-9036-2ab524a6ea75); [Falutz et al., 2007, PMID 18057338](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18057338/).

GHRP-6 is a growth hormone-releasing peptide studied as a secretagogue. In a small study of healthy men, repeated exposure increased growth hormone, ACTH and cortisol during the observation night. Another study in nine healthy men investigated how endogenous GHRH affects the acute GH response to GHRP-6. Those hormone findings do not test VAT reduction or patient benefit. [Frieboes et al., 1995, PMID 7617137](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7617137/); [GHRP-6 physiology study, PMID 9543138](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9543138/).

| Question | Tesamorelin | GHRP-6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence grade for the cited outcome | High for VAT reduction in indicated adults | Low for clinical benefit; acute hormone responses are documented |
| Studied population here | Adults with HIV and excess abdominal fat | Small healthy-volunteer physiology studies |
| FDA status | Approved for the specific labeled indication | No FDA-approved GHRP-6 product established in the cited FDA record |
| Long-term safety | Cardiovascular safety not established by labeling | Not established by the cited acute studies |

An FDA enforcement record identifies GHRP-6 among compounded substances that did not meet the cited conditions for compounding exemptions. That record does not confer product approval or establish safety for any marketed preparation. [FDA warning letter, 2020](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/tailor-made-compounding-llc-594743-04012020).

No cited study compares clinical outcomes between these compounds or establishes the safety or benefit of combining them. Claims about appetite, cortisol, side-effect frequency or insurance cannot be ranked across these different studies.
