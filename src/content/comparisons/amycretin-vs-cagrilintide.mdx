## Overview

Amycretin is Novo Nordisk's investigational unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist. Cagrilintide is Novo Nordisk's investigational long-acting amylin analogue. The oral first-in-human trial, the subcutaneous phase 1b/2a trial, the Phase 2 cagrilintide monotherapy trial, and REDEFINE 1 (CagriSema) are separate programmes. This page does not collapse them into one efficacy ratio.

See also [amycretin](/peptides/amycretin), [cagrilintide](/peptides/cagrilintide), [amycretin vs semaglutide](/compare/amycretin-vs-semaglutide), and [cagrilintide vs tirzepatide](/compare/cagrilintide-vs-tirzepatide).

## Clinical Trial Data

These are not head-to-head RCTs. Durations, endpoints, and estimands differ.

### Amycretin oral first-in-human (Lancet)

PMID 40550229, NCT05369390. Phase 1; 144 participants across parts A–D. Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Part C/D used a 12-week intervention. Change in body weight (%) to day 85 was an **exploratory** pharmacodynamic endpoint. The published abstract does not report a body-weight percent. Across parts A–D there were 364 treatment-emergent adverse events in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229]. This page does not quote a conference-extrapolated oral weight-change percent.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05369390 as completed Phase 1 (actual enrollment 144; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

### Amycretin subcutaneous phase 1b/2a (Lancet)

PMID 40550231, NCT06064006. 125 participants randomly allocated (101 amycretin, 24 placebo). Primary endpoint was treatment-emergent adverse events. Relative change in body weight from baseline was a **secondary** endpoint. A large number of participants withdrew.

Estimated mean body-weight change versus placebo:

- Part B, 60 mg, week 36: −24.3% vs −1.1% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part C, 20 mg, week 36: −22.0% vs 1.9% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part D, 5 mg, week 28: −16.2% vs 2.3% (P&lt;0.0001)
- Part E, 1.25 mg, week 20: −9.7% vs 2.0% (P=0.0003)

Gastrointestinal events were the most common treatment-emergent adverse events; the majority were mild to moderate [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231]. That is not a confirmatory obesity Phase 3 treatment-regimen result.

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06064006 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 125; TEAE primary; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02).

A per-alias PubMed search for amycretin on 2026-09-02 returned 21 records. This page quotes the two Lancet papers above and does not add later type 2 diabetes papers that were not fetched this increment.

### Cagrilintide monotherapy (Phase 2)

PMID 34798060, NCT03856047. n=706 randomly assigned (100–102 per cagrilintide arm; 99 liraglutide 3.0 mg; 101 placebo); 26-week treatment period. The paper reports both a trial-product estimand (assuming adherence) and a treatment-policy estimand (regardless of adherence).

Trial-product mean percent weight change at week 26: cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg 6.0%–10.8% versus placebo 3.0% (estimated treatment difference range 3.0–7.8 percentage points; P&lt;0.001). Cagrilintide 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0% (estimated treatment difference 1.8 percentage points; P=0.03). The abstract states that similar reductions were observed with the treatment-policy estimand; it does not publish a separate treatment-policy percent table. This page does not invent a 2.4 mg row [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

Gastrointestinal adverse events: 41%–63% on cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg versus 32% placebo, primarily nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT03856047 as completed Phase 2 (actual enrollment 706; hasResults true as of 2026-09-02). This page quotes the Lancet abstract, not the registry results module.

### CagriSema is not cagrilintide monotherapy

REDEFINE 1 (PMID 40544433, NCT05567796) randomised 3,417 adults without diabetes to cagrilintide-semaglutide, semaglutide 2.4 mg, cagrilintide 2.4 mg, or placebo for 68 weeks (21:3:3:7; 2108 / 302 / 302 / 705). Treatment-policy mean change: −20.4% with cagrilintide-semaglutide versus −3.0% placebo (estimated difference −17.3 percentage points; 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6; P&lt;0.001). The abstract does not publish the cagrilintide-alone percent. Gastrointestinal adverse events: 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05567796 as ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING Phase 3 (enrollment 3400 estimated; hasResults false as of 2026-09-02). This page does not quote an unpublished CagriSema versus amycretin percent.

## Side Effect Profile

Oral first-in-human: 364 TEAEs in 89 (62%) of 144 participants; 180 (49%) of 364 events were gastrointestinal [amycretin-oral-pmid-40550229].

Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a: gastrointestinal events most common; majority mild to moderate; high withdrawals, many unrelated to TEAEs. The abstract does not publish a discontinuation percent [amycretin-sc-pmid-40550231].

Phase 2 cagrilintide: gastrointestinal events 41%–63% versus 32% placebo; nausea 20%–47% versus 18% [cagrilintide-p2-pmid-34798060].

REDEFINE 1 CagriSema: gastrointestinal events 79.6% versus 39.9% placebo [redefine-1-pmid-40544433].

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cross-trial, not head-to-head:** amycretin weight figures are early-phase estimated means; cagrilintide monotherapy is a 26-week Phase 2 range.
2. **Oral first-in-human:** TEAE primary; day-85 body weight was exploratory; the abstract publishes no weight-change percent.
3. **Subcutaneous phase 1b/2a:** TEAE primary; 60 mg week 36 estimated mean −24.3% vs −1.1%.
4. **Monotherapy is a range, not a 2.4 mg row:** trial-product 6.0%–10.8% versus 3.0% placebo; 4.5 mg 10.8% versus liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0%. The abstract does not publish a treatment-policy percent table.
5. **CagriSema is the combination:** REDEFINE 1 treatment-policy −20.4% versus −3.0%. That figure is not a cagrilintide-alone result.
